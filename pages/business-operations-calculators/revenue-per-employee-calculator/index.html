Revenue Per Employee Formula

Revenue Per Employee
Revenue Per Employee = Total Annual Revenue / Total Number of Employees

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter total annual revenue - your company's gross revenue for the year.
  2. Enter total employees - full-time equivalent headcount.
  3. Select your industry - for benchmark comparison.
  4. Click "Calculate" to see your revenue per employee and industry comparison.

Example Scenario

A SaaS company with $10,000,000 in annual revenue and 50 employees has a revenue per employee of $200,000. The technology industry benchmark is $200K-$800K, placing this company at the lower end, suggesting room for growth or efficiency improvements.

Frequently Asked Questions

Revenue per employee is a productivity metric calculated by dividing total company revenue by the number of employees. It measures how efficiently a company uses its workforce.

Technology/SaaS: $200K-$800K, Professional Services: $100K-$300K, Retail: $100K-$200K, Manufacturing: $150K-$400K, Healthcare: $80K-$200K per employee.

It helps evaluate operational efficiency. Higher ratios generally indicate better productivity, though context and industry norms must be considered.

Invest in automation, optimize processes, upskill employees, focus on higher-margin products, and be strategic about headcount growth.
