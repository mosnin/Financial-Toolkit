How State Income Tax Works

Flat Rate States
Tax = Income × Flat Rate (e.g., IL 4.95%: $80,000 × 4.95% = $3,960)
Progressive States
Tax computed using graduated state brackets. Lower income at lower rates, higher income at higher rates.
No Income Tax States
AK, FL, NV, NH, SD, TN, TX, WA, WY — $0 state income tax on wages.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your annual income.
  2. Select your state from the dropdown.
  3. Select your filing status.
  4. Click Calculate State Tax.

Example: California vs Texas

Single filer earning $100,000:

  • California: Progressive rates → estimated state tax ~$6,500–$7,200
  • Texas: No state income tax → $0
  • Annual difference: ~$6,500+
