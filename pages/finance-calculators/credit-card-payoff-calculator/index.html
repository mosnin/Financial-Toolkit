Credit Card Payoff Formula

Credit card payoff calculations use the same amortization math as other loans. Given a fixed monthly payment:

Months to Payoff
n = -ln(1 - (B x r / P)) / ln(1 + r)

Where n is the number of months, B is the balance, r is the monthly interest rate (APR / 12), and P is the monthly payment. To find the required payment for a desired number of months, use the standard amortization payment formula.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your credit card balance - the current amount you owe.
  2. Enter your APR - the annual percentage rate on your card.
  3. Choose your approach - enter a monthly payment to see the payoff timeline, or enter a desired number of months to see the required payment.
  4. Click "Calculate" to see your payoff plan, total interest, and total cost.

Example Scenario

If you have a $5,000 credit card balance at 20% APR and pay $200 per month, it would take approximately 32 months to pay it off. You would pay about $1,314 in interest, for a total of $6,314. Increasing your payment to $300 per month would cut the payoff time to 20 months and save over $400 in interest.
