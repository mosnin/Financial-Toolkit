Ad Revenue Formulas

Effective Impressions
Impressions = Page Views x Ads per Page x (Fill Rate / 100)
Monthly Ad Revenue
Monthly Revenue = (Impressions / 1,000) x RPM
Revenue per Page View
RPPV = Monthly Revenue / Monthly Page Views
Optimization Potential (100% fill)
Potential = (Page Views x Ads per Page / 1,000) x RPM

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your monthly page views from Google Analytics or your platform dashboard.
  2. Set your RPM — find this in your ad network dashboard. If new, use $5-$10 for AdSense, $15-$25 for Mediavine as starting estimates.
  3. Enter the number of ad units per page — count how many ad slots you display on a typical content page.
  4. Set your fill rate — find this in your ad network reports. 90% is a reasonable default if unsure.
  5. Click Calculate Ad Revenue to see monthly earnings, annual projection, revenue per page view, and the revenue gap from your fill rate.

Example Scenario

A lifestyle blog generates 200,000 page views per month with an RPM of $18, 3 ads per page, and 88% fill rate from Mediavine.

  • Effective Impressions: 200,000 x 3 x 88% = 528,000
  • Monthly Revenue: (528,000 / 1,000) x $18 = $9,504
  • Annual Revenue: $9,504 x 12 = $114,048
  • Revenue per Page View: $9,504 / 200,000 = $0.0475
  • 100% Fill Rate Potential: (600,000 / 1,000) x $18 = $10,800
  • Optimization Upside: $10,800 - $9,504 = $1,296/month

Frequently Asked Questions

RPM (Revenue Per Mille) is the estimated revenue per 1,000 page views. It is calculated as (Total Revenue / Total Page Views) x 1,000. RPM varies by niche, geography, and season — typically $1-$5 for general content to $20-$50+ for finance and legal niches.

Fill rates of 90-100% are typical for premium ad networks like Mediavine or AdThrive. Google AdSense fill rates are often 80-95%. Header bidding setups with multiple demand partners typically achieve 95%+ fill rates.

Most successful publishers use 2-4 ads per page. Going above 5-6 ads often hurts user experience, increases bounce rates, and can reduce RPM as ad viewability scores drop. Quality placement in high-viewability positions beats quantity.

Mediavine and AdThrive (Raptive) generally offer the highest RPMs, often $10-$40+. They require minimum traffic thresholds (50,000 and 100,000 sessions/month). Google AdSense is accessible to all but typically delivers $2-$10 RPM.
