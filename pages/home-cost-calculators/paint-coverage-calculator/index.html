Paint Coverage Formula

The paint needed is calculated by determining the total paintable wall area and dividing by coverage per gallon:

Paint Formula
Wall Area = 2 x (Length + Width) x Height - (Doors x 21 sqft) - (Windows x 15 sqft) Gallons = (Wall Area x Coats) / 350 sqft per gallon

Standard doors are approximately 21 square feet (3ft x 7ft) and standard windows are approximately 15 square feet (3ft x 5ft). One gallon covers about 350 square feet per coat.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter room length and width - in feet.
  2. Enter ceiling height - standard is 8 feet.
  3. Enter number of doors and windows - these will be subtracted from the wall area.
  4. Select number of coats - 2 coats is standard for most projects.
  5. Click "Calculate Paint" to see gallons needed, wall area, and estimated cost.

Example Scenario

A 15 x 12 foot room with 8-foot ceilings, 2 doors, and 3 windows needs about 2.3 gallons for 2 coats. The paintable wall area is approximately 387 square feet after subtracting doors and windows. At $40 per gallon, the paint cost would be about $120.
