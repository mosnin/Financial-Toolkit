How to Use This Calculator

  1. Select your filing status.
  2. Enter your annual income for marginal rate estimation.
  3. Enter your property taxes paid annually.
  4. Enter mortgage interest from your Form 1098.
  5. Enter state income taxes or sales tax paid.
  6. Enter charitable contributions and any other deductions.
  7. Click Compare Deductions for your recommendation.

Example Scenario

A married couple filing jointly with AGI of $150,000: $8,000 property taxes, $14,000 mortgage interest, $6,000 state taxes, $3,000 charity.

  • SALT = min($8,000 + $6,000, $10,000) = $10,000
  • Itemized Total: $10,000 + $14,000 + $3,000 = $27,000
  • Standard Deduction: $29,200
  • Recommendation: Take the standard deduction (saves $220 more at 22% marginal rate)

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes, state and local property taxes are deductible as part of the SALT deduction, subject to the $10,000 SALT cap. You must itemize deductions to claim this benefit.

The Tax Cuts and Jobs Act caps the combined deduction for state and local income taxes, property taxes, and sales taxes at $10,000 per year ($5,000 for married filing separately).

For 2026, the estimated standard deduction is $14,600 for single filers, $29,200 for married filing jointly, and $21,900 for head of household.

You can deduct mortgage interest on up to $750,000 of mortgage debt ($375,000 if married filing separately) for loans originated after December 15, 2017. For older loans, the limit is $1 million.
