How Savings Goals Are Calculated

Formula
Amount Needed = Goal Amount − Current Savings
Months Remaining = Months between today and target date
Monthly Savings Required = Amount Needed ÷ Months Remaining
Daily Savings Rate = Amount Needed ÷ Days Remaining
Progress % = (Current Savings ÷ Goal Amount) × 100

This calculator uses simple linear savings (no interest). For long-term goals where you plan to hold savings in a high-yield account, the actual required monthly contribution may be slightly lower due to interest earned. For aggressive long-term investment goals, see the Financial Independence Calculator.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Name your goal: Giving a goal a name makes it feel real and concrete.
  2. Enter goal amount: The total dollar amount you need to save.
  3. Add current savings: What you have already set aside specifically for this goal.
  4. Set target date: Your deadline for reaching the goal.
  5. Review the plan: Check if the required monthly savings fits your budget. Adjust the date or goal if needed.

Example Scenario

Jamie wants to save $8,000 for a home down payment by December 2026 — 20 months away. Jamie already has $1,500 saved. Amount needed: $6,500. Monthly required: $6,500 ÷ 20 = $325/month. Daily rate: about $10.74/day. Progress: 18.75%. By automating a $325 transfer on the 1st of each month into a dedicated high-yield savings account, Jamie has a clear, achievable roadmap.
