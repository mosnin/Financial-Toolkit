How the FIRE Timeline Is Calculated

Formula
FIRE Number = Annual Expenses × 25 (4% SWR)
FV(month n) = PV × (1+r)^n + PMT × [(1+r)^n − 1] / r
Months to FIRE = first n where FV ≥ FIRE Number

+$500/mo Impact = Months to FIRE (base) − Months to FIRE (PMT+500)

The calculator iterates month by month, compounding your current savings and adding your monthly contribution at the monthly rate (annual rate / 12), until the growing portfolio meets your FIRE number. The same calculation runs again with an extra $500/month to show the time savings from a modest contribution increase.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your current age and savings: Include all investment accounts.
  2. Add your monthly savings amount: This is your total monthly investment contribution.
  3. Enter annual retirement expenses: What you'll spend each year in retirement in today's dollars.
  4. Set your expected return: 7% is a standard nominal return assumption for diversified portfolios.
  5. See your timeline: Years, months, and FIRE age — plus the impact of $500 more per month.

Example Scenario

Casey is 29 with $40,000 saved, contributes $2,200/month, and plans to spend $55,000/year in retirement. FIRE number: $1,375,000 (25x). At 7% annual return, Casey reaches FIRE in approximately 19 years and 4 months, at age 48. By increasing monthly savings to $2,700 (+$500), the FIRE date moves up by about 1 year and 8 months — FIRE at age 47 instead of 48.
