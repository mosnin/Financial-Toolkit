Burn Rate Formulas

Gross Burn Rate
Gross Burn Rate = Total Monthly Expenses
Net Burn Rate
Net Burn Rate = Monthly Expenses - Monthly Revenue
Runway (Months)
Runway = Cash in Bank / Net Burn Rate

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your monthly expenses -- total recurring costs including salaries, rent, software, marketing, and all other expenses.
  2. Enter your monthly revenue -- your recurring monthly income. Enter 0 if you are pre-revenue.
  3. Enter your cash in bank -- total available cash reserves, including any recent funding.
  4. Click "Calculate Burn Rate" to see your gross burn, net burn, runway in months, and estimated end date.

Example Scenario

A SaaS startup has $500,000 in the bank, spends $50,000/month, and earns $15,000/month in revenue.

  • Gross Burn Rate: $50,000/month
  • Net Burn Rate: $50,000 - $15,000 = $35,000/month
  • Runway: $500,000 / $35,000 = 14.3 months

The startup has roughly 14 months of runway. If they want 18 months of runway, they need to either reduce net burn to ~$27,800/month or raise more capital.

Frequently Asked Questions

Burn rate is the rate at which a startup spends its cash reserves. Gross burn rate is total monthly expenses, while net burn rate subtracts monthly revenue from expenses to show actual cash consumed per month.

Gross burn rate is your total monthly spending regardless of income. Net burn rate accounts for revenue: Net Burn = Monthly Expenses - Monthly Revenue. If you spend $50,000/month and earn $20,000/month, your net burn is $30,000/month.

Runway is the number of months a startup can continue operating before running out of cash. It is calculated as Cash in Bank / Net Burn Rate.

Most advisors recommend 12-18 months of runway. This gives enough time to hit milestones, adjust strategy, and raise the next round of funding without desperation.

Common strategies include renegotiating contracts, reducing headcount or hiring slowly, cutting non-essential software subscriptions, moving to cheaper office space, and focusing marketing spend on the highest-ROI channels.
