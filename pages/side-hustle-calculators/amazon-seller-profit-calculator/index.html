Amazon Profit Formulas

Referral Fee
Referral Fee = Selling Price x Referral Fee %
Total Amazon Fees
Total Amazon Fees = Referral Fee + FBA Fee + Storage Fee
Total Costs Per Unit
Total Costs = Product Cost + Total Amazon Fees + Ad Spend + Shipping to Amazon
Net Profit Per Unit
Net Profit = Selling Price - Total Costs
ROI
ROI = (Net Profit / Product Cost) x 100

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your selling price — what buyers pay on Amazon.
  2. Enter product cost — your landed cost from the manufacturer or supplier.
  3. Enter referral fee % — find your category's rate in Amazon Seller Central (typically 8–15%).
  4. Enter FBA fee — use Amazon's FBA Revenue Calculator for your specific product dimensions and weight.
  5. Enter storage and ad spend — amortize monthly totals down to a per-unit cost.
  6. Enter shipping to Amazon — your per-unit freight cost to the fulfillment center.
  7. Click Calculate to see your full cost breakdown, net profit, and ROI.

Example Scenario

Marcus sells a kitchen gadget on Amazon for $29.99. His product cost is $7.50, referral fee is 15%, FBA fee is $3.22, storage is $0.50/unit, ad spend is $2.00/unit, and shipping to Amazon is $0.75.

  • Referral fee: $29.99 x 15% = $4.50
  • Total Amazon fees: $4.50 + $3.22 + $0.50 = $8.22
  • Total costs: $7.50 + $8.22 + $2.00 + $0.75 = $18.47
  • Net profit: $29.99 - $18.47 = $11.52
  • ROI: ($11.52 / $7.50) x 100 = 153.6%

An ROI of over 150% is excellent for Amazon FBA, indicating strong product viability.

Frequently Asked Questions

Amazon's referral fee is a percentage of the total selling price paid to Amazon for each sale. It typically ranges from 6% to 45% depending on the product category. Most categories fall between 8% and 15%.

FBA (Fulfillment by Amazon) fees cover picking, packing, and shipping your product to the buyer. They are based on the product's size and weight tier. Small standard items may cost around $3-4, while large or heavy products can exceed $10 per unit.

Most experienced Amazon sellers target a minimum ROI of 30–50% on their cost of goods. A 50% ROI means for every $1 invested in inventory, you net $0.50 in profit. Higher ROI products are preferable but harder to find.
