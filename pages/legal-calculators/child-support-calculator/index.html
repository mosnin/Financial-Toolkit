Child Support Formula

This calculator uses a simplified income shares model:

Child Support Formula
Support = (Combined Income x Child Factor x Non-Custodial Share) + Extra Expenses Share

The child factor represents the percentage of combined income typically allocated to children (approximately 17% for one child, increasing with more children). The non-custodial parent's share is based on their proportion of combined income, adjusted by custody time. Additional expenses (childcare and insurance) are split proportionally by income.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter Parent 1's monthly gross income - this is the custodial parent's income before taxes.
  2. Enter Parent 2's monthly gross income - the non-custodial parent's income before taxes.
  3. Enter custody percentage - the percentage of time Parent 1 has custody (e.g., 70% means Parent 2 has 30%).
  4. Enter number of children - how many children need support.
  5. Enter childcare and insurance costs - monthly out-of-pocket expenses for the children.
  6. Click "Calculate Child Support" to see the estimated monthly payment.

Example Scenario

Parent 1 earns $5,000/month and has 70% custody of 2 children. Parent 2 earns $3,500/month (30% custody). Childcare costs $500/month and health insurance $200/month. Parent 2's income share is about 41%. With a child factor of 25% for two children, the base obligation allocated to Parent 2 is approximately $870. Adding their share of additional expenses ($287), the estimated monthly child support is approximately $1,157.
