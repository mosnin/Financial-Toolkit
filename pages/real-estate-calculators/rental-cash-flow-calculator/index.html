Cash Flow Formula

Monthly Cash Flow
Effective Rent = Monthly Rent x (1 - Vacancy Rate / 100) Management Cost = Effective Rent x (Management Fee / 100) Monthly Cash Flow = Effective Rent - Mortgage - Taxes - Insurance - Maintenance - Management Cost Cash-on-Cash Return = (Annual Cash Flow / Total Cash Invested) x 100

Effective rent accounts for vacancy by reducing gross rent proportionally. The management fee is calculated on collected rent, not gross potential rent.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter monthly rent -- the gross monthly rent you charge or expect to charge.
  2. Enter your monthly mortgage payment -- principal and interest only (PITI can be split out).
  3. Enter monthly property tax and insurance -- if escrowed, separate them from mortgage.
  4. Enter monthly maintenance estimate -- a common guideline is 1% of property value per year, divided by 12.
  5. Set the vacancy rate -- 5% is typical for strong rental markets.
  6. Set the management fee percentage -- 8-12% if using a property manager, 0% if self-managing.
  7. Enter total cash invested (optional) -- for cash-on-cash return calculation.

Example Scenario

Single-Family Rental Property

Monthly rent: $2,000. Mortgage: $1,200. Property tax: $300/month. Insurance: $100/month. Maintenance: $150/month. Vacancy rate: 5%. Management fee: 10%. Total cash invested: $80,000.

Effective rent = $2,000 x 0.95 = $1,900. Management cost = $1,900 x 0.10 = $190. Total expenses = $1,200 + $300 + $100 + $150 + $190 = $1,940.

Monthly cash flow = $1,900 - $1,940 = -$40 (slight negative cash flow). Annual = -$480. Cash-on-cash return = -0.60%.

This property barely breaks even. Raising rent to $2,200 would yield $105/month positive cash flow and a 1.58% cash-on-cash return.
