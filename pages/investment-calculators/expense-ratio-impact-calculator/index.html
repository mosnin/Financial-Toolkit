How Expense Ratio Impact Is Calculated

The portfolio values are calculated using compound growth, with and without the expense ratio subtracted from the annual return:

Portfolio Without Fees
Value = Investment × (1 + Annual Return %)^Years
Portfolio With Fees
Value = Investment × (1 + Annual Return % − Expense Ratio %)^Years

The difference between these two values is the total cost of the expense ratio over the investment period.

How to Use This Expense Ratio Impact Calculator

  1. Enter your investment amount — your initial lump-sum investment.
  2. Enter the annual return % — the expected annual return before fees.
  3. Enter the expense ratio % — the fund's annual fee (check the fund's prospectus or fact sheet).
  4. Enter the number of years — how long you plan to hold the investment.
  5. Click "Calculate Impact" to see the side-by-side comparison and the total fee cost.

Example Scenario

Suppose you invest $50,000 earning 8% annually. With no expense ratio, after 25 years your portfolio would be worth approximately $342,424. With a 1.00% expense ratio (net return 7%), the portfolio would be worth about $271,372 — a difference of over $71,000. That $71,000 is the compounded cost of the annual fee, representing more than the entire original investment.
