Frequently Asked Questions

Compare your salary to industry benchmarks for your role, experience level, location, and company size. Resources like the Bureau of Labor Statistics, Glassdoor, and LinkedIn Salary can provide data. Our salary competitiveness quiz walks you through the key factors to get a personalised assessment.

Before quitting, most experts recommend having at least six months of living expenses saved, a clear plan for income replacement (new job offer, freelance clients, or a business plan), and ideally health insurance arranged. Take the quiz to evaluate your specific situation.

Freelancing can yield higher gross income but comes with additional costs — self-employment taxes (15.3%), no employer benefits, inconsistent cash flow, and business expenses. The net financial advantage depends on your ability to find clients, your rate, and your discipline with savings. Our quiz helps you weigh the trade-offs.

Signs of career financial instability include working in a declining industry, having non-transferable skills, no emergency fund to cover a job loss, reliance on a single income source, and a company with poor financial health. Our career stability quiz evaluates all these risk factors together.
