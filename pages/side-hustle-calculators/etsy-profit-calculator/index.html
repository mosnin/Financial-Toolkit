Etsy Fee Formulas

Etsy charges multiple fees per transaction. Here is how each is calculated:

Listing Fee
$0.20 per item listed (charged at listing, renews every 4 months or when sold)
Transaction Fee
6.5% x Selling Price (applied to item price + any shipping collected)
Payment Processing Fee
(3% x Selling Price) + $0.25 per transaction
Net Profit Per Item
Selling Price - Material Cost - Shipping Cost - Listing Fee - Transaction Fee - Processing Fee - (Hours x Hourly Rate)
Effective Hourly Rate
(Net Profit + Labor Cost) / Time per Item

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your selling price — the total price the buyer sees and pays on Etsy.
  2. Enter material cost — the direct cost of all supplies, components, and packaging for one item.
  3. Enter your shipping cost — your actual out-of-pocket expense to ship one package.
  4. Enter time per item — include production, photography, listing, packing, and drop-off time.
  5. Enter your desired hourly rate — what you want your labor to be worth.
  6. Click Calculate to see the full fee breakdown, net profit, and whether your pricing supports your goals.

Example Scenario

Emma sells hand-poured soy candles on Etsy. Her candle selling price is $28.00, materials cost $7.50, shipping is $5.00, and she spends 1.5 hours per item at a target rate of $18/hour.

  • Listing fee: $0.20
  • Transaction fee: $28.00 x 6.5% = $1.82
  • Processing fee: ($28.00 x 3%) + $0.25 = $1.09
  • Total Etsy fees: $0.20 + $1.82 + $1.09 = $3.11
  • Labor cost: 1.5 x $18 = $27.00
  • Net profit: $28.00 - $7.50 - $5.00 - $3.11 - $27.00 = -$14.61

This example reveals Emma needs to either raise her price or reduce material costs to meet her $18/hour labor goal — a critical insight before scaling.
