CAC Formulas

Total CAC
CAC = (Marketing Spend + Sales Spend) / New Customers Acquired
Marketing CAC
Marketing CAC = Marketing Spend / New Customers Acquired
Sales CAC
Sales CAC = Sales Spend / New Customers Acquired

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your total marketing spend -- all marketing-related costs including advertising, content creation, marketing tools, and marketing team salaries for the period.
  2. Enter your total sales spend -- all sales-related costs including salaries, commissions, CRM tools, and travel for the period.
  3. Enter the number of new customers acquired during the same time period.
  4. Click "Calculate CAC" to see your overall CAC, plus the breakdown between marketing and sales costs per customer.

Example Scenario

A B2B SaaS company spent $25,000 on marketing and $15,000 on sales in Q1. They acquired 100 new customers during that quarter.

  • Total CAC: ($25,000 + $15,000) / 100 = $400 per customer
  • Marketing CAC: $25,000 / 100 = $250 per customer
  • Sales CAC: $15,000 / 100 = $150 per customer

If the average customer LTV is $1,200, the LTV:CAC ratio is 3:1, which is considered healthy for a SaaS business.

Frequently Asked Questions

CAC is the total cost of acquiring a new customer, including all marketing and sales expenses divided by the number of new customers acquired in a given period.

CAC = (Total Marketing Spend + Total Sales Spend) / Number of New Customers Acquired. Include all costs: advertising, salaries, tools, commissions, and overhead allocated to acquisition.

A good CAC depends on your customer lifetime value (LTV). The general benchmark is an LTV:CAC ratio of 3:1 or higher, meaning each customer generates at least 3x what it cost to acquire them.

Marketing CAC isolates the marketing spend per customer (Marketing Spend / New Customers). Sales CAC isolates the sales spend per customer (Sales Spend / New Customers). Together they equal total CAC.

Improve conversion rates, focus on high-performing channels, implement referral programs, optimize your sales process, invest in content marketing and SEO for organic acquisition, and improve customer targeting.
