Frequently Asked Questions

Attorney hourly rates vary widely by location, experience, and specialization. General practice attorneys charge $150-$350/hour, while specialized attorneys (corporate, patent, criminal defense) charge $300-$700+/hour. Major city rates are typically higher.

Child support calculations vary by state but generally consider both parents' incomes, custody arrangement, number of children, healthcare costs, and childcare expenses. Most states use either an income shares model or percentage of income model.

An uncontested divorce costs $500-$3,000, while a contested divorce averages $15,000-$30,000 per spouse and can exceed $100,000 in complex cases. Mediation ($3,000-$8,000 total) is typically much cheaper than litigation.

Settlements typically include medical expenses (past and future), lost wages, loss of earning capacity, pain and suffering (often 1.5-5x medical bills), and property damage. A multiplier method is commonly used to calculate non-economic damages.

A basic will costs $300-$1,000, a living trust $1,500-$5,000, and a comprehensive estate plan (will, trust, power of attorney, healthcare directive) costs $2,500-$10,000. Online services offer simpler documents for $100-$500.
