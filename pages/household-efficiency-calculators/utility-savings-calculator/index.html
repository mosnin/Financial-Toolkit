Utility Savings Formula

Savings Calculations
Monthly Savings per Utility = Current Bill × (Reduction % / 100) Total Monthly Savings = Electric Savings + Gas Savings + Water Savings Annual Savings = Total Monthly Savings × 12 5-Year Cumulative = Annual Savings × 5 CO2 Reduction = (Electric Savings / Avg Rate × 0.852 lbs/kWh) + (Gas Savings / Avg Therm Cost × 11.7 lbs/therm)

CO2 estimates use EPA average emissions factors: 0.386 kg (0.852 lbs) per kWh for electricity and 5.3 kg (11.7 lbs) per therm for natural gas. Actual figures vary by your utility's energy mix.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Gather your bills -- find your last three months of electric, gas, and water bills and average them.
  2. Set reduction targets -- be realistic. A 10% reduction is achievable through behavior changes alone; 20-25% typically requires some equipment upgrades.
  3. Click "Calculate Savings" to see your monthly, annual, and 5-year projected savings.
  4. Review the CO2 figure to understand the environmental impact of your efficiency improvements.

Frequently Asked Questions

Most households can achieve 10-20% reductions through behavioral changes alone. Upgrades like LED lighting, efficient appliances, and improved insulation can push savings to 25-40%.

The EPA estimates 0.852 lbs of CO2 per kWh of electricity and 11.7 lbs per therm of natural gas. This calculator converts dollar savings back to approximate energy units using average national rates to estimate CO2 reduction.

Behavioral changes produce savings immediately. Equipment upgrades like LED bulbs start saving the moment they are installed. Larger investments like insulation or new appliances take months to years to pay back their upfront costs.

Electricity typically offers the greatest dollar savings because it powers heating, cooling, lighting, and appliances. Gas savings are significant in cold climates. Water savings are smaller in dollar terms but matter greatly in drought-prone areas with tiered pricing.
