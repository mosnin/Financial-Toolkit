TikTok Earnings Formulas

Creator Fund Earnings
Creator Fund = (Monthly Views / 1,000) x Creator Fund Rate per 1,000 Views
Total Monthly Earnings
Monthly = Creator Fund + Brand Deals + Affiliate Commissions + Live Gifts
Annual Projection
Annual = Monthly Earnings x 12
Earnings Per 1,000 Views (all sources)
Blended Rate = (Total Monthly Earnings / Monthly Views) x 1,000

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter monthly views — find this in TikTok Analytics under the Overview tab.
  2. Enter creator fund rate — check your Creator Dashboard for your actual rate; $0.50–$0.80/1k is a common starting estimate.
  3. Enter brand deal income — total monthly value of all sponsored content agreements.
  4. Enter affiliate commissions — include TikTok Shop commissions plus any external affiliate programs you promote.
  5. Enter live gifts value — enter the cash value you receive after TikTok's approximately 50% cut on gift withdrawals.
  6. Click Calculate to see your monthly earnings, annual projection, and revenue breakdown by source.

Example Scenario

Taylor is a lifestyle TikTok creator with 2 million monthly views, a creator fund rate of $0.60/1,000 views, $2,000/month in brand deals, $300 in affiliate commissions, and $150 in live gifts.

  • Creator fund: (2,000,000 / 1,000) x $0.60 = $1,200
  • Total monthly: $1,200 + $2,000 + $300 + $150 = $3,650
  • Annual projection: $3,650 x 12 = $43,800
  • Blended rate per 1k views: ($3,650 / 2,000,000) x 1,000 = $1.83

Taylor's blended rate of $1.83 per 1,000 views is 3x her raw creator fund rate, showing the value of diversified monetization.

Frequently Asked Questions

TikTok's Creator Rewards Program typically pays $0.40 to $1.00 per 1,000 views, though rates vary significantly by content type, audience geography, engagement, and video length. Longer videos (over 1 minute) generally earn more.

The TikTok Creator Rewards Program replaced the original Creator Fund and offers improved payouts for longer videos that meet specific engagement thresholds. Eligibility requires 10,000+ followers and 100,000+ views in the past 30 days.

Brand deals on TikTok typically involve a creator posting sponsored content that promotes a product or service. Rates vary widely based on follower count, niche, and engagement rate. Creators with 100k followers may earn $500–$5,000 per post in competitive niches.

Most successful TikTok creators diversify their income beyond the creator fund, which alone is rarely enough for a full-time income. Brand deals, affiliate commissions, and directing traffic to other products or services are critical for sustainable TikTok income.
