Frequently Asked Questions

Standard house cleaning typically costs $120-$250 per visit for an average-sized home (1,500-2,500 sq ft). Costs vary by location, number of rooms, and the cleaning company.

Standard cleaning covers routine tasks like vacuuming, mopping, dusting, and bathroom cleaning. Deep cleaning adds intensive tasks like scrubbing grout, cleaning inside appliances, and detailed attention to often-missed areas. Deep cleaning costs 50-100% more.

Most households benefit from bi-weekly cleaning. Weekly cleaning is ideal for busy families or pet owners, while monthly cleaning works for smaller homes or those who maintain between visits.

Yes, move-out cleaning is typically the most expensive option, costing 75-150% more than standard cleaning. It includes deep cleaning plus tasks like cleaning inside all cabinets and appliances.

Most professional cleaning services bring their own supplies and equipment. Some may charge extra for specialty products or eco-friendly cleaning supplies. Always confirm what is included before booking.
