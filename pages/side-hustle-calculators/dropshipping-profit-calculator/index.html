Dropshipping Profit Formulas

Fees Per Sale
Fees = (Platform Fee % + Payment Processing %) x Selling Price
Return Cost Per Sale
Return Cost = Return Rate % x (Supplier Cost + Ad Spend)
Net Profit Per Sale
Net Profit = Selling Price - Supplier Cost - Fees - Ad Spend - Return Cost
Monthly Profit
Monthly Profit = Net Profit Per Sale x Monthly Sales Volume
Break-Even Selling Price
Break-Even = (Supplier Cost + Ad Spend) / (1 - Platform % - Processing % - Return Rate %)

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the selling price — what customers pay in your store.
  2. Enter supplier cost — the total amount you pay the supplier, including their shipping charge.
  3. Enter platform and payment processing fees — check your Shopify plan and payment processor for exact rates.
  4. Enter ad spend per sale — divide your total monthly ad spend by the number of sales it generates.
  5. Enter your return rate — use industry averages or your own data (5–10% is common for general merchandise).
  6. Enter monthly sales volume to see projected monthly profit at scale.
  7. Review the break-even price — if your current price is below this, you are losing money per sale.

Example Scenario

Alex runs a Shopify store selling a fitness product for $49.99. Supplier cost is $15.00, platform fee is 2%, payment processing is 2.9%, ad spend per sale is $8.00, return rate is 5%, and he sells 100 units/month.

  • Platform + processing fees: $49.99 x 4.9% = $2.45
  • Return cost: 5% x ($15 + $8) = $1.15
  • Net profit per sale: $49.99 - $15 - $2.45 - $8 - $1.15 = $23.39
  • Profit margin: ($23.39 / $49.99) x 100 = 46.8%
  • Monthly profit: $23.39 x 100 = $2,339

A 46.8% margin is strong for dropshipping, leaving good runway for increased ad spend or price testing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Most successful dropshippers target a net profit margin of 15–30% after all costs including ad spend. Margins below 10% leave very little room for error. High-ticket dropshipping often achieves better margins with lower volume.

Break-even price = (Supplier Cost + Ad Spend per Sale) / (1 - Platform Fee % - Payment Processing % - Return Rate %). This is the minimum price where you neither gain nor lose money.

Return rate directly erodes profit because you typically refund the customer but may not recover supplier costs or ad spend. A 5% return rate on a $30 product can eliminate hundreds of dollars of monthly profit at scale.

Shopify charges 0–2% transaction fees plus payment processing (typically 2.9% + $0.30 on Shopify Payments). Shopify Basic plans have no transaction fee if using Shopify Payments. Third-party payment processors add 0.5–2% extra fees.
