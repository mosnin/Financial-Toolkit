Commission Formulas

Real Estate Commission
Total Commission = Sale Price x (Commission Rate / 100) Agent Share = Total Commission x (Agent Split / 100) Brokerage Share = Total Commission - Agent Share Seller Net Proceeds = Sale Price - Total Commission

The total commission is calculated as a percentage of the sale price. This is then divided between the agent and their brokerage according to their agreed-upon split.

Note: In a typical transaction, the total commission is split between the listing side and buyer side first (usually 50/50), then each side is further split between agent and brokerage. This calculator shows the split for one side.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the sale price -- the agreed-upon selling price of the property.
  2. Enter the commission rate -- the total percentage commission (typically 5-6%).
  3. Enter the agent split -- the percentage the agent keeps of their side of the commission (e.g., 70% in a 70/30 split).
  4. Click "Calculate Commission" to see the breakdown.

Example Scenario

$450,000 Home Sale with 6% Commission

Sale price: $450,000. Commission rate: 6%. Agent/brokerage split: 70/30.

Total commission: $450,000 x 0.06 = $27,000.

Agent share: $27,000 x 0.70 = $18,900. Brokerage share: $27,000 x 0.30 = $8,100.

Seller net proceeds (before other closing costs): $450,000 - $27,000 = $423,000.
