How the Scoring Works

Scoring
Total Score = Sum of all 8 behavior ratings (max 40)
33–40 = Mindful Spender  |  25–32 = Conscious Spender
17–24 = Developing Spender  |  8–16 = Impulsive Spender

Each behavior is rated 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent). The assessment draws on behavioral economics research, including work by Thaler & Sunstein on nudge theory and Ariely's research on predictably irrational spending. Higher scores indicate stronger spending self-regulation, which correlates with greater long-term wealth accumulation.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Answer honestly: Rate each behavior based on your actual habits over the past 3 months, not how you aspire to behave.
  2. Review your category: Your category tells you where you stand and how your patterns compare to research benchmarks.
  3. Focus on your lowest scores: The two or three behaviors with the lowest ratings are your highest-leverage improvement opportunities.
  4. Revisit in 90 days: Habits take 60–90 days to solidify. Retake the assessment to measure your progress.

Example Scenario

Jordan scores: Impulsive Buying 2, Budget Tracking 2, Needs vs Wants 3, Sale Resistance 2, Subscription Review 1, Post-Purchase Regret 2, Comparison Shopping 3, Goal Alignment 2. Total: 17/40 — Developing Spender.

Top recommendations: Start a subscription audit (potential monthly savings: $40–$120), implement a 48-hour rule for non-essential purchases over $50, and set up a free budgeting app to begin tracking. These three changes alone could shift Jordan into the Conscious Spender tier within 90 days.
