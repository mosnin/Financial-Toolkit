Dividend Yield Formula

The dividend yield is calculated by dividing the annual dividend per share by the current stock price:

Dividend Yield Formula
Dividend Yield = (Annual Dividend Per Share / Current Stock Price) x 100

The effective yield accounts for quarterly compounding when dividends are reinvested:

Effective Yield Formula
Effective Yield = (1 + Yield/4)^4 - 1

How to Use This Dividend Yield Calculator

  1. Enter the current stock price - the market price per share of the stock.
  2. Enter the annual dividend per share - the total dividend paid per share over a year.
  3. Click "Calculate Yield" to see the dividend yield percentage and effective yield.

Example Scenario

Suppose a stock trades at $150.00 per share and pays an annual dividend of $3.50 per share. The dividend yield would be 2.33%. If dividends are reinvested quarterly, the effective yield would be approximately 2.35%, reflecting the compounding benefit.
