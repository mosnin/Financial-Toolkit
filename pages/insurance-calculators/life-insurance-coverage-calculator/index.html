Life Insurance Coverage Formula

The recommended coverage is calculated using the income replacement method with adjustments for debts and dependents:

Coverage Needs Formula
Recommended Coverage = (Annual Income x Years) + Total Debts + (Dependents x $40,000) - Existing Coverage

Income replacement ensures your family maintains their standard of living. Debt coverage pays off mortgages, loans, and credit cards. The dependent allowance accounts for childcare, education, and related expenses.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your annual income - your gross annual earnings.
  2. Enter years to replace - how many years your family would need income support.
  3. Enter total debts - mortgage, car loans, student loans, credit cards, etc.
  4. Enter number of dependents - children and others who rely on your income.
  5. Enter existing coverage - any life insurance you already have.
  6. Click "Calculate Coverage" to see your recommended coverage amount.

Example Scenario

A 35-year-old earning $75,000 per year with 20 years of income replacement needed, $200,000 in debts, 2 dependents, and $50,000 in existing employer coverage would need approximately $1,730,000 in total life insurance. This breaks down as $1,500,000 for income replacement, $200,000 for debts, $80,000 for dependents, minus $50,000 existing coverage.
