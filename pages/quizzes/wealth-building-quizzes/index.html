Frequently Asked Questions

A common benchmark is to have net worth equal to your annual salary by age 30, 3x by age 40, and 7x by age 60. Saving 15–20% of gross income and investing consistently in diversified assets are the two strongest predictors of long-term wealth accumulation.

Starting from $0, investing $1,000/month at a 7% average annual return takes roughly 30 years. Investing $2,000/month cuts that to about 22 years. Increasing your savings rate is more powerful than chasing higher returns — use our Millionaire Timeline Calculator to model your specific scenario.

Saving means accumulating cash — it's safe but slow. Wealth building means deploying that capital into productive assets (stocks, real estate, a business) that generate returns above inflation. True wealth building requires both: saving enough to invest, then ensuring your investments are working efficiently.
