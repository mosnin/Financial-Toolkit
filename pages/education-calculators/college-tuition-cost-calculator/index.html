Tuition Projection Formula

Future tuition is projected using the compound growth formula:

Projected Tuition Formula
Future Tuition = Current Tuition x (1 + r)^n

Where r is the annual tuition inflation rate (as a decimal) and n is the number of years until enrollment. The 4-year total sums each year's projected tuition, accounting for continued inflation during the college years.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the current annual tuition - look up the current published tuition for your target school.
  2. Enter years until enrollment - how many years before the student begins college.
  3. Enter the expected inflation rate - the average annual tuition increase you expect (5% is a common estimate).
  4. Click "Calculate Projected Cost" to see the estimated tuition at enrollment and the full 4-year total.

Example Scenario

Suppose current annual tuition is $25,000 and your child will enroll in 8 years, with an expected tuition inflation rate of 5% per year. At enrollment, annual tuition would be approximately $36,932. The projected 4-year total (with continued 5% increases each year) would be approximately $159,398.
