Credit Utilization Formula

Credit utilization is calculated by dividing your total credit card balances by your total credit limits:

Credit Utilization Ratio
Utilization = (Total Balances / Total Credit Limit) x 100

For example, if you have $3,000 in balances across cards with a combined $20,000 limit, your utilization is 15%.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Add up your total credit limit - check your statements or online accounts for each card's limit and sum them.
  2. Add up your total balances - the current balance on each credit card combined.
  3. Click "Calculate Utilization" to see your ratio, score impact assessment, and personalized recommendations.

Example Scenario

Mike has three credit cards: Card A with a $10,000 limit and $1,500 balance, Card B with a $5,000 limit and $500 balance, and Card C with a $5,000 limit and $1,000 balance. His total limit is $20,000 and total balance is $3,000, giving him a 15% utilization rate. This is in the good range and has a positive effect on his credit score.
