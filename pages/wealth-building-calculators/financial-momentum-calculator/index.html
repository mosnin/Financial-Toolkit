Financial Momentum Calculator

Financial momentum is the speed at which your wealth is growing right now — measured in dollars per month. Unlike a simple balance snapshot, momentum shows you whether your wealth is accelerating, decelerating, or stalling. This calculator computes your current wealth velocity, assigns a momentum score, and tells you exactly when your financial trajectory will reach the key milestones of $100k, $250k, $500k, and $1 million.

Financial Momentum Formula

Wealth velocity is the sum of monthly savings plus the monthly investment return on existing net worth:

Wealth Velocity ($/month)
V = PMTmonthly + NW × (r / 12)
Momentum Score
Score = V / NW × 100 (monthly growth %)
Years to Milestone
t = ln(Target / NW0 + PMT/r × NW0) / ln(1 + r/12)

Where PMT is monthly savings, NW is current net worth, and r is annual return rate. Milestone years are calculated by iterative projection until each target is reached.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your current net worth — your total assets minus liabilities.
  2. Enter your monthly savings rate ($) — how much you save and invest each month.
  3. Enter your current monthly income — used to calculate your savings rate percentage.
  4. Enter your annual return rate — expected annual investment return.
  5. Enter years to project — your planning horizon.
  6. Click "Calculate Momentum" to see your wealth velocity, momentum score, and milestone timeline.

Example Scenario

A 35-year-old with a $75,000 net worth, saving $1,000/month, earning $6,000/month, with a 7% annual return has a current wealth velocity of $1,438/month ($1,000 savings + $438 monthly investment return). The momentum score is 1.92% per month. At this trajectory, they reach $250,000 in about 8 years, $500,000 in 14 years, and $1 million in approximately 22 years — demonstrating how momentum accelerates as the portfolio grows.

Frequently Asked Questions

Wealth velocity is how fast your net worth is growing in dollar terms per month. It combines your monthly savings contributions with investment returns on your existing portfolio. As your portfolio grows, investment returns add more to velocity even without increasing contributions — this is the core compounding dynamic.

The momentum score in this calculator is the ratio of your monthly wealth velocity to your current net worth, expressed as a monthly growth percentage. A score above 1% per month indicates strong momentum. Most wealth-builders operate in the 0.3–0.8% range depending on net worth and savings rate.

As your net worth grows, investment returns generate an increasing dollar amount per month even at the same percentage return. Your momentum "snowballs" — a 7% annual return on $100,000 adds about $583/month, but the same return on $500,000 adds $2,917/month — nearly a 5x velocity increase without any change in behavior.

The savings rate here is your monthly savings as a percentage of your monthly income. It is used to calculate your financial momentum score and to contextualize your wealth-building pace. A higher savings rate directly increases both your monthly contributions and long-term momentum.
