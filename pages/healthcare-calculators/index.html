Frequently Asked Questions

An HSA is a tax-advantaged account for medical expenses available to people enrolled in a High Deductible Health Plan (HDHP). Contributions are tax-deductible, growth is tax-free, and withdrawals for qualified medical expenses are also tax-free. Use our Health Savings Account Calculator to see how much you can save and what your annual tax benefit is worth.

Your deductible is the amount you pay before insurance kicks in. A copay is a fixed amount you pay per visit or prescription. Coinsurance is the percentage of costs you share with your insurer after meeting your deductible. Our Out-of-Pocket Cost Calculator combines all three to show your true annual healthcare expense.

ACA premium tax credits are available to individuals and families with incomes between 100% and 400% of the federal poverty level who are not eligible for other qualifying coverage. Our Health Insurance Subsidy Calculator estimates your subsidy amount based on your household income, size, and location.

HDHPs make sense if you are generally healthy, have low expected medical costs, and want to take advantage of HSA tax savings. If you have chronic conditions or expect significant medical expenses, a lower-deductible plan may cost less overall. Compare total annual costs using our Out-of-Pocket Cost Calculator before choosing a plan.

A common starting point is 5% to 10% of your gross income, though this varies widely by age, health status, and coverage type. Include your annual premium, expected out-of-pocket costs, dental, vision, and any regular prescription costs. Our Out-of-Pocket Cost Calculator and Medical Procedure Cost Estimator help you build a realistic annual healthcare budget.
