Frequently Asked Questions

A common benchmark from "The Millionaire Next Door" suggests your net worth should be at least (Age × Annual Pre-Tax Income) / 10. More practically: 1x your income by 30, 3x by 40, 6x by 50, and 8x by 60. These are guidelines, not rules — starting late and accelerating is always better than not starting.

Standard financial advice recommends saving 15–20% of gross income for retirement alone. Those targeting early financial independence often save 30–50%+. Even increasing your savings rate by 5% meaningfully compresses your timeline to financial freedom. The savings rate matters more than investment returns for most people.

Passive income — dividends, rental income, royalties, interest — is the hallmark of advanced wealth building. It reduces your dependence on earned income and accelerates compounding. Even $200–$500/month in passive income dramatically changes your wealth trajectory over 10–20 years. Focus on building this stream once your debt is cleared and emergency fund is solid.
