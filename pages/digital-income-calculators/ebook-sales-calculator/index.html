Ebook Revenue Formulas

Royalty per Copy
Royalty per Copy = List Price x (Royalty Rate / 100)
Monthly Royalty Income

Annual Royalty Income

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your monthly sales — the total number of ebook copies sold across all titles each month.
  2. Set your list price — the average selling price per copy. If you sell at different prices, use a weighted average.
  3. Enter your royalty rate — 70% for Amazon KDP in the qualifying price range, 35% for titles outside that range, or 90-97% for direct sales platforms.
  4. Enter the number of titles you have published to see per-title monthly income.
  5. Optionally enter a monthly growth rate to project where your income could be in 12 months.
  6. Click Calculate Revenue to see monthly royalties, annual income, and per-title averages.

Example Scenario

An author publishes 5 ebooks on Amazon KDP, each priced at $4.99, and sells a combined 300 copies per month. Sales grow at 8% per month as reviews accumulate.

  • Royalty per copy: $4.99 x 70% = $3.49
  • Monthly royalty: 300 x $3.49 = $1,047/month
  • Annual royalty: $1,047 x 12 = $12,564/year (at flat sales)
  • Income per title: $1,047 / 5 = $209/title/month
  • Month 12 projection (8% growth): $1,047 x (1.08)^11 = ~$2,390/month

Frequently Asked Questions

Amazon KDP pays a 70% royalty on ebooks priced between $2.99 and $9.99 (in select territories), and a 35% royalty on ebooks priced outside that range or in certain countries. There is also a small delivery fee deducted for the 70% tier based on file size. Enrolling in KDP Select can provide additional income from Kindle Unlimited page reads.

Sales vary widely. New authors with no platform may sell fewer than 10 copies per month initially. Authors with an email list, strong reviews, and active marketing can sell 100-1,000+ copies monthly. Bestselling self-published authors in competitive niches may sell thousands of copies per month across multiple titles.

Selling directly through your own site (via Gumroad, Payhip, or your own checkout) keeps 95-100% of revenue but requires you to drive all traffic. Platforms like Amazon offer built-in discovery but take a larger cut. Many authors use both: selling direct to their audience while leveraging Amazon for discovery. Direct sales generate higher margins per copy.

For Amazon, $2.99-$4.99 is a sweet spot that qualifies for 70% royalties and attracts impulse buyers. For nonfiction or how-to ebooks sold directly, $9.99-$29.99 is common. Premium guides or professional ebooks sold through your own channels can command $49-$197 or more. Pricing should reflect the value delivered and your audience's willingness to pay.
