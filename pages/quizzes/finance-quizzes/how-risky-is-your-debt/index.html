How Risky Is Your Debt?

Not all debt is created equal — a low-interest mortgage is fundamentally different from a high-APR credit card balance. This 8-question quiz analyses the composition, scale, and trajectory of your debt to give you an honest risk rating and a specific plan to reduce it.
