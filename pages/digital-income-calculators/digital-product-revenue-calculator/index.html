Digital Product Revenue Formulas

Gross Revenue
Gross Revenue = Product Price x Monthly Sales
Net After Refunds
Net After Refunds = Gross Revenue x (1 - Refund Rate / 100)
Platform + Processing Fees
Fees = (Net After Refunds x Platform Fee%) + (Sales x (Processing% x Price + Flat Fee))
Net Profit
Net Profit = Net After Refunds - All Fees - Ad Spend
Cost Per Acquisition
CPA = Ad Spend / Monthly Sales

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your product price — the selling price of your digital product.
  2. Set your monthly sales volume — how many units you sell or expect to sell per month.
  3. Enter your platform fee percentage — check your platform's current pricing (Gumroad, Teachable, Podia, etc.).
  4. Set your payment processing fee — the percentage and flat fee charged by Stripe, PayPal, or your payment processor.
  5. Enter your refund rate — the percentage of sales that are refunded.
  6. Set your monthly ad spend — total advertising budget (enter 0 for organic sales).
  7. Click Calculate Revenue for gross revenue, net after fees, profit margin, annual revenue, and CPA.

Example Scenario

A creator sells an online course for $197, with 30 sales/month, 10% Gumroad platform fee, 2.9% + $0.30 Stripe fee, 4% refund rate, and $300/month in Facebook ads.

  • Gross Revenue: 30 x $197 = $5,910
  • Refund deduction (4%): -$236 = Net $5,674
  • Platform Fee (10%): -$567
  • Processing (2.9% + $0.30 x 30): -$174
  • Ad Spend: -$300
  • Net Profit: $5,674 - $567 - $174 - $300 = $4,633
  • Profit Margin: $4,633 / $5,910 = 78.4%
  • CPA: $300 / 30 = $10 per sale

Frequently Asked Questions

Platform fees vary: Gumroad charges 10% on the free plan. Teachable charges 5% on Basic, 0% on Pro/Business. Podia charges 0%. Payhip charges 5% on the free plan. Always check current pricing as platforms regularly update their fee structures.

Refund rates for digital products typically range from 1% to 5%. Online courses often see 3-7% refund rates, especially with 30-day money-back guarantees. Ebooks and smaller downloads usually see 1-3% refund rates.

CPA is total advertising spend divided by the number of sales. $1,000 ad spend generating 50 sales = $20 CPA. To be profitable, your CPA must be significantly lower than your net revenue per sale after fees and refunds.

After platform fees and advertising, healthy digital product businesses target 50-80% profit margins. Lower margins can be acceptable when scaling with paid traffic as volume compensates. Organic sales with minimal fees can achieve 85-95% margins.
