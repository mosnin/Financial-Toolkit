Frequently Asked Questions

The U.S. uses a progressive tax system with marginal tax brackets. Each bracket taxes only the income within that range, not your entire income. Your effective tax rate is usually lower than your marginal rate because lower portions of income are taxed at lower rates.

Quarterly estimated tax payments are due April 15, June 15, September 15, and January 15 of the following year. If a due date falls on a weekend or holiday, the deadline moves to the next business day.

Short-term capital gains apply to assets held for one year or less and are taxed at your ordinary income tax rate. Long-term capital gains apply to assets held for more than one year and are taxed at preferential rates of 0%, 15%, or 20% depending on your income.

Take whichever is higher. The 2025 standard deduction is $15,000 for single filers and $30,000 for married filing jointly. Itemize if your deductible expenses (mortgage interest, state taxes, charitable donations, medical expenses) exceed these amounts.

Self-employment tax is 15.3% on net self-employment earnings (12.4% Social Security on income up to $168,600 plus 2.9% Medicare on all earnings). You can deduct half of this tax when calculating your adjusted gross income.
