Calculation Methodology

Total Monthly Cost
Total Monthly = Sum of all active tool monthly costs
Annual Cost (Monthly Billing)
Annual (Monthly) = Total Monthly × 12
Annual Savings (Annual vs Monthly Billing)
Savings = Annual (Monthly Billing) − Annual (Annual Billing, ~20% discount avg.)
Cost per User per Month
Per User = Tool Monthly Cost / Tool User Count
Cost per Employee per Month
Per Employee = Total Monthly / Total Headcount

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your total team size (headcount) and select your default billing cycle.
  2. For each SaaS tool, enter the tool name, monthly cost (enter the annual price divided by 12 for annually-billed tools), and number of seats/users.
  3. Leave tool name blank to skip that row.
  4. Click Calculate Total Cost to see total monthly/annual spend, per-user cost, cost per employee, and potential annual billing savings.

Example Scenario

A 20-person startup with the following SaaS stack on monthly billing:

  • Slack (20 users): $160/mo ($8/user)
  • Notion (20 users): $160/mo ($8/user)
  • HubSpot Starter (5 users): $90/mo
  • GitHub Team (10 users): $40/mo
  • Figma Professional (3 users): $45/mo
  • Google Workspace (20 users): $240/mo ($12/user)

Total: $735/month ($8,820/year) — Switching all to annual billing at a 20% average discount would save approximately $1,764/year. Cost per employee: $36.75/month.

Frequently Asked Questions

Small businesses (under 50 employees) spend an average of $25,000–$100,000 per year on SaaS. Mid-size companies average $100,000–$500,000 annually. The typical company runs 80–100 different SaaS applications.

Most SaaS vendors offer 15–25% discounts for annual prepayment. On a $1,000/month stack, switching to annual billing could save $1,800–$3,000 per year. The trade-off is reduced flexibility if your needs change during the year.

SaaS sprawl is the uncontrolled growth of software subscriptions, often with redundant tools. Control it by auditing subscriptions quarterly, consolidating overlapping tools, removing unused licenses, and requiring central approval for new software purchases.

Divide total monthly SaaS spend by your total headcount. For example, $5,000/month for 25 employees equals $200/employee/month. This metric helps benchmark against peers and justify or reduce the software budget during planning.
