Property Tax Formula

Property taxes are calculated using assessed value, the assessment ratio, and the mill rate:

Formula
Assessed Value = Market Value × Assessment Ratio
Taxable Value = Assessed Value − Exemptions
Annual Tax = Taxable Value × Tax Rate

Many jurisdictions don't tax 100% of market value — they apply an assessment ratio (often 70–100%). If your state has a 90% assessment ratio on a $400,000 home, the assessed value is $360,000.

Example

A home worth $400,000 in a county with a 1.25% tax rate and a $25,000 homestead exemption:

  • Taxable value: $400,000 − $25,000 = $375,000
  • Annual tax: $375,000 × 1.25% = $4,687.50
  • Monthly: $390.63
