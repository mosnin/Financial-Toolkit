Deck Material Formula

Deck materials are estimated based on deck area with a multiplier for substructure and waste:

Material Formula
Board Feet = Deck Area (sqft) x 2.5 (includes joists, substructure, 10% waste) Material Cost = Deck Area x Cost per sqft + Railing Length x Railing Cost per ft

Cost per square foot varies by material: pressure-treated $8-$12, composite $18-$28, cedar $14-$22. Railing costs $25-$60 per linear foot depending on material.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter deck length and width - the overall dimensions of your deck in feet.
  2. Select the decking material - pressure-treated, composite, or cedar.
  3. Enter railing length - the total linear feet of railing needed (0 if no railing).
  4. Click "Calculate Materials" to see board feet, material cost, and total estimate.

Example Scenario

A 20 x 15 foot deck (300 sq ft) using composite decking with 40 feet of railing would cost approximately $8,700. The decking material costs about $6,900 ($23/sqft), railing adds about $1,800 ($45/ft), and you would need about 750 board feet of decking material.

Frequently Asked Questions

Deck costs range from $15-$25/sqft for pressure-treated wood, $25-$45 for cedar, and $35-$65 for composite. A typical 300-square-foot deck costs $4,500-$19,500 depending on material and features.

Pressure-treated wood is the most affordable but requires regular maintenance. Cedar offers natural beauty and rot resistance. Composite is the most durable and lowest maintenance but costs more upfront.

Multiply the deck area by 2.5 to account for joists, substructure, and 10% waste. For a 300-square-foot deck, plan for about 750 board feet.

Deck railing costs $20-$40 per linear foot for wood, $30-$60 for composite, and $50-$120+ for cable or glass systems. Railing can add 20-30% to total deck cost.

Most jurisdictions require a building permit for attached decks and decks over 30 inches above grade. Permit costs range from $100-$500. Check with your local building department.
