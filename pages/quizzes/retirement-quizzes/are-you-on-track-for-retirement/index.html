Are You On Track for Retirement?

This 8-question quiz benchmarks your retirement savings against age-based milestones (1x, 3x, 6x, and 10x your salary), evaluates your savings rate, investment mix, employer match participation, debt load, Social Security knowledge, and target retirement age. Answer honestly to see whether you're on track — or how far you need to go to get there.

Frequently Asked Questions

Fidelity's widely-cited benchmarks suggest having 1x your salary saved by age 30, 3x by 40, 6x by 50, and 10x by 67. These are general guidelines — your specific number depends on your planned retirement lifestyle, Social Security income, and expected longevity. Use them as checkpoints, not strict rules.

Most financial advisors recommend saving 15% of gross income for retirement (including any employer match). If you started late, you may need to save 20–25% to catch up. The earlier you start, the lower your required savings rate due to the compounding effect on your investments.

Failing to capture your full employer 401(k) match is effectively leaving part of your compensation on the table. A typical 50% match on up to 6% of salary means if you earn $60,000, you forfeit up to $1,800 per year — plus all its future compounded growth. Over a 30-year career, this could cost you $150,000 or more.
