Water Savings Formula

Savings Calculation
Gallons Saved/Day = Current Daily Use × Savings % Annual Gallons Saved = Gallons Saved/Day × 365 Annual Bill Savings = (Annual Gallons Saved / 1,000) × Water Rate Monthly Bill Reduction = Annual Bill Savings / 12 Payback Period = Upgrade Cost / Annual Bill Savings Savings % by upgrade type: Low-Flow Fixtures: ~25% of indoor water use Efficient Appliances: ~20% of total use Drip Irrigation: ~35% of outdoor water use

Average US household uses approximately 80-100 gallons per person per day. Indoor uses include toilets (24%), showers (20%), faucets (19%), and laundry (17%). The calculator applies savings percentages based on EPA WaterSense research.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Find your current monthly water bill from your utility statement.
  2. Estimate your daily usage -- your bill may show gallons used per billing period; divide by days to get daily use.
  3. Enter your water rate per 1,000 gallons -- found on your utility bill.
  4. Select your upgrade type -- each has a different typical savings percentage.
  5. Enter upgrade cost (optional) to see the payback period.

Example Scenario

Low-Flow Fixture Installation -- Family of 4

Monthly bill: $60 | Daily use: 320 gallons | Rate: $6.00/1,000 gal | Upgrade: Low-Flow Fixtures | Cost: $300

Gallons saved per day: 320 × 25% = 80 gallons/day

Annual gallons saved: 80 × 365 = 29,200 gallons/year

Annual bill savings: (29,200 / 1,000) × $6.00 = $175.20/year

Monthly reduction: $175.20 / 12 = $14.60/month

Payback period: $300 / $175.20 = 1.7 years
