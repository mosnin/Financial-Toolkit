LTV Formula

The loan-to-value ratio is a straightforward calculation:

Loan-to-Value Ratio
LTV = (Loan Amount / Property Value) x 100

An LTV above 80% typically triggers a PMI requirement. Equity equals the property value minus the loan balance.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the loan amount - the total mortgage balance or amount you plan to borrow.
  2. Enter the property value - the appraised or purchase price of the property.
  3. Click "Calculate LTV" to see your ratio, PMI status, and equity.

Example Scenario

If you have a $240,000 loan on a $300,000 property, your LTV is 80.0%. You are right at the threshold where PMI may or may not be required. Your equity is $60,000, or 20% of the property value.

Frequently Asked Questions

LTV is the ratio of your mortgage loan amount to the appraised value of the property, expressed as a percentage. An LTV of 80% means you borrowed 80% of the home's value.

Private mortgage insurance is typically required when the LTV exceeds 80%, meaning you put less than 20% down. PMI can be removed once your LTV drops to 78-80%.

A lower LTV generally qualifies you for better interest rates because the lender has less risk. Borrowers with LTV below 80% typically get the best rates.
