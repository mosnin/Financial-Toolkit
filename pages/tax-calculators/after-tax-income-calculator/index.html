How After-Tax Income Is Calculated

Step 1: Determine AGI
AGI = Gross Income − Pre-Tax Deductions (401k, HSA, etc.)
Step 2: Federal Income Tax
Taxable Income = AGI − Standard Deduction
Apply progressive brackets (10% to 37%)
Step 3: FICA
Social Security = min(Gross, $168,600) × 6.2%
Medicare = Gross × 1.45%
Step 4: State Income Tax
State Tax = AGI × Effective State Rate (varies by state)
Step 5: Net Income
Net = Gross − Federal Tax − State Tax − Social Security − Medicare

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your annual gross income (before any deductions).
  2. Select your filing status for the correct standard deduction and brackets.
  3. Add any pre-tax deductions like 401(k) contributions or HSA deposits.
  4. Select your state to include state income tax in the estimate.
  5. Click Calculate Net Income to see your breakdown.

Example Scenario

A single filer in California earning $85,000 per year with $5,000 in 401(k) contributions:

  • AGI: $85,000 − $5,000 = $80,000
  • Federal Taxable Income: $80,000 − $14,600 = $65,400 → Federal Tax: ~$9,848
  • Social Security: $85,000 × 6.2% = $5,270
  • Medicare: $85,000 × 1.45% = $1,233
  • California State Tax (~9.3%): $80,000 × 9.3% = ~$7,440
  • Net Take-Home: $85,000 − $9,848 − $5,270 − $1,233 − $7,440 = ~$61,209/yr ($5,101/mo)
