Freelance Income Formula

Your net freelance income is calculated as:

Net Freelance Income Formula
Net Income = (Hourly Rate x Billable Hours x Weeks) - Expenses - Taxes

Where taxes include both income tax and self-employment tax (15.3%). Your effective hourly rate is your net income divided by total hours worked.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your hourly rate - the rate you charge clients per hour.
  2. Enter billable hours per week - hours you can actually bill to clients.
  3. Enter weeks per year - account for vacation and downtime (typically 46-50).
  4. Enter annual business expenses - software, equipment, insurance, etc.
  5. Enter your estimated tax rate - combined income tax and self-employment tax.
  6. Click "Calculate Income" to see your complete income projection.

Example Scenario

A freelance developer charging $75/hour, billing 25 hours/week for 48 weeks, with $10,000 in expenses and a 30% combined tax rate: Gross income is $90,000, after expenses $80,000, taxes $24,000, net income $56,000. Effective hourly rate based on 40-hour weeks is about $23.33.

Frequently Asked Questions

Multiply your hourly rate by billable hours per week, then by weeks worked per year. Subtract expenses and estimated taxes (including self-employment tax of 15.3%) to get net income.

The self-employment tax rate is 15.3%, covering both employer and employee portions of Social Security (12.4%) and Medicare (2.9%).

Common deductible expenses include home office costs, equipment, software, internet, phone, professional development, travel, marketing, and health insurance premiums.

Most freelancers can bill 20-30 hours per week, with remaining time on admin, marketing, and business development. 25 billable hours is realistic.

Your effective hourly rate is net income divided by total hours worked (including non-billable time). It shows what you actually earn per hour after expenses and taxes.
