Hourly to Salary Formula

The conversion from an hourly rate to an annual salary is straightforward:

Annual Salary Formula
Annual Salary = Hourly Rate x Hours Per Week x Weeks Per Year

For a standard full-time schedule, this is Hourly Rate x 40 x 52 = Hourly Rate x 2,080.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your hourly rate - the amount you earn per hour of work.
  2. Enter hours per week - typically 40 for full-time work.
  3. Enter weeks per year - use 52 for paid time off or fewer for unpaid time off.
  4. Click "Calculate Salary" to see your annual, monthly, biweekly, and weekly salary.

Example Scenario

If you earn $25 per hour and work 40 hours per week for 52 weeks, your annual salary would be $52,000. That is $4,333.33 per month, $2,000 biweekly, $1,000 per week, and $200 per day.

Frequently Asked Questions

Multiply your hourly rate by the number of hours you work per week, then multiply by the number of weeks you work per year. For example, $25/hour x 40 hours x 52 weeks = $52,000 annual salary.

A standard full-time work year is 2,080 hours (40 hours per week x 52 weeks). However, accounting for vacation and holidays, actual working hours may be closer to 1,920-2,000 hours.

Use 52 weeks if you receive paid time off (PTO) since you are paid for all 52 weeks. Use 50 weeks (or fewer) if you have unpaid time off, to get a more accurate annual income estimate.

$20 per hour working 40 hours a week for 52 weeks equals $41,600 per year before taxes. Monthly that is approximately $3,467, and biweekly it is approximately $1,600.
