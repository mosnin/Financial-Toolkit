State Sales Tax Formula

Tax Calculation
Tax Amount = Purchase Price × Effective State Rate
Total Price = Purchase Price + Tax Amount

Note: State rates shown are base state rates. Most localities add county/city taxes on top. The combined average state + local rate is typically 1–3% higher than the state rate shown.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the purchase price before tax.
  2. Select the state where the purchase is made.
  3. Select the product category to apply the correct exemption or reduced rate.
  4. Click Calculate Sales Tax.

Example Scenario

Buying a $250 jacket in California:

  • California State Rate: 7.25%
  • Tax: $250 × 7.25% = $18.13
  • Total: $250 + $18.13 = $268.13
  • Note: Add local taxes (e.g., Los Angeles adds ~2.25%) for the full combined rate.
