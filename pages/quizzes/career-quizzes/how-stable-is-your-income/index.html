How Stable Is Your Income?

Income stability is one of the most overlooked dimensions of financial health. It's not just about how much you earn — it's about how predictable and secure that income is. This quiz assesses your employment type, industry resilience, company health, skills replaceability, and financial buffers to give you a clear stability score.

Frequently Asked Questions

Healthcare, utilities, government, essential retail (grocery), and education tend to hold up best in recessions. Technology and finance can be volatile. Hospitality, travel, luxury goods, and construction are historically most vulnerable. Being in a recession-resistant industry doesn't guarantee individual job security, but it significantly reduces systemic risk.

The standard 3-month fund is designed for stable employment situations. If your income is volatile or your industry is risky, target 6–12 months. Freelancers and self-employed professionals often need 6+ months due to payment delays and income variability. Use the emergency fund calculator to find your exact target.

Income diversification can include freelancing or consulting on the side, dividend-paying investments, rental income, digital products, or a part-time business. The key is building income streams that don't all depend on the same single employer. Even $500–$1,000/month in side income materially reduces your financial vulnerability.
