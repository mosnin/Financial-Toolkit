YouTube Earnings Formulas

AdSense Revenue
AdSense Revenue = (Monthly Views / 1,000) x RPM
Total Monthly Earnings
Monthly Earnings = AdSense Revenue + Sponsorships + Affiliate Income + Merchandise
Annual Earnings Projection
Annual = Monthly Earnings x 12
Earnings Per View
Earnings Per View = Total Monthly Earnings / Monthly Views

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter monthly views — use your YouTube Studio analytics for accurate data.
  2. Enter your RPM — find this in YouTube Studio under Revenue tab. Use $3–$5 as a starting estimate if you are not yet monetized.
  3. Enter sponsorship income — include all brand deal and integration payments you receive monthly.
  4. Enter affiliate income — total commissions earned from links in video descriptions or pinned comments.
  5. Enter merchandise revenue — sales from YouTube Merch Shelf or external stores like Shopify or Gumroad.
  6. Click Calculate to see total monthly and annual earnings plus your earnings per view ratio.

Example Scenario

Jordan runs a personal finance YouTube channel with 150,000 monthly views and an RPM of $8.50. He earns $2,000/month in sponsorships, $600 in affiliate commissions, and $150 in merch sales.

  • AdSense revenue: (150,000 / 1,000) x $8.50 = $1,275
  • Total monthly earnings: $1,275 + $2,000 + $600 + $150 = $4,025
  • Annual projection: $4,025 x 12 = $48,300
  • Earnings per view: $4,025 / 150,000 = $0.0268/view

Jordan earns about 2.7 cents per view — well above the AdSense-only rate — demonstrating the power of diversified YouTube income streams.

Frequently Asked Questions

RPM (Revenue Per Mille) is the amount you actually earn per 1,000 views after YouTube's 45% cut. CPM is what advertisers pay per 1,000 ad impressions. RPM is always lower than CPM and is the more useful metric for creators estimating their own income.

YouTube RPM varies widely by niche, audience geography, and time of year. Finance and business channels often see $10–30+ RPM. Entertainment and gaming channels may see $2–6 RPM. US and UK audiences typically generate higher RPMs than global audiences.

On average, YouTube creators earn $2–$10 per 1,000 views through AdSense alone. However, total channel revenue including sponsorships, affiliate income, and merchandise can be 2–5x the AdSense revenue for established creators.

To join the YouTube Partner Program and earn AdSense revenue, you need at least 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 watch hours in the past 12 months, or 1,000 subscribers and 10 million Shorts views in 90 days.
