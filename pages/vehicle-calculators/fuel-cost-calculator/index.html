Fuel Cost Formula

Fuel cost is calculated using a straightforward formula:

Annual Fuel Cost
Annual Fuel Cost = (Miles Per Year / MPG) x Price Per Gallon
Cost Per Mile
Cost Per Mile = Price Per Gallon / MPG

How to Use This Fuel Cost Calculator

  1. Enter your annual miles - how many miles you drive per year (the average is about 13,500).
  2. Enter your vehicle's MPG - the combined fuel efficiency rating of your vehicle.
  3. Enter the fuel price - the current price per gallon in your area.
  4. Click "Calculate Fuel Cost" to see your annual cost, monthly cost, and cost per mile.

Example Scenario

If you drive 15,000 miles per year in a vehicle that gets 25 MPG and gas costs $3.50 per gallon, you would use 600 gallons of fuel annually. Your annual fuel cost would be $2,100 ($175 per month), and your fuel cost per mile would be $0.14.

Frequently Asked Questions

Divide your annual miles driven by your vehicle's MPG to get gallons used, then multiply by the price per gallon. For example, 15,000 miles at 25 MPG with $3.50/gallon gas costs $2,100 per year.

The average fuel cost per mile depends on gas prices and your vehicle's fuel efficiency. With gas at $3.50/gallon and a vehicle getting 25 MPG, the fuel cost per mile is about $0.14. More efficient vehicles can be under $0.10 per mile.

You can reduce fuel costs by maintaining proper tire pressure, avoiding aggressive driving, using cruise control on highways, keeping up with vehicle maintenance, reducing idling, and combining trips.

The average American drives approximately 13,500 miles per year according to the Federal Highway Administration. This varies by state, age, and whether you commute by car.

In most areas, driving an electric vehicle costs significantly less per mile than a gas car. The average EV costs about $0.04-$0.05 per mile in electricity, compared to $0.10-$0.20 per mile for gasoline. Use our EV Charging Cost Calculator for a detailed comparison.
