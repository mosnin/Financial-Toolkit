Frequently Asked Questions

Studies show the average US household subscribes to 4–5 streaming services, spending $50–$80 per month on streaming alone. Over a year, that adds up to $600–$960 in entertainment subscriptions. Our Streaming Service Cost Calculator totals all your active services so you can see the full picture and decide which ones are worth keeping.

It depends on how long you plan to use the software and how frequently it is updated. Perpetual licenses cost more upfront but can be cheaper over 3–5 years if you do not need every new version. Subscriptions include automatic updates and are typically more cost-effective for tools that evolve rapidly. Our Software Licensing Cost Calculator runs the 5-year comparison for you.

For a household with 2–3 people streaming in HD and working from home, 100–200 Mbps download speeds are typically sufficient. 4K streaming requires about 25 Mbps per device. Video calls need 3–8 Mbps per stream. Our Internet Speed Requirement Calculator adds up your simultaneous usage to recommend the minimum plan you need without overpaying for more than necessary.

SaaS sprawl happens when teams independently adopt tools without central visibility into total spending. Best practices include maintaining a software inventory, reviewing subscriptions quarterly, consolidating overlapping tools, and negotiating annual contracts for tools with confirmed long-term value. Our SaaS Subscription Cost Calculator helps you build that inventory and quantify total annual spend per tool and per seat.

The right cloud storage plan depends on how much data you need to store, how many devices you sync, and whether you need collaboration features. Free tiers (15–50 GB) work for light users. Power users and families typically need 200 GB to 2 TB. Our Cloud Storage Cost Calculator compares pricing across major providers so you can find the best value for your storage needs.
