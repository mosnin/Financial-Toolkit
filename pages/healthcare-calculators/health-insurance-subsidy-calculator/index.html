How the ACA Subsidy Formula Works

The ACA premium tax credit is based on the difference between the benchmark Silver plan premium and your expected contribution:

FPL % (2026 estimates)
FPL % = Annual Income / FPL for Household Size × 100
Expected Contribution (% of income)
Ranges from 0% (under 150% FPL) to 8.5% (400%+ FPL)
Subsidy Amount (monthly)
Subsidy = Benchmark Silver Premium - (Income × Contribution % / 12)

If this result is negative, no subsidy is available. The actual subsidy is capped at the benchmark plan premium.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your annual household income - use your Modified Adjusted Gross Income (MAGI).
  2. Enter household size - everyone claimed on your tax return including yourself.
  3. Select a plan tier - note the subsidy benchmark is always the Silver plan regardless of which tier you choose.
  4. Click Calculate to see your estimated subsidy and net monthly premium.

Example Scenario

A single adult with an annual income of $35,000 (approximately 250% of the 2026 FPL for a 1-person household) is looking at a Silver plan with a $490/month benchmark premium. Their expected contribution is about 6% of income, or $175/month. The estimated subsidy is $315/month ($490 - $175), reducing their monthly premium to $175 and saving $3,780 per year compared to paying full price.
