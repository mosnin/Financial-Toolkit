Income Replacement Formula

The calculator uses two approaches and shows both: one based on your income with your chosen replacement rate, and one based on your actual pre-retirement spending.

Target Retirement Income
Target Income = Current Income x (Replacement Rate / 100)
Portfolio Required (4% Rule)
Portfolio Income Needed = Target Income - Social Security Required Savings = Portfolio Income Needed / 0.04
Monthly Savings to Reach Goal
Using FV formula: PMT = Required Savings x (r/12) / ((1 + r/12)^(n*12) - 1) where r = annual return and n = years until retirement

How to Use This Income Replacement Calculator

  1. Enter your current annual income - your gross pre-tax salary or total household income.
  2. Enter your pre-retirement annual expenses - your actual spending, not your income.
  3. Set your replacement rate - 70-80% is typical; adjust up for active retirements.
  4. Enter expected Social Security - check ssa.gov for your personalized benefit estimate.
  5. Enter years until retirement and your expected investment return.
  6. Click "Calculate Income Needs" to see your savings target and required monthly contributions.

Example Scenario

A couple earning $120,000 per year with $85,000 in annual expenses, targeting 80% income replacement, and expecting $28,000/year from Social Security needs their portfolio to produce $68,000/year ($96,000 - $28,000). Applying the 4% rule, they need $1.7 million saved. With 25 years until retirement and a 7% return, they need to save approximately $2,400/month to reach this goal.

Frequently Asked Questions

Using actual spending is often more accurate than income, since spending tells you what you truly need. Income includes savings contributions and taxes that disappear in retirement. High savers may find their spending is much lower than their income.
