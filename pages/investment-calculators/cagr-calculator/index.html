CAGR Formula

CAGR is calculated by dividing the end value by the start value, raising that ratio to the power of 1 divided by the number of years, then subtracting 1:

CAGR Formula
CAGR = (End Value / Start Value)^(1 / n) − 1

Where n is the number of years. The total growth percentage is simply:

Total Growth Formula
Total Growth = (End Value − Start Value) / Start Value × 100

How to Use This CAGR Calculator

  1. Enter the start value — the initial investment or beginning portfolio value.
  2. Enter the end value — the final value at the end of the investment period.
  3. Enter the number of years — the total duration of the investment (decimals accepted).
  4. Click "Calculate CAGR" to see the compound annual growth rate, total growth, and total gain.

Example Scenario

Suppose you invested $5,000 in a mutual fund and it grew to $12,000 over 7 years. The total growth is 140%, but the CAGR is approximately 13.35% per year. This represents the steady annual rate at which your investment would need to grow each year, with compounding, to reach the same end value. The total dollar gain is $7,000.
