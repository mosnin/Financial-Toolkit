Are You Ready for a Promotion?

A promotion isn't just about working hard — it's about demonstrating the right skills, being visible to the right people, and making the right ask at the right time. This quiz evaluates your performance history, skills gap, organizational visibility, and readiness to negotiate. Answer 8 questions to find out where you stand.

Frequently Asked Questions

Most companies expect 12–18 months of demonstrated performance at your current level before promoting. However, if you've taken on responsibilities clearly above your pay grade and received consistently strong reviews, you can make a compelling case earlier. Promotion timing also depends on whether a position exists to be promoted into.

Promotions typically come with 10–20% salary increases at most companies, though 15% is a common target. However, if the new title significantly changes your market value, you should negotiate based on external benchmarks — not just what the company offers. Use the raise percentage calculator to quantify your ask.

First, ask explicitly what the criteria are for promotion and what gaps you need to close. If you meet the criteria consistently and still face delay, that's a sign the opportunity may not exist internally. At that point, external offers become both a reality check and a negotiating tool — companies often promote when faced with a competing offer.
