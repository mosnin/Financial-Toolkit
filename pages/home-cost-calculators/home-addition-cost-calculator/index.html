Home Addition Cost Formula

Home addition costs are estimated using cost-per-square-foot ranges based on addition type and quality:

Cost Formula
Estimated Cost = Addition Sq Ft x Cost per Sq Ft (based on type and quality)

Costs per square foot vary significantly: garages $50-$120, sunrooms $80-$200, bedrooms $100-$300, family rooms $120-$350, and bathrooms $150-$450. Quality level shifts these ranges up or down.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the addition square footage - total size of the new space.
  2. Select the addition type - bedroom, bathroom, garage, sunroom, or family room.
  3. Choose quality level - basic, mid-range, or high-end finishes.
  4. Click "Calculate Cost" to see estimated total, cost per square foot, and cost range.

Example Scenario

A 400-square-foot bedroom addition at mid-range quality would cost approximately $80,000 ($200 per square foot). The estimate ranges from $68,000 to $92,000 depending on specific materials, site conditions, and contractor pricing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Home addition costs range from $80-$200/sqft for standard quality, $150-$350 for mid-range, and $300-$600+ for high-end. Bathroom additions cost more per square foot due to plumbing.

Garage additions return 60-65%, bathroom additions 50-60%, and bedroom additions 40-55%. Two-story additions maximize value by sharing foundation and roof costs.

A simple room addition takes 2-4 months, a bathroom addition 3-5 months, and large additions 4-8+ months. Permitting adds 2-8 weeks before construction begins.
