Moving Abroad Cost Calculator

Moving abroad can dramatically change your financial situation — for better or worse depending on destination and income. This calculator estimates your total upfront relocation costs, compares your projected monthly expenses abroad versus at home, and calculates how many months until your lower cost of living (if applicable) breaks even with your move-in costs.

Use this tool to evaluate whether your target destination makes financial sense and how much runway you need before you go.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Select your cost tier — low, medium, or high based on your destination country's relative cost of living.
  2. Enter your current monthly expenses — your total US monthly spending including rent, food, transport, and lifestyle.
  3. Enter your estimated monthly budget abroad — research specific costs at your destination using resources like Numbeo.com.
  4. Enter all one-time upfront costs — shipping, visa fees, deposits, flights, and healthcare enrollment.
  5. Set your emergency fund target — recommended at 6× your monthly abroad budget.

Example Scenario

Sarah is a remote worker earning $75,000/year who wants to move to Lisbon, Portugal. Her current US monthly expenses are $4,500. She estimates she can live comfortably in Lisbon for $2,800/month. Upfront costs: shipping $4,000, visa $1,200, housing deposit $2,800, flights $1,800, healthcare setup $1,000 — total upfront $10,800. Monthly savings abroad: $1,700. Break-even: 6.4 months. After that, she saves $20,400/year. Her emergency fund target is $16,800 (6 months of abroad expenses).
