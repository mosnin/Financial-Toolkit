How Closing Costs Are Calculated

Closing costs fall into three main buckets:

  • Lender fees — Origination, underwriting, and discount points charged by your mortgage lender (roughly 0.5–1% of the loan)
  • Third-party fees — Appraisal, title search, title insurance, attorney fees, and home inspection charges (~1–2% of purchase price)
  • Prepaid items — Upfront deposits into escrow for property taxes and homeowners insurance, plus prepaid mortgage interest for the partial month of closing

Transfer taxes vary widely by state. Some states charge nothing; others (like New York) can add 1–2%+ of the sale price.

Example Scenario

Sarah is buying a $400,000 home with a $320,000 conventional loan. Her estimated closing costs break down like this:

  • Origination fee (0.5%): $1,600
  • Underwriting fee: $850
  • Appraisal: $550
  • Title insurance: $1,200
  • Recording fees: $250
  • Transfer taxes (0.5%): $2,000
  • Prepaid taxes (2 months): $833
  • Prepaid insurance: $1,500
  • Prepaid interest (15 days): $427

Total: ~$9,210 (2.3% of purchase price)
