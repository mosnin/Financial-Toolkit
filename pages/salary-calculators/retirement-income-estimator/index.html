Retirement Savings Formula

Your retirement nest egg grows through compound interest on both your initial savings and monthly contributions:

Future Value Formula
FV = PV(1+r)^n + PMT[((1+r)^n - 1) / r]

Where PV is current savings, r is monthly return rate, n is total months, and PMT is monthly contribution. Monthly retirement income uses the 4% rule: Nest Egg x 4% / 12.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter current savings - your total retirement savings to date.
  2. Enter monthly contribution - how much you save each month for retirement.
  3. Enter years until retirement - how many years until you plan to retire.
  4. Enter expected annual return - your projected investment return (6-8% is typical).
  5. Enter withdrawal rate - typically 4% for a 30-year retirement.
  6. Click "Estimate Retirement" to see your projected nest egg and income.

Example Scenario

With $50,000 in current savings, $500/month contributions, 25 years to retirement, and a 7% return, your nest egg would grow to approximately $556,000. Using the 4% rule, this provides about $22,240 annually or $1,853 monthly in retirement income.
