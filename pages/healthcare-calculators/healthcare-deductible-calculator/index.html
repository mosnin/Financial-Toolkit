How the Deductible Comparison Works

This calculator computes total annual cost by adding annual premiums plus the portion of expected medical costs you pay out-of-pocket (limited by your deductible), then subtracting your HSA contribution as a tax-advantaged offset:

Annual Premium Cost
Annual Premiums = Monthly Premium × 12
Out-of-Pocket Medical Exposure
Medical Exposure = min(Expected Annual Medical Costs, Annual Deductible)
Net Total Cost
Total = Annual Premiums + Medical Exposure - HSA Contribution

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your plan's annual deductible - the amount you pay before insurance covers costs.
  2. Enter your monthly premium - the amount you pay each month regardless of usage.
  3. Enter expected annual medical costs - your realistic estimate based on past usage or planned procedures.
  4. Enter your HSA contribution - if on an HDHP, the amount you plan to put in your HSA (tax savings reduce effective cost).
  5. Click Calculate to see your total annual cost breakdown.

Example Scenario

A healthy individual is comparing two plans. Plan A (HDHP): $180/month premium, $2,500 deductible, and they'll contribute $2,000 to an HSA. Plan B (PPO): $420/month premium, $500 deductible. They expect about $1,200 in medical costs this year. Plan A total: $2,160 premiums + $1,200 exposure - $2,000 HSA = $1,360. Plan B total: $5,040 premiums + $500 exposure = $5,540. The HDHP saves over $4,000 per year — for a healthy person who rarely hits their deductible.
