Affiliate Income Formulas

Monthly Clicks
Monthly Clicks = Monthly Traffic x (CTR / 100)
Monthly Conversions

Monthly Affiliate Income

Income per Visitor (EPV)
EPV = Monthly Income / Monthly Traffic

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your monthly visitors — the total traffic reaching your site or content each month.
  2. Set your click-through rate — what percentage of visitors click an affiliate link. Check your analytics for this figure, or use 2-5% as a starting estimate.
  3. Enter your conversion rate — the percentage of clicks that become purchases. Most programs average 1-3%.
  4. Input your average commission — the dollar amount earned per conversion across all programs.
  5. Enter the number of programs you actively promote to scale the output accordingly.
  6. Click Calculate Income to see projected monthly income, clicks, conversions, annual total, and earnings per visitor.

Example Scenario

A personal finance blogger receives 25,000 visitors per month and promotes 4 affiliate programs including a credit card, budgeting software, and two investment platforms.

  • Monthly Clicks: 25,000 x 4% CTR = 1,000 clicks
  • Monthly Conversions: 1,000 x 2.5% = 25 conversions
  • Monthly Income: 25 x $45 avg commission x 4 programs = $4,500/month
  • Annual Income: $4,500 x 12 = $54,000/year
  • Income per Visitor: $4,500 / 25,000 = $0.18 per visitor

Frequently Asked Questions

A good affiliate CTR depends heavily on your niche and content type. In-content links typically see 1-5% CTR, while dedicated review pages or comparison articles can achieve 10-20%. Banner ads tend to average 0.1-0.5%.

Average affiliate conversion rates range from 0.5% to 2% for most programs. High-intent audiences reviewing specific products can see 3-7% or higher. The conversion rate depends on the merchant's landing page, product price, and audience fit.

Focus on improving your click-through rate with better placement and calls-to-action, choose higher-commission programs, target higher-intent keywords, optimize merchant selection for conversion, and promote higher-priced products with recurring commissions.

Promoting multiple programs allows you to match the best products to your content, reduce dependency on any one merchant, and test which offers convert best with your audience. However, too many programs can dilute focus — 3 to 8 well-chosen programs is usually optimal.
