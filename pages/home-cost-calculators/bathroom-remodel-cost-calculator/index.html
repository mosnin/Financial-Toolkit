Bathroom Remodel Cost Formula

Bathroom remodel costs are calculated based on the size, scope of work, and fixtures quality:

Cost Formula
Estimated Cost = Bathroom Sq Ft x Base Cost per Sq Ft x Fixtures Multiplier

Base costs range from $75-$150/sqft for basic, $150-$350/sqft for mid-range, and $350-$600/sqft for luxury scope. The fixtures multiplier adjusts costs based on your chosen quality level.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the bathroom size - measure the total square footage of your bathroom.
  2. Select the remodel scope - basic for cosmetic updates, mid-range for substantial upgrades, or luxury for a premium transformation.
  3. Choose fixtures quality - standard, upgraded, or premium fixtures.
  4. Click "Calculate Cost" to see your estimated renovation cost.

Example Scenario

An 80-square-foot bathroom undergoing a mid-range remodel with upgraded fixtures would cost approximately $24,000. This includes new tile, a modern vanity, updated lighting, and designer-quality faucets and showerhead.

Frequently Asked Questions

A basic bathroom remodel costs $5,000-$15,000, a mid-range remodel runs $15,000-$40,000, and a luxury renovation can cost $40,000-$80,000+. The final price depends on bathroom size, fixtures quality, and labor rates in your area.

Labor and tile work are typically the most expensive components, accounting for 40-60% of total costs. Plumbing changes, custom showers, and high-end fixtures can also drive costs up significantly.

A simple bathroom refresh takes 1-2 weeks, a mid-range remodel takes 3-6 weeks, and a full gut renovation can take 6-10 weeks or more. Having only one bathroom may require temporary accommodations.
