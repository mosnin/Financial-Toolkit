Salary After Tax Formula

Your take-home pay is calculated by subtracting all applicable taxes from your gross annual salary:

After-Tax Salary Formula
Take-Home Pay = Gross Salary - Federal Tax - State Tax - FICA

Where Federal Tax uses progressive brackets based on filing status, State Tax is a flat percentage of gross income, and FICA is 7.65% (Social Security 6.2% + Medicare 1.45%).

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your annual salary - your total gross yearly compensation before taxes.
  2. Select your filing status - single, married filing jointly, or head of household.
  3. Enter your state tax rate - use 0% for states with no income tax.
  4. Click "Calculate Take-Home Pay" to see your after-tax income broken down by period.

Example Scenario

If you earn a $75,000 annual salary, file as single, and live in a state with a 5% income tax, your estimated annual take-home pay would be approximately $56,588. That breaks down to roughly $4,716 per month, $2,177 biweekly, or $1,088 per week.
