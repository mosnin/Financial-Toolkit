Investment Return Formula

The future value of an investment with annual contributions is calculated using compound growth:

Future Value with Contributions
FV = PV x (1 + r)^t + C x [((1 + r)^t - 1) / r]

Where FV is the future value, PV is the initial investment, r is the annual return rate (decimal), t is the number of years, and C is the annual contribution.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your initial investment - the amount you are starting with.
  2. Enter the expected annual return - the yearly percentage return you expect.
  3. Enter the investment period - how many years you plan to invest.
  4. Enter annual contributions - additional money you plan to invest each year (optional).
  5. Click "Calculate Returns" to see your projected portfolio value.

Example Scenario

If you invest $10,000 with an 8% annual return over 20 years and add $5,000 each year, your portfolio would grow to approximately $275,172. Of that, $110,000 is your total invested amount and $165,172 is returns earned through compound growth.
