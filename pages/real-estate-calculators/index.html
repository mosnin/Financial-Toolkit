Frequently Asked Questions

A common guideline is that your total monthly housing costs should not exceed 28% of your gross monthly income. Use our Affordability Calculator to get a personalized estimate that factors in your debts, down payment, interest rate, property taxes, and insurance.

It depends on your local market, how long you plan to stay, mortgage rates, and your financial situation. Our Rent vs Buy Calculator compares the total cost of ownership against renting over your planned time horizon so you can see which option wins.

Cap rates typically range from 4% to 10% depending on location, property type, and risk. Higher cap rates indicate higher potential returns but often come with more risk. Use our Cap Rate Calculator to evaluate specific properties and compare them side by side.

Refinancing usually makes sense when you can lower your rate by at least 0.5% to 1%, plan to stay in the home long enough to recoup closing costs, or want to switch from an adjustable-rate to a fixed-rate loan. Our Refinance Calculator shows your break-even point and lifetime savings.

Closing costs typically range from 2% to 5% of the home purchase price. They include lender fees, title insurance, appraisal, escrow, and prepaid items like property taxes and homeowners insurance. Use our Closing Cost Calculator for a detailed estimate based on your purchase price and location.
