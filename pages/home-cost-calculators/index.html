Frequently Asked Questions

Kitchen remodels range from $15,000-$30,000 for a minor remodel, $30,000-$75,000 for a mid-range remodel, and $75,000-$150,000+ for a major upscale remodel. The biggest cost drivers are cabinets, countertops, and appliances.

A common guideline is to budget 1-2% of your home's value annually for maintenance. A $300,000 home should budget $3,000-$6,000 per year. Older homes typically need closer to 2-3% due to more frequent repairs.

Garage door replacement, manufactured stone veneer, and minor kitchen remodels typically offer the highest ROI (70-100%+). Bathroom remodels, deck additions, and energy-efficient window replacements also provide strong returns.

Solar panels typically pay for themselves in 6-10 years through electricity savings, after which they generate free power for 15-20+ more years. Federal tax credits (currently 30%) significantly reduce upfront costs. ROI depends on your location, electricity rates, and sun exposure.

Asphalt shingle roofs last 20-30 years, metal roofs 40-70 years, tile roofs 50+ years, and slate roofs 75-100+ years. Climate, ventilation, and maintenance affect lifespan. Signs you need replacement include curling shingles, granule loss, and leaks.
