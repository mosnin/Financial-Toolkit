How the Estimate Works

This calculator estimates potential credit score improvement based on the key FICO scoring factors:

FICO Score Factor Weights
Payment History: 35% | Utilization: 30% | Account Age: 15% | Credit Mix: 10% | New Credit: 10%

The projected improvement is based on optimizing your utilization to below 10% and maintaining 100% on-time payments over the next 6-12 months. Actual results may vary based on your complete credit profile.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your current credit score - check your free score through your bank or credit monitoring service.
  2. Enter your credit utilization - the percentage of your total credit limit currently in use.
  3. Enter your on-time payment percentage - the percentage of all payments you have made on time.
  4. Enter your average account age - how long your credit accounts have been open on average.
  5. Click "Calculate Improvement" to see your projected improvement and actionable tips.

Example Scenario

James has a credit score of 620, with 55% utilization, 88% on-time payments, and an average account age of 2 years. By paying down his balances to under 10% utilization and making all payments on time for the next 6-12 months, he could potentially see a 60-80 point improvement, bringing his score into the 680-700 range.
