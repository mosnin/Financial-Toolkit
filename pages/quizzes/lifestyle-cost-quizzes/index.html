Frequently Asked Questions

A common guideline is the 20/4/10 rule: put at least 20% down, finance for no more than four years, and keep total vehicle expenses (payment, insurance, fuel, maintenance) under 10% to 15% of your gross monthly income. Our car affordability quiz helps you evaluate your specific situation.

Sustainable spending means you can maintain your lifestyle indefinitely without going into debt, while still saving for emergencies, retirement, and future goals. If you are regularly dipping into savings or relying on credit to cover monthly expenses, your spending is not sustainable.

The full cost of relocating includes moving services ($1,000 to $10,000+ depending on distance), temporary housing, security deposits, utility connection fees, and the cost-of-living difference between cities. Our quiz helps you assess whether your finances are strong enough to absorb a move.

The traditional guideline is no more than 28% to 30% of your gross monthly income on housing costs. In high-cost cities, many people spend 35% to 40%. Going above this level puts strain on savings and other financial goals. Our quiz evaluates your housing affordability in full context.
