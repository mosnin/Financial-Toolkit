How to Use This Calculator

  1. Select your filing status.
  2. Enter your AGI for marginal rate estimation and medical expense threshold.
  3. Enter property taxes and state/local taxes (combined SALT cap: $10,000).
  4. Enter mortgage interest from your Form 1098.
  5. Enter charitable contributions and total medical expenses.
  6. Click Compare Deductions for your recommendation.

Frequently Asked Questions

You should itemize if your total allowable itemized deductions exceed your standard deduction. For 2026, the standard deduction is $14,600 (single), $29,200 (married), and $21,900 (head of household). Most taxpayers benefit from the standard deduction.

Common itemized deductions include SALT (capped at $10,000), mortgage interest, charitable contributions, medical expenses exceeding 7.5% of AGI, and casualty/theft losses in federally declared disaster areas.
