Savings Goal Formula

When a return rate is applied, the number of months to reach a savings goal is calculated using the future value of an annuity formula solved for time:

Months to Goal (with interest)
n = ln((FV * r + PMT) / (PV * r + PMT)) / ln(1 + r)

Where FV is the savings goal, PV is current savings, PMT is the monthly contribution, and r is the monthly interest rate. Without interest, it simplifies to (Goal - Current) / Monthly Contribution.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your savings goal - the total amount you want to save.
  2. Enter your current savings - what you have saved already.
  3. Enter your monthly contribution - how much you can save each month.
  4. Enter the annual return rate - expected interest or investment return (use 0 for no returns).
  5. Click "Calculate" to see your timeline and breakdown.

Example Scenario

Suppose you want to save $50,000 for a down payment. You currently have $5,000 saved and can contribute $500 per month into an account earning 4% annually. This calculator would show you need approximately 78 months (about 6.5 years) to reach your goal, with about $44,000 in total contributions and $6,000 in interest earned.

Frequently Asked Questions

The time depends on the gap between your goal and current savings, your monthly contribution, and the return rate. With interest, a logarithmic formula determines the exact number of months needed. Without interest, simply divide the remaining amount by your monthly contribution.

If your savings are in a high-yield savings account, CD, or investment account, including the expected return rate gives a more accurate timeline. For a regular checking account with no interest, use 0% as the return rate.

Financial experts often recommend saving 15-20% of your income. However, any consistent amount helps. The key is choosing an amount you can sustain without compromising essential expenses.

Increase your monthly contributions, reduce unnecessary expenses, earn a higher return by moving savings to a high-yield account, or supplement income with side work. Even small increases in contributions can shave months off your timeline.
