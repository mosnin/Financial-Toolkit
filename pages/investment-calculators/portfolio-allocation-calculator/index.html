Portfolio Allocation Formula

The dollar amount for each asset class is calculated by multiplying the total portfolio value by the target percentage:

Allocation Formula
Dollar Amount = Total Portfolio Value x (Target Allocation % / 100)

How to Use This Portfolio Allocation Calculator

  1. Enter your total portfolio value - the combined value of all your investments.
  2. Enter target allocation percentages for stocks, bonds, real estate, and cash.
  3. Ensure allocations total 100% for accurate results.
  4. Click "Calculate Allocation" to see the dollar amount for each asset class.

Example Scenario

With a $500,000 portfolio and a target allocation of 60% stocks, 25% bonds, 10% real estate, and 5% cash, you should have $300,000 in stocks, $125,000 in bonds, $50,000 in real estate, and $25,000 in cash.

Frequently Asked Questions

Portfolio allocation is the process of dividing your investment portfolio among different asset classes such as stocks, bonds, cash, and real estate to balance risk and reward according to your goals.

Your ideal allocation depends on your age, risk tolerance, time horizon, and financial goals. A common rule of thumb is to subtract your age from 110 to get your stock allocation percentage.

Most financial advisors recommend rebalancing annually or when any asset class drifts more than 5% from its target allocation. Over-frequent rebalancing can increase transaction costs.

Your target allocations should total 100%. If they do not, the calculator will alert you so you can adjust your percentages accordingly before calculating dollar amounts.

Real estate can provide diversification, income, and inflation protection. Many advisors recommend a 5-20% allocation to real estate through REITs or direct investment, depending on your overall strategy.
