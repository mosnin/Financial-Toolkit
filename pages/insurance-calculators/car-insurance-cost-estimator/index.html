How Car Insurance Premiums Are Calculated

Car insurance premiums are estimated using a base rate applied to the vehicle value, then adjusted by multipliers for driver age, driving record, and coverage level:

Premium Estimation Formula
Annual Premium = Vehicle Value x Base Rate x Age Factor x Record Factor x Coverage Factor

The base rate is typically around 3-5% of the vehicle value. The age factor adjusts for the statistical risk of different age groups. The record factor increases premiums for poor driving history. The coverage factor reflects the extent of protection chosen.
