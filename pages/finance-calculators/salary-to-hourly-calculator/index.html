Salary to Hourly Formula

Formulas
Hourly Rate = Annual Salary / (Weeks per Year x Hours per Week)

Daily Rate = Hourly Rate x (Hours per Week / 5)
Weekly Rate = Annual Salary / Weeks per Year
Monthly Rate = Annual Salary / 12

The standard calculation assumes a 5-day work week. The daily rate reflects a typical workday based on your weekly hours divided by 5 working days.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your annual salary: Your gross (pre-tax) annual salary.
  2. Set hours per week: The default is 40 hours (standard full-time). Change this if you work different hours.
  3. Set weeks per year: The default is 52 weeks. Reduce this if you have unpaid time off (e.g., 50 weeks if you take 2 weeks unpaid vacation).
  4. Click "Calculate": View your hourly, daily, weekly, and monthly rates.

Example Scenario

Pat earns $75,000 per year and works 40 hours per week, 52 weeks per year:

  • Hourly rate: $75,000 / (52 x 40) = $36.06/hour
  • Daily rate: $36.06 x 8 = $288.46/day
  • Weekly rate: $75,000 / 52 = $1,442.31/week
  • Monthly rate: $75,000 / 12 = $6,250.00/month

If Pat actually works 45 hours per week, the effective hourly rate drops to $32.05/hour, which shows the true cost of unpaid overtime.
