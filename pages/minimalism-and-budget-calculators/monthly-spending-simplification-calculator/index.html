How the Simplification Formula Works

Formulas
Gap per Category = Current Spending − Simplified Target
Total Monthly Reduction = Sum of all positive gaps
Annual Savings = Total Monthly Reduction × 12
10-Year Wealth = Monthly Reduction × [((1 + r/12)^120 − 1) / (r/12)]
Simplification Score = Total Reduction / Total Current Spending × 100

The simplification score measures what percentage of your current spending is redirected by adopting the simplified budget. A score of 15–25% is typical for a meaningful simplification. The 10-year wealth impact assumes the monthly savings are invested consistently at the specified rate.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter current spending: Use your last 2–3 months of bank statements to get accurate averages for each category.
  2. Set simplified targets: For each category, enter what you believe is a lean but realistic monthly amount that covers your genuine needs.
  3. Review category gaps: The calculator shows the gap for each category in real time after you calculate.
  4. Set your investment return: Use 7% for a balanced portfolio estimate.

Example Scenario

Alex reviews their spending and sets simplified targets:

  • Entertainment: $280 → $100 (gap: $180) — free events and one streaming service

Frequently Asked Questions

A simplified budget is a lean spending plan that covers all essential needs and a few intentional wants, while eliminating excess and waste. Rather than tracking every dollar, a simplified budget focuses on meaningful categories and sets a realistic target for each that you can maintain without stress.

For each category, ask: What is the minimum I need to spend here to meet my actual needs and maintain my quality of life? Research average spending for your city and income level as a benchmark, then set targets slightly below your current spending and gradually reduce as you build the habit.

A simplified budget typically includes housing, food, transportation, utilities, healthcare, entertainment, dining out, subscriptions, personal care, and a miscellaneous buffer. This calculator uses 10 standard categories that cover the vast majority of household spending.

Seeing the 10-year impact of monthly reductions motivates sustainable behavior change. A $400/month reduction seems modest, but invested at 7% for 10 years grows to over $66,000. Visualizing the long-term outcome of small daily choices is one of the most powerful tools in personal finance.
