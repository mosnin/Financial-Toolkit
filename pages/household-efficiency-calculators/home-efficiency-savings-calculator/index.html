Home Efficiency Score Formula

Efficiency Score Calculation
Score starts at 40 base points (for having a home) Each optimization completed adds points: LED Lighting: +12 points Smart Thermostat: +15 points Adequate Insulation: +18 points Low-Flow Fixtures: +8 points Insurance Shopped: +12 points Preventive Maintenance: +15 points Max Score: 100 points Potential savings = uncompleted optimizations × estimated savings value

The efficiency score is a simplified composite index. A score of 70+ indicates a well-optimized home. Scores below 60 suggest significant savings opportunities remain.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your annual operating costs -- utilities, maintenance, insurance, and HOA.
  2. Check off optimizations you have already completed -- each one improves your efficiency score.
  3. Click "Calculate Efficiency Score" to see your score, potential savings, and priority improvements.
  4. Review the priority list -- tackle the highest-impact improvements first.

Frequently Asked Questions

For a median US home, annual operating costs (excluding mortgage principal) typically run $7,000-15,000 per year including utilities, insurance, maintenance, and taxes. The exact amount depends heavily on home size, age, and location.

An efficiency score measures how many cost-reduction opportunities you have implemented. A score of 70+ indicates a well-optimized home. Each improvement you make raises your score and lowers your ongoing operating costs.

Homeowners who shop insurance annually typically save 10-25% by switching providers. Bundling home and auto can save an additional 5-15%. Given average costs of $1,500-2,000/year, this represents $150-500 in annual savings.

Best return order: (1) Smart thermostat -- payback under 2 years; (2) LED lighting -- payback 6-18 months; (3) Weatherstripping/air sealing -- payback 1-3 years; (4) Attic insulation -- payback 3-7 years; (5) New HVAC -- payback 5-10 years.
