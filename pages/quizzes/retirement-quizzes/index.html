Frequently Asked Questions

A common guideline is: 1x your salary saved by age 30, 3x by 40, 6x by 50, 8x by 60, and 10x by retirement. These benchmarks assume you want to maintain your current lifestyle in retirement. Take our retirement savings quiz for a personalised target based on your income, expenses, and goals.

The 4% rule suggests you can withdraw 4% of your retirement portfolio in year one, then adjust for inflation each year, and your savings will last at least 30 years. To use this rule, multiply your desired annual retirement spending by 25 to find your target nest egg. For example, $50,000 per year requires $1.25 million saved.

Whether $500,000 is enough for early retirement depends on your annual spending, expected investment returns, years until traditional retirement age, and healthcare costs. Using the 4% rule, $500,000 supports $20,000 per year in spending. Take the early retirement quiz to see your specific picture.

You are likely saving enough if your contributions plus employer matches equal at least 15% of your gross income, you are investing in tax-advantaged accounts (401k, IRA), your savings are growing faster than inflation, and your projected nest egg at retirement covers 25 times your expected annual spending. Our quiz evaluates all these factors.
