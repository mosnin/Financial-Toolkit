Coverage Needs Formula

Each coverage type is calculated based on different aspects of your financial profile:

Life Insurance
10x Annual Income + Total Debts + ($40K x Dependents)
Property Insurance
100% of Property/Asset Value (replacement cost)
Disability Insurance
65% of Monthly Gross Income
Liability Coverage
Net Worth (Assets - Debts), minimum $1,000,000

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter total assets - home equity, vehicles, savings, investments, etc.
  2. Enter annual income - your household's total gross annual income.
  3. Enter family size - number of people in your household.
  4. Enter total debts - mortgage, car loans, student loans, credit cards.
  5. Click "Assess Needs" to see recommended coverage by type.

Example Scenario

A family of 4 with $500,000 in assets, $100,000 annual income, and $250,000 in debts would need approximately $1,330,000 in life insurance, $500,000 in property coverage, $5,417/month in disability benefits, and $1,000,000 in liability coverage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Most people need health, auto, and home/renters insurance. If you have dependents, add life insurance. Disability insurance protects income, and umbrella insurance adds extra liability protection.

Insure assets at replacement cost, carry life insurance of 10-15x income if you have dependents, and maintain liability coverage at least equal to your net worth.

Many people are under-insured in life and disability coverage while over-insured in extended warranties. Compare your current coverage against these recommendations to identify gaps.

Review annually and after major life events like marriage, having children, buying a home, or significant income changes.
