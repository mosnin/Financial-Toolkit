Compound Growth Formula

The compound growth formula calculates future value based on initial principal, growth rate, and compounding frequency:

Compound Growth (Lump Sum)
FV = P × (1 + r/n)n×t
With Monthly Additions
FV = P × (1 + r/n)n×t + PMT × [((1 + r/n)n×t − 1) / (r/n)]
Effective Annual Rate (EAR)
EAR = (1 + r/n)n − 1

Where P is the initial amount, r is the annual growth rate (decimal), n is compounding periods per year, t is years, and PMT is the monthly addition.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your initial amount — the starting investment or lump sum.
  2. Enter the annual growth rate — expected annual return percentage.
  3. Choose compounding frequency — how often growth is compounded.
  4. Enter the number of years — your investment time horizon.
  5. Optionally enter monthly additions — regular contributions added each month.
  6. Click "Calculate Growth" to see the final value, growth multiple, and full year-by-year table.

Example Scenario

Investing $10,000 at a 7% annual growth rate compounded monthly for 20 years grows to approximately $40,387 — a 4.04x multiple and $30,387 in total growth. Adding $200 per month increases the final value to roughly $106,753, a growth multiple of 5.1x on total contributions, demonstrating how even modest regular additions dramatically amplify the compounding effect.
