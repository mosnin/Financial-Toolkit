Insurance Savings Formula

Discounts are applied sequentially to your current premium:

New Premium Estimate
New Premium = Current Premium x (1 - Deductible %) x (1 - Bundling %) x (1 - Safe Driver %)

Each discount is applied to the remaining premium after previous discounts, which reflects how most insurers calculate stacked discounts.

How to Use This Car Insurance Savings Calculator

  1. Enter your current annual premium - the total you pay per year for car insurance.
  2. Enter higher deductible savings - the estimated percentage savings from raising your deductible (typically 15-30%).
  3. Enter bundling discount - the percentage discount for bundling with home or other policies (typically 5-25%).
  4. Enter safe driver discount - the percentage discount for a clean driving record (typically 10-25%).
  5. Click "Calculate Savings" to see your estimated new premium and total savings.

Example Scenario

Suppose you currently pay $2,400/year for car insurance. By raising your deductible (15% savings), bundling with home insurance (10% savings), and qualifying for a safe driver discount (15% savings), your estimated new premium would be about $1,560 per year - a savings of $840 annually or $70 per month.

Frequently Asked Questions

Raising your deductible from $500 to $1,000 can save you 15-30% on your collision and comprehensive premiums. Going from $500 to $2,000 could save even more, but make sure you can afford the higher deductible if you need to file a claim.

Bundling auto and home insurance with the same company typically saves 5-25% on your premiums. Some insurers offer even larger discounts when you add more policies like umbrella or life insurance.

A safe driver discount rewards drivers with a clean driving record - no accidents, tickets, or claims for a certain period (usually 3-5 years). This discount typically saves 10-25% on your premium and is one of the most common auto insurance discounts.

Other savings strategies include maintaining good credit, taking a defensive driving course, installing anti-theft devices, driving fewer miles (low-mileage discount), paying annually instead of monthly, and shopping around for quotes every 1-2 years.

Experts recommend comparing insurance quotes at least once a year or whenever you experience a life change (moving, getting married, buying a new car). Insurance rates vary significantly between companies, and loyalty does not always mean the best price.
