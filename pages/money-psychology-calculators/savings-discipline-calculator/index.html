How the Discipline Score Is Calculated

Scoring Breakdown
Goal Achievement (40 pts): Actual ÷ Goal × 40
Consistency (20 pts): Months saved ÷ 3 × 20
Automation (20 pts): Automation level × 6.67
Emergency Fund (20 pts): Fund level × 6.67
Total Score = Sum of all four components (max 100)

The score weights goal achievement highest because hitting your target is the core outcome. Consistency, automation, and emergency fund status are behavioral and structural inputs that predict whether goal achievement is sustainable long-term or a one-off event.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your monthly income and goals: Use your actual take-home pay and a realistic monthly savings target (20% of income is the benchmark).
  2. Record actual savings: What did you actually transfer to savings accounts or investments this month?
  3. Rate your consistency and automation: Honest answers here reveal whether your savings are habitual or sporadic.
  4. Act on the suggestions: The calculator pinpoints your weakest component so you know exactly where one change will move your score the most.

Example Scenario

Alex earns $4,500/month and has a $600 savings goal. This month they saved $380. They saved 2 of the last 3 months, have partial automation, and 1–3 months emergency fund.

Score: Goal Achievement 25.3/40 + Consistency 13.3/20 + Automation 13.3/20 + Emergency 13.3/20 = 65/100. Gap: $220/month. Top recommendation: Automate the remaining $220 to a separate account on payday — this closes the gap and boosts score to approximately 85 without any extra effort.
