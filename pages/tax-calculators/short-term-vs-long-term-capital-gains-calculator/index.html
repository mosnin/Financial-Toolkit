How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the capital gain amount (sale price minus cost basis).
  2. Enter your annual ordinary income before the gain.
  3. Select your filing status.
  4. Click Compare Tax Rates to see side-by-side results.

Example Scenario

Single filer with $120,000 ordinary income and a $40,000 capital gain:

  • Short-Term Rate: In 22% bracket → Tax: ~$8,800
  • Long-Term Rate: Total income $160,000 → in 15% LTCG bracket → Tax: $6,000
  • Tax Savings from Holding >1 year: $2,800
