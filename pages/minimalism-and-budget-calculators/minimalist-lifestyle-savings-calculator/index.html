The Minimalist Savings Formula

Formulas
Monthly Savings = Current Spending − Minimalist Target
Annual Savings = Monthly Savings × 12
Future Value = Monthly Savings × [((1 + r/12)^(12×t) − 1) / (r/12)]
Lifestyle Multiplier = Future Value / (Monthly Savings × 12 × t)

The formula uses the future value of an annuity to calculate how monthly savings grow when invested at a compound rate. The lifestyle savings multiplier reveals how many times more wealth you accumulate compared to simply keeping the savings in cash — illustrating the true power of investing the minimalism dividend.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter current monthly spending: Include all regular monthly expenses — housing, food, transport, entertainment, subscriptions, and discretionary spending.
  2. Set a minimalist target: Research the actual minimums for your lifestyle. A minimalist budget focuses on needs and a few high-value wants.
  3. Choose a return rate: Use 7% for a conservative diversified portfolio estimate, or adjust based on your investment strategy.
  4. Set your time horizon: The longer the period, the more dramatic the compound growth effect becomes.

Example Scenario

Morgan currently spends $4,200/month. After adopting a minimalist lifestyle, the target is $2,700/month — a difference of $1,500/month.

  • Annual savings: $18,000
  • Invested at 7% for 10 years: approximately $310,000
  • Invested at 7% for 20 years: approximately $981,000
  • Lifestyle savings multiplier (20 years): ~2.7x the simple savings amount

By spending $1,500 less each month, Morgan could have nearly $1 million more after 20 years — the compounding effect transforms modest monthly discipline into transformational wealth.
