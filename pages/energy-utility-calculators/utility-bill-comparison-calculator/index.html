Utility Comparison Formula

Savings Calculations
Current Monthly Bill = Monthly Usage × Current Rate New Monthly Bill = Monthly Usage × Alternative Rate Monthly Savings = Current Bill − New Bill Annual Savings = Monthly Savings × 12 5-Year Savings = Annual Savings × 5 Switching ROI = (Annual Savings / Current Annual Bill) × 100 Rate Difference = Current Rate − Alternative Rate

This calculation assumes consistent usage throughout the year. Actual savings depend on usage patterns and whether the alternative rate is fixed or variable.

Always check for contract terms and any switching fees before changing providers, as these can reduce net savings.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your current provider rate -- find this on your utility bill as the energy charge per kWh or per therm.
  2. Enter the alternative provider rate -- from a competing supplier's offer or quote.
  3. Enter your monthly usage -- in kWh (electricity) or therms (natural gas) from your recent bills.
  4. Click "Compare Utility Bills" to see your potential savings and ROI from switching.

Example Scenario

Switching from $0.18/kWh to $0.14/kWh at 900 kWh/Month

Current rate: $0.18/kWh | Alternative rate: $0.14/kWh | Monthly usage: 900 kWh

Current Monthly Bill = 900 × $0.18 = $162.00

New Monthly Bill = 900 × $0.14 = $126.00

Monthly Savings = $36.00

Annual Savings = $36 × 12 = $432/year

5-Year Savings = $432 × 5 = $2,160

Switching ROI = $432 / $1,944 = 22.2%

Frequently Asked Questions

In deregulated energy markets, residential customers can choose their electricity or gas supplier. States with deregulated electricity markets include Texas, Ohio, Pennsylvania, New York, and Illinois. Check your state's public utilities commission website to find out if your area is deregulated.

Compare the total rate per kWh or therm including all fees, not just the advertised rate. Check if rates are fixed or variable. Variable rates can be lower initially but may increase. Also evaluate contract length, cancellation fees, renewable energy options, and customer service reputation.

Switching fees depend on your current contract. Many month-to-month plans have no switching fee. Fixed-rate contracts may have early termination fees, typically $50-$150. Always read the fine print before switching and factor any switching costs into your savings calculation.

Switching electricity or gas suppliers typically takes 1-3 billing cycles. Your physical service and reliability do not change -- only the supplier billing changes. The distribution infrastructure remains owned by the local utility.
