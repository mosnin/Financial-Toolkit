Business Growth Rate Formulas

These formulas help you understand growth across different time horizons.

Total Growth Rate
Growth Rate = ((Revenue Period 2 - Revenue Period 1) / Revenue Period 1) x 100
Monthly Growth Rate (CMGR)
Monthly Growth Rate = ((Revenue P2 / Revenue P1)^(1 / Months) - 1) x 100
Annualized Growth Rate
Annualized Growth Rate = ((1 + Monthly Rate)^12 - 1) x 100
Projected Next Period Revenue
Next Period = Revenue P2 x (1 + Monthly Rate)^Months

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter Revenue Period 1 -- your starting revenue figure (e.g., revenue from the first quarter or month).
  2. Enter Revenue Period 2 -- your ending revenue figure for the comparison period.
  3. Enter the time between periods in months (e.g., 3 for quarterly, 12 for annual).
  4. Click "Calculate Growth Rate" to see your total, monthly, and annualized growth rates plus a revenue projection.

Example Scenario

A SaaS company had $100,000 in revenue in January and grew to $130,000 by July (6 months later).

  • Total Growth Rate: (($130,000 - $100,000) / $100,000) x 100 = 30%
  • Monthly Growth Rate (CMGR): (($130,000 / $100,000)^(1/6) - 1) x 100 = 4.47%
  • Annualized Growth Rate: ((1.0447)^12 - 1) x 100 = 68.1%
  • Projected Next Period (6 months): $130,000 x (1.0447)^6 = $169,000

This 4.47% monthly CMGR annualizes to a very strong 68% growth, indicating a high-growth business trajectory.

Frequently Asked Questions

Business growth rate measures the percentage increase in revenue between two periods. It is calculated as ((Revenue Period 2 - Revenue Period 1) / Revenue Period 1) x 100.

A healthy growth rate varies by industry and company stage. Startups often target 10-20% monthly growth, while established companies may aim for 10-25% annually. High-growth companies often exceed 50% annually.

Monthly growth rate measures growth per month. Annualized growth rate compounds the monthly rate over 12 months using the formula: ((1 + monthly rate)^12 - 1) x 100, giving the equivalent annual growth.

Once you know your monthly growth rate, you can project next period revenue by multiplying current revenue by (1 + monthly growth rate)^number of months. This assumes growth continues at the same pace.
