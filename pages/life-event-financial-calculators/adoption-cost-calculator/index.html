Methodology & Cost Ranges

Adoption costs are highly variable. This calculator sums your entered expenses to give a personalized total:

Total Adoption Cost
Total = Agency Fees + Legal Fees + Travel + Home Study + Medical & Other
Financing Needed
Financing Needed = Total Cost − Current Savings
Months to Save
Months = Financing Needed / Monthly Savings Rate

Typical cost ranges by type: Foster care $0–$5,000 (often state-subsidized). Domestic infant $25,000–$50,000. International $30,000–$55,000. Federal adoption tax credit of up to $15,950 (2024) can significantly reduce your net cost.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Select your adoption type — this will pre-fill typical cost ranges you can then customize.
  2. Enter your specific fees — get actual quotes from your adoption agency, attorney, and home study provider.
  3. Enter current savings — any money already designated for adoption.
  4. Enter your monthly savings rate — what you can consistently set aside each month.
  5. Review results — see total cost, financing gap, and how long until you can proceed.

Example Scenario

Maria and David are pursuing domestic infant adoption. Their agency quoted $18,000 in fees, legal fees are $5,500, home study costs $2,500, birth parent medical assistance is $4,000, and travel is estimated at $1,500 — a total of $31,500. They have $8,000 saved and can save $700/month. The financing needed is $23,500, which will take approximately 34 months (about 2 years 10 months) to accumulate at their savings rate. They should also look into the federal adoption tax credit to offset costs at filing time.

Frequently Asked Questions

International adoption ranges from $25,000–$55,000 or more depending on the country. These figures include agency, legal, travel, and home study fees.

A home study is a required assessment by a licensed social worker that evaluates your home, family, finances, and background to confirm you are suitable to adopt. Cost typically ranges from $1,000 to $3,500 depending on the agency and state. It includes home visits, interviews, background checks, and written reports.
