Is Your Salary Competitive?

Your salary may feel reasonable — but is it actually market rate? This quiz evaluates your experience, education, industry, location, and role to benchmark your pay against what employers are actually offering. Answer 7 quick questions to find out where you stand.

Frequently Asked Questions

A competitive salary falls at or above the 50th percentile for your specific role, industry, experience level, and geographic market. It also accounts for total compensation — including benefits, bonuses, equity, and retirement contributions — not just base pay.

At minimum once a year, and especially before any performance review, job offer negotiation, or promotion discussion. Markets shift quickly in fast-moving industries like tech, where pay bands can change 10–20% year over year.

Start by documenting your contributions and gathering market data from Glassdoor, LinkedIn Salary, and the Bureau of Labor Statistics. Then schedule a compensation review meeting. If an internal raise is unlikely, exploring external opportunities is often the fastest way to close a pay gap.
