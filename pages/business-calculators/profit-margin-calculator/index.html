Profit Margin Formulas

Understanding the formulas behind profit margin calculations helps you better analyze your business finances.

Gross Profit
Gross Profit = Revenue - Cost of Goods Sold
Profit Margin %
Profit Margin = (Gross Profit / Revenue) x 100
Markup %
Markup = (Gross Profit / COGS) x 100

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your revenue -- the total income from sales of your product or service.
  2. Enter your cost of goods sold (COGS) -- the direct costs attributable to the production of the goods sold, including materials and direct labor.
  3. Click "Calculate Profit Margin" to see your gross profit, profit margin percentage, and markup percentage.
  4. Analyze the results to understand your pricing efficiency and compare against industry benchmarks.

Example Scenario

Suppose your business generates $250,000 in revenue and your cost of goods sold is $150,000.

  • Gross Profit: $250,000 - $150,000 = $100,000
  • Profit Margin: ($100,000 / $250,000) x 100 = 40%
  • Markup: ($100,000 / $150,000) x 100 = 66.67%

This means for every dollar of revenue, you keep 40 cents as gross profit. Your markup over cost is about 66.67%.
