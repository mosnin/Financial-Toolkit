Savings Gap Calculation Formula

The calculator projects your savings forward and compares it to the required nest egg using the 4% rule:

Projected Savings at Retirement
FV = Current Savings x (1 + r)^n + Monthly Savings x ((1 + r)^n - 1) / (r/12) where r = monthly return and n = months to retirement
Required Nest Egg (4% Rule)
Required = Target Annual Income / 0.04
Monthly Savings to Close Gap
Gap = Required - Projected Monthly = Gap x (r/12) / ((1 + r/12)^n - 1)

How to Use This Savings Gap Calculator

  1. Enter your current retirement savings - total of all retirement accounts (401k, IRA, etc.).
  2. Enter your current age and target retirement age to set the accumulation period.
  3. Enter your current monthly savings - what you actually contribute each month.
  4. Enter your expected return - 6-7% is a commonly used conservative estimate.
  5. Enter your target annual income from your portfolio - subtract Social Security from your total income need first.
  6. Click "Calculate Savings Gap" to see your gap or surplus and a clear monthly savings target.

Example Scenario

A 45-year-old with $150,000 saved, contributing $800/month, targeting retirement at 65 with a 7% return will accumulate approximately $980,000. If the target annual portfolio income is $60,000, the required nest egg is $1.5 million (using 4% rule) — leaving a gap of about $520,000. To close this gap on the same 20-year timeline, they would need to increase monthly savings to approximately $1,800/month.
