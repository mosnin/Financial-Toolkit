Energy Efficiency Savings Formula

ROI Calculation
Annual Savings = Monthly Bill × Savings % × 12 Payback Period (years) = Upgrade Cost / Annual Savings 10-Year Net Savings = (Annual Savings × 10) - Upgrade Cost 10-Year ROI % = (10-Year Net Savings / Upgrade Cost) × 100 Savings % by upgrade type: LED Lighting: 10-15% of total bill Smart Thermostat: 10-12% of total bill Insulation: 15-20% of total bill New HVAC: 20-30% of total bill (adjusted by home size)

Savings percentages are based on EPA Energy Star and DOE research. Your actual savings may be higher or lower depending on your climate zone, current system age, and usage habits.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your current monthly energy bill -- use an average of your last 12 months if possible.
  2. Enter your home size -- used to refine savings estimates for insulation and HVAC upgrades.
  3. Select your upgrade type -- each type has a different typical savings percentage based on research data.
  4. Enter the upgrade cost -- include installation labor. Use the hint below the field for typical cost ranges.
  5. Click "Calculate ROI" to see your payback period and 10-year return.

Example Scenario

Smart Thermostat Installation -- $250 Cost

Current monthly bill: $200 | Home size: 2,000 sq ft | Upgrade: Smart Thermostat | Cost: $250

Estimated savings: 11% of $200 = $22/month

Annual savings: $22 × 12 = $264/year

Payback period: $250 / $264 = 0.95 years (~11.4 months)

10-Year net savings: ($264 × 10) - $250 = $2,390

10-Year ROI: $2,390 / $250 = 956%

Frequently Asked Questions

LED bulbs use 75% less energy than incandescent bulbs and last 25 times longer. For a typical home spending $150/month on electricity, switching entirely to LEDs can save $15-30 per month, or roughly 10-15% of the lighting-related portion of your bill.

Yes. Smart thermostats typically save 10-12% on heating and 15% on cooling per the EPA. At a cost of $150-250 installed, most homeowners recoup the investment within 1-2 years and continue saving for the device's 10+ year lifespan.

Attic insulation typically pays back in 3-7 years depending on climate and current insulation level. In extreme climates the payback can be faster. Insulation also adds home value and improves year-round comfort.

HVAC systems typically last 15-20 years. If your system is over 10 years old and needs major repairs, replacement often makes more financial sense. A new high-efficiency system (SEER 18+) can use 20-40% less energy than early-2000s systems, and federal tax credits may offset part of the cost.
