Commercial Insurance Premium Formula

Business insurance premiums are estimated separately for each coverage type:

General Liability
GL Premium = (Revenue / $100K) x Industry Rate
Commercial Property
Property Premium = (Asset Value / $1K) x Property Rate
Workers Compensation
WC Premium = (Employees x Avg Salary / $100) x Class Rate

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Select your business type - industry category that best fits.
  2. Enter annual revenue - gross annual revenue.

Example Scenario

A retail business with $1,000,000 in revenue, 10 employees, and $250,000 in assets would pay approximately $2,000 for general liability, $875 for commercial property, and $3,750 for workers compensation - totaling about $6,625 per year or $552 per month.

Frequently Asked Questions

Most businesses need general liability, commercial property, workers compensation, and business interruption insurance. Some also need professional liability, commercial auto, and cyber liability.

A BOP bundles general liability and commercial property insurance at a 15-20% discount compared to purchasing them separately. It is designed for small to mid-sized businesses.

Costs vary widely: general liability ($400-$1,500/year), commercial property ($500-$3,000/year), workers comp ($700-$5,000+/year). Total depends on industry, size, and risk.

Key factors include industry type, revenue, employees, assets, claims history, location, and coverage limits. Higher-risk industries and larger businesses pay more.
