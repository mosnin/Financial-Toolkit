Deductible Optimization Formula

The total expected annual cost for each deductible option is calculated as:

Total Annual Cost Formula
Total Cost = Annual Premium + (Deductible x Expected Claims per Year)

The optimal deductible is the option with the lowest total expected annual cost. A higher deductible lowers your premium but increases your expected out-of-pocket cost when claims are filed.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter deductible and premium for each option (at least two).
  2. Enter expected claims per year - use 0.1 for one claim per decade, 0.5 for every two years, etc.
  3. Click "Compare Options" to see total costs and the optimal choice.

Example Scenario

Comparing a $500 deductible with $1,200/year premium versus a $1,000 deductible with $1,000/year premium, assuming 0.5 claims per year: Option 1 costs $1,200 + ($500 x 0.5) = $1,450. Option 2 costs $1,000 + ($1,000 x 0.5) = $1,500. The $500 deductible is optimal in this case, saving $50 per year.
