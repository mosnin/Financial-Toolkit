Settlement Tax Formula

The tax treatment of your settlement depends on its type:

Settlement Tax Calculation
Taxable Portion = Settlement Amount x Taxable Percentage (varies by type)
Tax Owed = Taxable Portion x Combined Tax Rate
Net = Settlement - Attorney Fees - Tax Owed

Personal injury (physical) settlements are generally 100% tax-free. Employment settlements are typically 100% taxable. Property settlements are taxable to the extent they exceed your basis (cost) in the property. Punitive damages and emotional distress (non-physical) damages are fully taxable.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the total settlement amount - the gross amount before attorney fees and taxes.
  2. Select the settlement type - this determines what percentage is taxable.
  3. Enter attorney fees - the amount paid to your attorney (contingency or hourly).
  4. Enter your estimated tax rate - combine federal and state rates (typically 25-40% for most people).
  5. Click "Calculate Taxes" to see the taxable portion, estimated tax, and your net amount.

Example Scenario

You receive a $100,000 employment discrimination settlement with $33,000 in attorney fees (33% contingency). Since employment settlements are fully taxable, at a 30% combined tax rate you would owe approximately $30,000 in taxes. After paying $33,000 in attorney fees and $30,000 in taxes, your net amount would be approximately $37,000. Note: for employment cases, attorney fees may be deductible, which could reduce the taxable amount.
