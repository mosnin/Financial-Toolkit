Financial Disclaimer

Last updated: March 29, 2026

Important: CalcWise provides financial calculators for educational and informational purposes only. Our tools do not constitute financial advice, and results should not be relied upon as the sole basis for making financial decisions.

1. Not Financial Advice

CalcWise is not a financial advisor, broker, planner, or fiduciary. The calculators, content, and information provided on this website are for general educational and informational purposes only. Nothing on this Site constitutes or should be construed as financial advice, investment advice, tax advice, legal advice, or any other type of professional advice.

The use of our calculators does not create a client-advisor relationship between you and CalcWise. We do not recommend or endorse any specific financial products, services, institutions, or strategies.

2. Estimates Only

All results generated by CalcWise calculators are estimates based on the inputs you provide and standardized financial formulas. These estimates are intended to provide a general understanding of financial scenarios and should be treated as approximations, not guarantees. Actual outcomes may differ significantly due to factors including but not limited to:

  • Changes in interest rates, tax laws, or market conditions
  • Fees, closing costs, and other charges not fully captured by our models
  • Individual creditworthiness, lender-specific terms, and underwriting criteria
  • State and local tax variations
  • Inflation, economic cycles, and other macroeconomic factors
  • Insurance premiums, HOA fees, and property-specific costs
  • Rounding differences and computational simplifications

3. Real Estate Calculator Disclaimer

Our real estate calculators (including mortgage, affordability, rent-vs-buy, cap rate, and property investment calculators) provide estimates based on simplified models. Actual mortgage payments, qualification amounts, and investment returns depend on many factors not fully represented in our tools, including your credit score, the specific lender's terms, property location, homeowners insurance rates, private mortgage insurance requirements, and local property tax assessments.

Our calculators do not account for all possible fees, such as origination fees, discount points, appraisal costs, title insurance, survey fees, or recording fees. Always obtain a Loan Estimate from your lender for accurate figures.

4. Investment Calculator Disclaimer

Our investment and compound interest calculators assume consistent rates of return and regular contributions. In reality, investment returns fluctuate and past performance does not guarantee future results. These calculators do not account for investment fees, expense ratios, capital gains taxes, or the risk of loss of principal.

All investments carry risk, including the potential loss of the entire amount invested. Our calculators should not be used as the basis for investment decisions without consultation with a qualified financial advisor who understands your complete financial situation.

5. Business Calculator Disclaimer

Our business calculators (including profit margin, break-even, burn rate, and ROI calculators) are based on simplified models that may not capture the full complexity of your business operations. Actual business financial performance depends on numerous factors including market conditions, competitive dynamics, operational efficiency, and regulatory requirements.

These tools are designed to support preliminary analysis and should not replace professional business planning, accounting, or financial advisory services.

6. Tax Disclaimer

CalcWise calculators do not provide tax advice. Any tax-related estimates or calculations are approximate and based on general assumptions. Tax laws are complex, vary by jurisdiction, and change frequently. Your actual tax liability depends on your complete financial situation. Always consult a qualified tax professional for advice specific to your circumstances.

7. No Guarantee of Accuracy

While we make every reasonable effort to ensure the accuracy of our calculators, we do not guarantee that our tools are free from errors. Financial formulas involve complex mathematics, and our implementations necessarily make simplifying assumptions. We encourage users to verify important calculations independently and to report any discrepancies they discover.

8. Consult Qualified Professionals

Before making any significant financial decisions, we strongly recommend consulting with qualified professionals, including:

  • Certified Financial Planner (CFP): For comprehensive financial planning and investment advice
  • Certified Public Accountant (CPA): For tax planning and compliance
  • Licensed Mortgage Broker: For home loan qualification and terms
  • Real Estate Attorney: For property transactions and legal matters
  • Licensed Insurance Agent: For insurance coverage decisions

9. Assumption of Risk

By using CalcWise calculators, you acknowledge that you understand the limitations described in this disclaimer. You agree that any financial decisions you make are your own responsibility, and CalcWise bears no liability for outcomes resulting from your use of our tools or reliance on our calculator results.
