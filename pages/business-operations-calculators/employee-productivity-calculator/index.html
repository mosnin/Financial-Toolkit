Employee Productivity Formulas

Productivity is measured using two key ratios:

Revenue Per Employee
Revenue Per Employee = Total Revenue / Number of Employees
Revenue Per Hour
Revenue Per Hour = Total Revenue / Total Hours Worked

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter total annual revenue - the gross revenue your business generated.
  2. Enter number of employees - full-time equivalent headcount.
  3. Enter total hours worked - combined annual hours for all employees.
  4. Click "Calculate Productivity" to see your per-employee and per-hour metrics.

Example Scenario

A company generates $5,000,000 in annual revenue with 25 employees working a combined 52,000 hours. Revenue per employee = $200,000 and revenue per hour = $96.15. These figures can be compared against industry benchmarks to assess performance.
