Net Worth Formula

Formula
Net Worth = Total Assets - Total Liabilities

Your net worth is simply the total value of everything you own minus the total value of everything you owe. A positive net worth means your assets exceed your debts, while a negative net worth means you owe more than you own.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your assets: Fill in the current value of your cash and savings, investments (including retirement accounts), real estate, vehicles, and any other valuable assets.
  2. Enter your liabilities: Fill in the outstanding balances on your mortgage, student loans, credit cards, auto loans, and other debts.
  3. Click "Calculate Net Worth": The calculator will compute your total assets, total liabilities, and net worth.
  4. Review your results: Analyze where your wealth is concentrated and identify areas for improvement.

Frequently Asked Questions

Net worth is the difference between your total assets (what you own) and your total liabilities (what you owe). It provides a snapshot of your overall financial health at a specific point in time.

Assets include cash and savings accounts, investments (stocks, bonds, mutual funds, retirement accounts), real estate property values, vehicle values, and other valuable possessions like jewelry or collectibles.

Liabilities include mortgage balances, student loan debt, credit card balances, auto loans, personal loans, medical debt, and any other outstanding debts you owe.

It is recommended to calculate your net worth at least once a quarter (every 3 months). This helps you track your financial progress and identify trends over time.
