Frequently Asked Questions

A side hustle is financially worth it when your effective hourly rate (net profit divided by hours worked) exceeds your opportunity cost — i.e., the value of your time elsewhere. Factor in taxes, expenses, and the mental load it carries. Our "Is Your Side Hustle Worth It?" quiz walks through all of these dimensions.

Digital products, content creation, affiliate marketing, and online courses tend to be the most scalable because the marginal cost of serving an additional customer is near zero. Service-based hustles like freelancing or consulting are less scalable but often command higher hourly rates in the short term.

Most financial advisors recommend 6–12 months of living expenses as a runway before making the leap to full-time freelancing. This gives you time to build your pipeline without the panic of running out of money. You should also have consistent revenue from at least 2–3 clients before quitting your day job.
