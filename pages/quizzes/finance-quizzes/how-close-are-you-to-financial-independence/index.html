How Close Are You to Financial Independence?

Financial independence means having enough invested assets to cover your living expenses indefinitely without needing to work. This 8-question quiz assesses your savings rate, investment portfolio, passive income, and FI number awareness to show you exactly where you stand on the path to financial freedom — and what to do next.

Frequently Asked Questions

The 4% rule states that you can safely withdraw 4% of your investment portfolio each year without running out of money over a 30-year period. Your FI number is therefore 25 times your annual spending (annual spending ÷ 0.04). A household spending $60,000/year needs $1.5 million invested.

Savings rate has an enormous effect. Saving 10% takes roughly 43 years to reach FI. Saving 25% takes about 32 years. Saving 50% takes 17 years. Saving 75% takes just 7 years. The compounding effect of both more savings and lower spending dramatically shortens the timeline.

Yes. Passive income from rental properties, dividends, royalties, or businesses that run without your active involvement directly reduces the portfolio size needed for FI. $2,000/month in passive income reduces your required FI portfolio by $600,000 (using the 4% rule).

Most FI community members and academic research favour low-cost index funds (such as total market index funds) over individual stocks. They provide instant diversification, low fees, and historically match or beat most active management strategies over long periods. Our investment return calculator can model both scenarios.
