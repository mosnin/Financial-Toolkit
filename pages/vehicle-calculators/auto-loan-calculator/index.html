Auto Loan Payment Formula

The monthly payment for a fixed-rate auto loan uses the standard amortization formula:

Monthly Payment Formula
M = P [ r(1 + r)^n ] / [ (1 + r)^n - 1 ]

Where M is the monthly payment, P is the loan principal, r is the monthly interest rate (APR / 12), and n is the total number of monthly payments.

How to Use This Auto Loan Calculator

Example Scenario

Suppose you borrow $28,000 at 5.9% APR for 60 months. Your monthly payment would be approximately $541.05. Over the life of the loan, you would pay $4,462.77 in interest for a total of $32,462.77.

Frequently Asked Questions

An auto loan payment is calculated using the amortization formula: M = P[r(1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n - 1], where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate (APR divided by 12), and n is the total number of monthly payments.

APR (Annual Percentage Rate) represents the yearly cost of borrowing money, expressed as a percentage. It includes the interest rate and may include certain fees. A lower APR means lower borrowing costs.

Common auto loan terms are 36, 48, 60, and 72 months. Shorter terms have higher monthly payments but lower total interest. Most financial experts recommend 48 to 60 months for a good balance between affordability and total cost.

Most auto loans allow early payoff, which can save you significant interest. However, some lenders charge prepayment penalties. Check your loan agreement before making extra payments.

Your credit score is one of the biggest factors in determining your auto loan rate. Borrowers with excellent credit (750+) typically qualify for the lowest rates, while those with lower scores may pay several percentage points more.
