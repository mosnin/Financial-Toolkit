Fence Cost Formula

Fence costs are estimated using per-linear-foot rates adjusted for height and material:

Cost Formula
Total = (Length x Material Cost/ft x Height Factor) + (Length x Labor/ft) + (Gates x Gate Cost)

Material costs per linear foot at 6ft height: wood $12-$18, vinyl $18-$28, chain-link $7-$12. Labor adds $8-$15 per foot. Each gate adds $200-$500 depending on material and size.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the fence length - total linear feet of fencing needed.
  2. Select the fence height - typically 4, 5, 6, or 8 feet.
  3. Choose the material - wood, vinyl, or chain-link.
  4. Enter number of gates - standard walk-through gates.
  5. Click "Calculate Cost" to see material, labor, gate, and total costs.

Example Scenario

A 200-foot wood privacy fence at 6 feet tall with 2 gates would cost approximately $7,800. Material costs come to about $3,000, labor is about $4,000, and two gates add about $800. This averages to roughly $39 per linear foot installed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Fence costs per linear foot installed: chain-link $10-$20, wood privacy $20-$40, vinyl $25-$50. Gates add $150-$600 each depending on size and style.

Vinyl fencing lasts 20-30+ years with virtually no maintenance. Chain-link lasts 15-20 years. Wood lasts 10-20 years depending on wood type and maintenance.

Privacy fences are typically 6 feet tall. Decorative fences are 3-4 feet. Pool fences must meet local code, typically 4-5 feet minimum. Check local zoning for maximum height restrictions.

Many municipalities require permits for fences over 4-6 feet tall. Some HOAs have additional restrictions. Check with your local building department and HOA before starting.
