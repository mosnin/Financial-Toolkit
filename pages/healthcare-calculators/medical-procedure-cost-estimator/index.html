How This Estimator Works

This tool uses national average cost ranges for each procedure category and applies your insurance status to estimate out-of-pocket:

Average Procedure Cost
Used = Midpoint of low and high national average range
If Deductible NOT Met
Out-of-Pocket = Full Procedure Cost (up to remaining deductible amount)
If Deductible Met
Out-of-Pocket = Procedure Cost × (Coinsurance % / 100)

How to Use This Estimator

  1. Select the procedure - choose the closest matching category to your planned or recent procedure.
  2. Note the national average range displayed after selecting — this gives immediate context.
  3. Select your insurance tier - this affects the negotiated rate discount applied.
  4. Indicate deductible status - whether you've already met your annual deductible.
  5. Enter coinsurance - the percentage you pay after your deductible (typically 10-30%).
  6. Click Calculate to see your estimated out-of-pocket range.

Example Scenario

A patient needs a colonoscopy. National average range: $800-$2,500 (midpoint ~$1,650). They have a Silver plan employer-sponsored insurance with a negotiated rate discount of roughly 40%, bringing the effective cost to about $990. Their deductible is already met for the year. With 20% coinsurance, their out-of-pocket is approximately $198 — a far cry from paying list price without insurance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Medical procedure costs vary due to geographic location, facility type (hospital vs outpatient center), surgeon experience, insurance negotiated rates, and in/out-of-network status. The same procedure may cost $2,000 at one facility and $8,000 at another.

Hospitals must publish their prices under the Hospital Price Transparency Rule. You can also call your insurance company for an estimated cost of care, use your insurer's price comparison tool, or ask any provider for a "Good Faith Estimate" under the No Surprises Act.

Ambulatory Surgery Centers (ASCs) typically charge 40-60% less than hospitals for the same procedure. If your procedure can be safely performed in an ASC, choosing this setting can significantly reduce both your total cost and your out-of-pocket share.
