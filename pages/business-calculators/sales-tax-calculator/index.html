Sales Tax Formulas

Add Tax to Price
Tax Amount = Price x (Tax Rate / 100)
Total Price = Price + Tax Amount
Reverse (Find Pre-Tax Price)
Pre-Tax Price = Total Price / (1 + Tax Rate / 100)
Tax Amount = Total Price - Pre-Tax Price

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Choose your calculation mode: add tax to a price, or reverse-calculate the pre-tax price from a total.
  2. Enter the item price or total price depending on the mode.
  3. Enter the applicable tax rate as a percentage.
  4. Click Calculate Tax to see the tax amount, pre-tax price, and total.

Example Scenario

You are purchasing a laptop priced at $999.99 in a jurisdiction with a combined sales tax rate of 8.25%.

  • Tax Amount: $999.99 x 0.0825 = $82.50
  • Total Price: $999.99 + $82.50 = $1,082.49

Reverse calculation: If you paid $1,082.49 total and the tax rate was 8.25%:

  • Pre-Tax Price: $1,082.49 / 1.0825 = $999.99
