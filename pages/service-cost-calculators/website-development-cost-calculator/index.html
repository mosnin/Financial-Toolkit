Website costs range widely: a simple brochure site costs $2,000-$10,000, a business website $10,000-$50,000, an e-commerce site $20,000-$100,000+, and a custom web application $50,000-$500,000+.

Template designs use pre-built layouts customized with your content and branding. Custom designs are built from scratch, offering unique branding and tailored user experiences at a higher cost.
