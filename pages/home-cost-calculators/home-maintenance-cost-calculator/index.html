Home Maintenance Budget Formula

The annual maintenance budget is estimated using the 1% rule adjusted for home age and size:

Budget Formula
Base Budget = Home Value x 1% Age Adjustment: 0-10 years (0.75x), 10-25 years (1.0x), 25-50 years (1.5x), 50+ years (2.0x) Size Adjustment: +$1/sqft for homes over 2,500 sqft Annual Budget = Base Budget x Age Factor + Size Adjustment

The budget is then distributed across maintenance categories using industry-standard percentages: HVAC 20%, roofing/exterior 20%, plumbing/electrical 15%, appliances/interior 15%, landscaping 15%, and miscellaneous 15%.

How to Use This Calculator

  2. Enter the home age - how many years old the home is.
  3. Enter the square footage - total living area.

Example Scenario

A $350,000 home that is 15 years old and 2,000 square feet should budget approximately $3,500 per year ($292/month) for maintenance. This breaks down to roughly $700 for HVAC, $700 for roofing and exterior, $525 for plumbing and electrical, $525 for appliances, $525 for landscaping, and $525 for emergencies.
