Roofing Cost Formula

Roofing costs are estimated using material and labor rates per square foot, adjusted for building height:

Cost Formula
Total = (Roof Area x Material $/sqft) + (Roof Area x Labor $/sqft x Story Multiplier)

Material costs per square foot: asphalt $1.50-$3.50, metal $3.50-$7.00, tile $7.00-$15.00. Labor costs range from $2.00-$5.00 per square foot, with a multiplier for multi-story buildings.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the roof area - total square footage of your roof surface.
  2. Select the material type - asphalt shingles, metal, or tile.
  3. Choose the number of stories - affects labor costs due to height and access difficulty.
  4. Click "Calculate Cost" to see material cost, labor cost, and total.

Example Scenario

A 2,000-square-foot roof on a two-story home using asphalt shingles would cost approximately $12,500. Material costs come to about $5,000 ($2.50/sqft) and labor costs are about $7,500 ($3.40/sqft with the two-story premium).

Frequently Asked Questions

A new roof costs $5,000-$12,000 for asphalt shingles, $10,000-$25,000 for metal roofing, and $20,000-$50,000+ for tile on an average-sized home. Total cost depends on roof area, material, pitch, and labor rates.

Asphalt shingles last 20-30 years, metal roofing lasts 40-70 years, and tile roofing can last 50-100+ years. Higher upfront cost materials often provide better long-term value.

Late spring through early fall is ideal for most roofing projects. Mild, dry weather ensures proper material installation. Off-season scheduling may offer lower prices but weather delays are more likely.
