Are You Living Paycheck to Paycheck?

Living paycheck to paycheck affects people at every income level — it is less about how much you earn and more about the gap between income and spending. This 7-question quiz identifies the specific warning signs in your situation and shows you what to do about it with a realistic, step-by-step action plan.

Absolutely. Studies consistently show that a significant portion of six-figure earners have minimal savings. Lifestyle inflation — expanding spending to match every income increase — is the primary cause regardless of income level.

The fastest way is to create an immediate gap between income and spending: cut one major expense (housing, car, subscriptions), automate a savings transfer on payday before you can spend it, and stop adding new debt. Even $200/month saved changes your financial psychology.

A healthy checking account buffer is 1–2 months of expenses, separate from your emergency fund. This prevents overdrafts from timing mismatches and reduces the financial anxiety of watching your balance hit zero before payday.

Using credit cards is not a problem if you pay the full balance every month. The red flag is carrying a balance — it means you're spending money you don't yet have, creating a debt cycle that typically worsens over time without deliberate intervention.
