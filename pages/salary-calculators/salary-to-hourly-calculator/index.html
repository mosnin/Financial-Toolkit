Salary to Hourly Formula

The conversion from annual salary to hourly rate is:

Hourly Rate Formula
Hourly Rate = Annual Salary / (Hours Per Week x Weeks Per Year)

For a standard full-time schedule: Hourly Rate = Annual Salary / 2,080.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your annual salary - your total yearly compensation before taxes.
  2. Enter hours per week - how many hours you actually work each week.
  3. Enter weeks per year - use 52 for a full year or adjust for unpaid leave.
  4. Click "Calculate Hourly Rate" to see your hourly, daily, and weekly rates.

Example Scenario

If you earn a $60,000 annual salary and work 40 hours per week for 52 weeks, your hourly rate is $28.85. That translates to $230.77 per day and $1,153.85 per week. If you actually work 50 hours per week, your effective hourly rate drops to $23.08.

Frequently Asked Questions

Divide your annual salary by the number of working hours in a year. For a standard 40-hour work week over 52 weeks, divide by 2,080. For example, $60,000 / 2,080 = $28.85 per hour.

A $50,000 annual salary equals approximately $24.04 per hour based on a standard 2,080-hour work year. That breaks down to about $192.31 per day or $961.54 per week.

Converting salary to hourly helps you compare job offers between salaried and hourly positions, understand the true value of your time, negotiate freelance rates, or evaluate whether overtime is worthwhile.

This calculator shows your base hourly equivalent. If you regularly work more than 40 hours per week, your effective hourly rate will be lower. Increase the hours per week field to see your actual rate.
