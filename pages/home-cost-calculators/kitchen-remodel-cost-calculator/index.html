Kitchen Remodel Cost Formula

Kitchen remodel costs are calculated based on the size of the space, the scope of work, and the appliance package:

Cost Formula
Total Cost = (Kitchen Sq Ft x Cost per Sq Ft for Scope) + Appliance Package Cost

Cost per square foot ranges from $75-$150 for minor remodels, $150-$250 for major remodels, and $250-$450+ for luxury renovations. Appliance costs are added separately based on your chosen package.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your kitchen size - measure the total square footage of your kitchen space.
  2. Select the remodel scope - minor for cosmetic updates, major for significant changes, or luxury for a high-end transformation.
  3. Choose your appliance package - from keeping existing appliances to installing luxury professional-grade units.
  4. Click "Calculate Cost" to see your estimated total, cost per square foot, and cost breakdown.

Example Scenario

A 200-square-foot kitchen undergoing a major remodel with standard new appliances would cost approximately $44,500. The construction cost would be around $40,000 ($200 per sq ft) plus $4,500 for new standard appliances. The total cost per square foot comes to about $222.

Frequently Asked Questions

A minor kitchen remodel costs $10,000-$25,000 on average, a major remodel runs $25,000-$75,000, and a luxury renovation can exceed $75,000-$150,000+. Costs depend on kitchen size, materials, appliances, and labor rates in your area.

Cabinetry and countertops are typically the most expensive components, often accounting for 30-40% of the total budget. Appliances and labor are the next largest cost categories.

A minor kitchen remodel takes 4-6 weeks on average. A major remodel takes 2-4 months, and a full gut renovation can take 4-6 months or longer depending on the scope of work and permit requirements.

Kitchen remodels typically recoup 50-80% of their cost at resale, with minor remodels offering the highest ROI. Beyond resale value, a modern kitchen improves daily functionality and enjoyment of your home.
