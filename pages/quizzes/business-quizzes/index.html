Frequently Asked Questions

Profitability means your revenue consistently exceeds total costs, including COGS, operating expenses, taxes, and owner compensation. A healthy net profit margin varies by industry — typically 10% is considered good. Our quiz examines your margins, revenue consistency, and cost structure to give you a clear assessment.

A business is typically ready to hire when it has consistent revenue covering at least 1.25 times the total employee cost (salary plus benefits plus employer taxes), a backlog of work that cannot be handled solo, and at least three months of payroll in cash reserves. Take our hiring readiness quiz to assess your situation.

Healthy cash flow means more money is flowing into the business than out, with enough runway to cover at least two to three months of operating expenses even during a slow period. A positive operating cash flow with growing reserves signals a financially strong business.

Your prices are likely too low if customers almost never push back on price, your margins are thin, you are working at full capacity but still struggling to pay yourself, or competitors charge significantly more for similar offerings. Our pricing quiz helps identify whether you are undercharging and by how much.
