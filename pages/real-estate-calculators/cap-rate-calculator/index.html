Cap Rate Formula

Capitalization Rate
Cap Rate = (NOI / Property Value) x 100 NOI = Gross Rental Income - Operating Expenses

Net Operating Income (NOI) is the annual income remaining after all operating expenses are subtracted from gross rental income. It excludes mortgage payments, capital expenditures, and depreciation.

The cap rate expresses the property's return as a percentage of its value, enabling easy comparison across different properties and markets.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the property value -- the purchase price or current market value.
  2. Enter gross annual rental income -- the total rent collected per year before expenses.
  3. Enter annual operating expenses -- include property taxes, insurance, maintenance, and management fees (do not include mortgage payments).
  4. Click "Calculate Cap Rate" to see the NOI, cap rate, and monthly NOI.

Example Scenario

$500,000 Investment Property

You are considering a rental property listed at $500,000. It generates $48,000 per year in rental income ($4,000/month). Annual operating expenses total $18,000 (property taxes, insurance, repairs, and management fees).

NOI = $48,000 - $18,000 = $30,000

Cap Rate = $30,000 / $500,000 = 6.00%

This means the property generates a 6% return on its value before financing costs, which is competitive in many urban markets.
