Frequently Asked Questions

Mobile app costs range from $15,000-$50,000 for a simple app, $50,000-$150,000 for medium complexity, and $150,000-$500,000+ for complex, feature-rich apps.

Consider your target audience. iOS users tend to spend more, while Android has larger global market share. Starting with one platform and expanding later can reduce initial costs.

A simple app takes 2-4 months, medium complexity 4-8 months, and complex apps 8-18+ months. Timeline depends on features, platform, team size, and iteration cycles.

Native apps offer best performance and platform-specific features. Cross-platform frameworks (React Native, Flutter) build for both from one codebase, saving 30-40% but with some performance trade-offs.

Ongoing costs include server hosting ($50-$2,000+/month), app store fees ($99/year Apple, $25 one-time Google), maintenance (15-20% of initial cost annually), and marketing/support.
