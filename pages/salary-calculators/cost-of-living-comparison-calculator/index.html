Cost of Living Comparison Formula

The equivalent salary is calculated using the ratio of cost of living indexes:

Equivalent Salary Formula
Equivalent Salary = Current Salary x (Target City Index / Current City Index)

If the target city has a higher index, you will need a higher salary to maintain the same standard of living.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your current salary - your annual compensation in your current city.
  2. Enter the current city COL index - look up your city's cost of living index (100 = national average).
  3. Enter the target city COL index - look up the COL index of the city you are considering.
  4. Click "Compare Cost of Living" to see the equivalent salary and difference.

Example Scenario

If you earn $75,000 in a city with a COL index of 100 and want to move to a city with an index of 130, you would need $97,500 (30% more) to maintain your current lifestyle. That is a $22,500 annual difference, or $1,875 per month.

Frequently Asked Questions

A cost of living index measures the relative cost of living in a city compared to a national average (baseline of 100). An index of 120 means the city is 20% more expensive than average.

Divide the target city's index by your current city's index, then multiply by your current salary. For example: (130/100) x $70,000 = $91,000.

Cost of living indexes typically include housing, groceries, utilities, transportation, healthcare, and miscellaneous goods and services. Housing costs often drive the biggest differences.
