How Costs Are Calculated

Total Monthly Cost
Total = Sum of all selected service monthly prices
Annual Cost
Annual = Total Monthly × 12
Cost per Viewing Hour
$/hr = Monthly Price / Hours Watched per Month

Services with fewer than 4 hours/month usage and a cost per hour above $3.00 are flagged as candidates for cancellation or rotation.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Check each streaming service you currently subscribe to.
  2. Adjust the monthly price if your plan differs from the pre-filled price (e.g., you have a premium tier or bundle).
  3. Enter estimated hours watched per month on each service to get a cost-per-hour metric.
  4. Add any custom services not in the list.
  5. Click Calculate Streaming Cost to see your total and get consolidation tips.

Example Scenario

A household subscribing to 5 streaming services:

  • Netflix Standard (no ads): $15.49/mo — 40 hrs/mo → $0.39/hr
  • Disney+ Premium: $13.99/mo — 10 hrs/mo → $1.40/hr
  • Hulu (with ads): $7.99/mo — 5 hrs/mo → $1.60/hr
  • HBO Max: $15.99/mo — 8 hrs/mo → $2.00/hr
  • Apple TV+: $9.99/mo — 2 hrs/mo → $5.00/hr (low value)

Total: $63.45/month ($761.40/year) — Canceling Apple TV+ and rotating to it seasonally would save $119.88/year.
