Settlement Estimation Formula

The multiplier method is one of the most common ways insurance companies estimate personal injury settlement values:

Settlement Formula
Settlement = (Medical Bills x Multiplier) + Lost Wages

Where the multiplier reflects the severity of your injuries (1.5 for minor, up to 5 for severe/permanent injuries). The result is then compared to the insurance policy limit, which acts as a practical cap on the settlement amount.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your total medical bills - include all medical expenses related to the injury (ER visits, surgeries, physical therapy, medications).
  2. Enter your lost wages - income you have lost due to the injury, including missed work and reduced earning capacity.
  3. Select a pain and suffering multiplier - use 1.5-2 for minor injuries, 2-3 for moderate, and 3-5 for severe or permanent injuries.
  4. Enter the insurance policy limit - the maximum amount the at-fault party's insurance will pay.
  5. Click "Calculate Settlement" to see your estimated settlement range.

Example Scenario

Suppose you have $15,000 in medical bills and $5,000 in lost wages from a car accident. Using a multiplier of 2.5 for moderate injuries, your pain and suffering estimate would be $37,500. Adding lost wages gives a total claim value of $42,500. With a $100,000 policy limit, your estimated settlement range would be approximately $34,000 to $51,000, accounting for negotiation variance.
