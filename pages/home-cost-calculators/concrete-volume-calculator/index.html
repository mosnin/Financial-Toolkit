Concrete Volume Formula

Concrete volume is calculated by multiplying the three dimensions and converting to cubic yards:

Volume Formula
Cubic Feet = Length (ft) x Width (ft) x Depth (ft) Cubic Yards = Cubic Feet / 27 Bags (80-lb) = Cubic Feet / 0.6

Note: depth is often measured in inches. Convert to feet by dividing by 12. One 80-lb bag yields approximately 0.6 cubic feet of concrete.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the length - the longest dimension of your pour in feet.
  2. Enter the width - the shorter dimension in feet.
  3. Enter the depth - the thickness of the slab in inches.
  4. Click "Calculate Volume" to see cubic yards, bags needed, and estimated costs.

Example Scenario

A 20 x 10 foot patio slab poured 4 inches deep requires approximately 2.47 cubic yards of concrete. That equals about 124 bags of 80-lb concrete mix ($6.50/bag = $806) or roughly 2.5 yards of ready-mix delivery ($150/yard = $375).
