How Quarterly Estimated Taxes Work

Estimated Annual Tax
Net SE Income = Business Income − Business Expenses
SE Tax = Net SE Income × 0.9235 × 15.3%
AGI = Net SE + Other Income − SE Tax/2 − Deductions
Federal Income Tax = From brackets
Total Tax = Federal Tax + SE Tax
Safe Harbor Calculation
If Prior Year AGI ≤ $150,000: Safe Harbor = Prior Year Tax × 100%
If Prior Year AGI > $150,000: Safe Harbor = Prior Year Tax × 110%
Per Quarter = Safe Harbor / 4
Quarterly Payment
Pay the LESSER of: (Current Year Estimated Tax / 4) or (Safe Harbor / 4)
to avoid underpayment penalties.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your expected business or self-employment income for the year.
  2. Enter any other income from wages, investments, or rental properties.
  3. Enter your expected deductions (business expenses, retirement contributions, etc.).
  4. Enter your prior year total tax from last year's Form 1040.
  5. Enter prior year AGI to determine the correct safe harbor percentage.
  6. Click Calculate Quarterly Payments.
