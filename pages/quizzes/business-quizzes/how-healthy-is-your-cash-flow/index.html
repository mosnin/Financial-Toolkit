How Healthy Is Your Cash Flow?

A profitable business can still fail from poor cash flow. Cash flow measures the timing of money in and out — and gaps between the two can cripple even a growing company. This 8-question quiz assesses your runway, collection periods, payment patterns, and cash buffers to give you a clear picture of your cash flow health.

Frequently Asked Questions

Cash runway is how long your business can operate at its current burn rate before running out of cash. Calculate it by dividing your current cash balance by your average monthly net cash outflow. For example, $90,000 in cash with $15,000 monthly expenses = 6 months of runway. Healthy businesses target 3–6+ months of runway at all times.

The cash conversion cycle (CCC) measures how long it takes to convert your business activities into cash. It's calculated as: Days Inventory Outstanding + Days Sales Outstanding - Days Payable Outstanding. A shorter (or negative) CCC means you collect cash quickly and pay suppliers later — which is excellent for cash flow.

Working capital (current assets minus current liabilities) should be positive, with a current ratio above 1.5x ideally. Most small businesses should maintain 1–3 months of operating expenses in liquid working capital. Businesses with seasonal revenue fluctuations should hold more to bridge slow periods.
