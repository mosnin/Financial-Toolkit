Newsletter Revenue Formulas

Sponsorship Revenue
Sponsorship Revenue = (Subscribers / 1,000) x CPM x Sponsored Issues per Month
Paid Subscription MRR
Paid MRR = Subscribers x (Paid Tier % / 100) x Paid Tier Price
Monthly Affiliate Estimate
Affiliate = Subscribers x (Open Rate / 100) x (Click Rate / 100) x Revenue per Click x Issues/Month
Revenue per Subscriber
RPS = Total Monthly Revenue / Total Subscribers

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your total subscribers — the full list size across free and paid.
  2. Set your open rate — check your email platform dashboard for this average.
  3. Enter your click rate — used to estimate affiliate link clicks per issue.
  4. Set your sponsorship CPM — what you charge per 1,000 subscribers per issue. Research comparable newsletters for benchmarks.
  5. Enter sponsored issues per month — how often you include a paid sponsor.
  6. Set your paid tier conversion and price for premium subscriber revenue.
  7. Enter your estimated affiliate revenue per click — check your affiliate dashboards for historical EPC (earnings per click).

Example Scenario

A finance newsletter with 8,000 subscribers, 45% open rate, 6% click rate, $60 CPM, 4 sponsored issues/month, 4% paid tier at $15/month, and $0.75 affiliate earnings per click.

  • Sponsorship: (8,000 / 1,000) x $60 x 4 = $1,920/month
  • Paid MRR: 8,000 x 4% x $15 = $4,800/month
  • Monthly affiliate clicks: 8,000 x 45% x 6% x 4 issues = 864 clicks
  • Affiliate Revenue: 864 x $0.75 = $648/month
  • Total Monthly Revenue: $7,368
  • Revenue per Subscriber: $7,368 / 8,000 = $0.92/subscriber/month

Frequently Asked Questions

Newsletter sponsorship CPMs range widely by niche. General newsletters see $20-$50 CPM. Finance, tech, and B2B newsletters often command $50-$150 CPM. High-value audiences with strong engagement can achieve $100-$400+ CPM.

Most sponsors want at least 30-40% open rates indicating strong engagement. Average open rates are 20-25%. Newsletters with 40%+ open rates are considered premium and can command higher CPMs from sponsors.

Affiliate links and paid subscriptions can start from day one for niche expert newsletters. Sponsorships typically require 1,000+ subscribers. Premium sponsors often prefer 5,000-10,000+ engaged subscribers before committing to paid placements.

Paid newsletter conversion typically ranges from 2% to 10%. High-value niche newsletters covering finance, investing, or B2B topics can achieve 5-20% paid conversion. General interest newsletters usually see 1-5% conversion to paid tiers.
