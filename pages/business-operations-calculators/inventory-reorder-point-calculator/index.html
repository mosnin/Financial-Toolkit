Reorder Point Formula

The reorder point is calculated using the following formula:

Reorder Point Formula
ROP = (Daily Demand x Lead Time) + Safety Stock

Where Daily Demand is the average number of units sold per day, Lead Time is the number of days between placing and receiving an order, and Safety Stock is the buffer inventory to protect against variability.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter average daily demand - the typical number of units you sell or use each day.
  2. Enter lead time - how many days it takes from ordering to receiving inventory.
  3. Enter safety stock - extra units you want to keep as a buffer.
  4. Click "Calculate Reorder Point" to see when you should place your next order.

Example Scenario

A retailer sells an average of 50 units per day. Their supplier takes 7 days to deliver, and they maintain a safety stock of 100 units. The reorder point would be (50 x 7) + 100 = 450 units. When inventory drops to 450 units, it is time to reorder.

Frequently Asked Questions

A reorder point is the inventory level at which a new order should be placed to replenish stock before it runs out. It accounts for lead time demand and safety stock to prevent stockouts.

Safety stock is extra inventory kept to guard against variability in demand or supply. It is typically calculated based on demand variability, lead time variability, and your desired service level.

Setting the reorder point too low increases the risk of stockouts, which can lead to lost sales, backorders, and dissatisfied customers.

Lead time is the total time from placing an order with a supplier to receiving the goods. It includes order processing, manufacturing, shipping, and receiving time.

You should recalculate reorder points whenever there are significant changes in demand patterns, supplier lead times, or business conditions. Many businesses review them quarterly or monthly.
