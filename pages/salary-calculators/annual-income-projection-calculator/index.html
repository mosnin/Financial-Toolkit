Income Projection Formula

Your projected annual income accounts for mid-year raises:

Projected Annual Income
Total = (Monthly x Pre-Raise Months) + (Monthly x (1 + Raise%) x Post-Raise Months) + Side Income x 12 + Bonus

This accounts for the fact that a raise partway through the year only affects the remaining months.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter current monthly income - your gross monthly pay from your primary job.
  2. Enter expected raise percentage - the raise you anticipate (0 if none expected).
  3. Enter raise month - the month (1-12) when the raise takes effect.
  4. Enter monthly side income - freelance, rental, or other regular side income.
  5. Enter expected bonus - any annual bonus you anticipate.
  6. Click "Project Income" to see your projected annual total.

Example Scenario

With $6,000 monthly income, a 5% raise in July (month 7), $500/month side income, and a $5,000 bonus: Primary income is $36,000 (Jan-Jun) + $37,800 (Jul-Dec at $6,300) = $73,800. Add $6,000 side income and $5,000 bonus for a total projected income of $84,800.
