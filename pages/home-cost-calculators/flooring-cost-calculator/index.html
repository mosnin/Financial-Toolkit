Flooring Cost Formula

Flooring costs are calculated by combining material and installation costs per square foot:

Cost Formula
Total Cost = Area x (Material Cost/sqft + Labor Cost/sqft)

Material costs per sqft: hardwood $6-$12, laminate $2-$5, tile $4-$10, carpet $2-$5. Professional labor adds $3-$8 per sqft depending on material complexity.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the floor area - the total square footage to be covered.
  2. Select the material - hardwood, laminate, tile, or carpet.
  3. Choose installation type - DIY for materials only or professional for full service.
  4. Click "Calculate Cost" to see your material cost, labor cost, and total.

Example Scenario

Installing hardwood flooring in a 500-square-foot living room with professional installation would cost approximately $6,500. Material costs come to about $4,500 ($9/sqft) and labor costs are about $2,000 ($4/sqft).
