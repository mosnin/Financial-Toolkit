Bonus Tax Formula

Bonuses are taxed as supplemental income. Using the flat percentage method:

After-Tax Bonus Formula
After-Tax Bonus = Bonus - (Bonus x Federal Rate) - (Bonus x State Rate) - (Bonus x 7.65%)

The standard federal withholding rate for supplemental income is 22% for bonuses up to $1 million.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the bonus amount - the gross bonus before any taxes.
  2. Enter the federal tax rate - typically 22% for bonuses (flat method).
  3. Enter your state tax rate - use 0% for no-income-tax states.
  4. Select your filing status for reference.
  5. Click "Calculate Bonus" to see your after-tax bonus amount.

Example Scenario

If you receive a $10,000 bonus, with 22% federal tax ($2,200), 5% state tax ($500), and 7.65% FICA ($765), your total taxes would be $3,465. Your after-tax bonus would be $6,535.
