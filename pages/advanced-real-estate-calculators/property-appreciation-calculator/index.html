Property Appreciation Formula

Compound Annual Appreciation
Future Value = Current Value × (1 + Rate)^Years Total Appreciation = Future Value − Current Value Appreciation % = (Total Appreciation / Current Value) × 100

Compound appreciation means each year's gain is calculated on the updated, higher value -- not just the original purchase price. This exponential growth is what makes real estate a powerful long-term wealth-building asset.

The equity gained figure represents the increase in your net worth attributable solely to market appreciation, separate from any mortgage paydown.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the current property value -- use the current market value or your purchase price.
  2. Enter the annual appreciation rate -- the national average is 3-4%; check your local market for a more accurate figure.
  3. Enter the number of years -- your intended holding period or planning horizon.
  4. Click "Calculate Future Value" to see the projected value, total appreciation, and equity gained.

Example Scenario

$350,000 Home at 3.5% Appreciation Over 10 Years

You purchase a home for $350,000 in a market that has historically appreciated at 3.5% per year. After 10 years:

Future Value = $350,000 × (1.035)^10 = $493,386

Total Appreciation = $493,386 − $350,000 = $143,386

Appreciation % = $143,386 / $350,000 = 40.97%

This means your property gains over $143,000 in value purely from market appreciation -- not counting any principal paydown on your mortgage.
