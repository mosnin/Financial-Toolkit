Self-Employment Tax Formula

Step 1: SE Tax Base
SE Tax Base = Net SE Income × 92.35% (0.9235)
Step 2: SE Tax
SE Tax = SE Tax Base × 15.3% (up to Social Security wage base)
For income above $168,600 SS base: additional 2.9% Medicare only
Step 3: AGI Adjustment
Deductible SE = SE Tax × 50%
AGI = Net SE Income + Other Income − Deductible SE
Step 4: Total Tax
Federal Income Tax = Applied to (AGI − Standard Deduction)
Total Tax = SE Tax + Federal Income Tax

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your net self-employment income — business revenue minus all business expenses.
  2. Enter any other ordinary income such as W2 wages.
  3. Select your filing status.
  4. Click Calculate SE Tax to see your full tax breakdown.

Example Scenario

A freelance graphic designer with $70,000 net SE income, filing single:

  • SE Tax Base: $70,000 × 92.35% = $64,645
  • SE Tax: $64,645 × 15.3% = $9,891
  • Deductible Half: $9,891 / 2 = $4,946
  • AGI: $70,000 − $4,946 = $65,054
  • Taxable Income: $65,054 − $14,600 = $50,454
  • Federal Income Tax: ~$6,793
  • Total Tax: $9,891 + $6,793 = $16,684
  • Effective Rate: 23.8% of net SE income
