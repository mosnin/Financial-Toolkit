Income Formulas

Short-Term Rental Revenue
Booked Nights = 30.44 x (Occupancy Rate / 100) Avg Stay Length = 3 nights (assumed) Number of Stays = Booked Nights / Avg Stay Length Gross Revenue = (Booked Nights x Nightly Rate) + (Number of Stays x Cleaning Fee) Platform Fees = Gross Revenue x (Platform Fee / 100) Monthly Revenue = Gross Revenue - Platform Fees Monthly Profit = Monthly Revenue - Monthly Expenses

The effective nightly rate divides your monthly profit by booked nights to show what you truly earn per night after all costs.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your nightly rate -- research comparable listings in your area for competitive pricing.
  2. Set the occupancy rate -- this represents the percentage of nights booked per month.
  3. Enter the cleaning fee -- charged per guest stay to cover turnover costs.
  4. Enter total monthly expenses -- mortgage, utilities, internet, insurance, supplies, maintenance.
  5. Set the platform fee percentage -- Airbnb charges 3% (split-fee) or 14-16% (host-only).

Example Scenario

Beach Condo at $150/Night

Nightly rate: $150. Occupancy: 65%. Cleaning fee: $100/stay. Monthly expenses: $2,000. Platform fee: 3%.

Booked nights: 30.44 x 0.65 = ~19.8 nights. With an average 3-night stay, that is about 6.6 stays per month.

Gross revenue: (19.8 x $150) + (6.6 x $100) = $2,970 + $660 = $3,630. Platform fee: $3,630 x 0.03 = $109. Net revenue: $3,521.

Monthly profit: $3,521 - $2,000 = $1,521. Annual profit: $18,252. Effective nightly rate: $1,521 / 19.8 = $76.82.

Frequently Asked Questions

Average Airbnb occupancy rates range from 50-75% depending on location, season, and property type. Popular tourist destinations may see 70-90%, while other areas average 45-60%. Start conservatively and adjust based on local market data.

Airbnb typically charges hosts 3% per booking under the split-fee model, or 14-16% under the host-only fee model. VRBO charges 5%, and other platforms vary. Factor in the fee model you use.

Cleaning fees are charged per stay and help offset turnover costs. They add to total revenue but can affect booking demand if set too high. Many hosts set cleaning fees to cover actual cleaning costs while keeping nightly rates competitive.

Short-term rentals can generate 2-3x more revenue than long-term rentals but come with higher expenses, more management effort, seasonal variability, and regulatory risks. Net profit depends heavily on location, occupancy rates, and management efficiency.

Budget for mortgage, utilities, internet, cleaning, linens and supplies, furniture replacement, insurance, property management (if applicable), maintenance, lawn care, and local taxes or permits.
