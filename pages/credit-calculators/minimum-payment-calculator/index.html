How Minimum Payments Work

Credit card minimum payments are typically calculated as:

Minimum Payment Calculation
Minimum Payment = MAX(Balance x Minimum %, Flat Minimum Floor)

Each month, interest accrues on the remaining balance at the monthly rate (APR / 12). The minimum payment first covers the interest charge, and only the remainder reduces your principal balance. As the balance decreases, the minimum payment shrinks, extending the payoff timeline dramatically.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your current balance - the total amount you owe on the card.
  2. Enter your APR - found on your credit card statement or account details.
  3. Enter the minimum payment percentage - typically 1-3% of balance.
  4. Enter the flat minimum floor - the minimum dollar amount required (usually $25).
  5. Click "Calculate" to see how long payoff will take and the true cost.

Example Scenario

You have a $5,000 balance at 22.99% APR with a 2% minimum payment (floor of $25). Making only the minimum each month, it would take over 27 years to pay off the balance. You would pay approximately $8,400 in interest alone, bringing the total cost to over $13,400 for what was originally a $5,000 balance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Most credit card issuers calculate minimum payments as either a percentage of the balance (typically 1-3%) or a flat dollar amount (usually $25-35), whichever is greater. Some also add any fees and accrued interest to the minimum.

When you pay only the minimum, most of your payment goes toward interest rather than reducing the principal. As the balance slowly decreases, the minimum payment also decreases, creating a cycle that can take decades to resolve.

Even small amounts above the minimum can dramatically reduce payoff time. Paying double the minimum or adding a fixed extra amount each month can cut years off your payoff timeline and save thousands in interest.

Missing a minimum payment can result in late fees, a penalty APR (often 29.99%), and a negative mark on your credit report that stays for 7 years. Always make at least the minimum payment on time.
