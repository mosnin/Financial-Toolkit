Overtime Pay Formula

Total pay with overtime is calculated as:

Total Pay Formula
Total Pay = (Hourly Rate x Regular Hours) + (Hourly Rate x OT Multiplier x OT Hours)

For standard time-and-a-half: Total = (Rate x 40) + (Rate x 1.5 x OT Hours).

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your hourly rate - your standard pay per hour.
  2. Enter regular hours - hours at standard rate (typically 40).
  3. Enter overtime hours - additional hours beyond regular.
  4. Select the OT multiplier - typically 1.5x (time and a half).
  5. Click "Calculate Pay" to see your complete pay breakdown.

Example Scenario

If you earn $25/hour, work 40 regular hours and 10 overtime hours at 1.5x, your regular pay is $1,000, your overtime pay is $375 (10 x $37.50), for a total of $1,375. Your effective hourly rate across all 50 hours is $27.50.

Frequently Asked Questions

Overtime pay is calculated by multiplying your regular hourly rate by the overtime multiplier (typically 1.5x) for each hour worked beyond the standard 40-hour work week.

Time and a half means you earn 1.5 times your regular hourly rate for overtime hours. If you earn $20/hour, your overtime rate would be $30/hour.

Under the FLSA, non-exempt employees must receive overtime pay for hours worked over 40 in a workweek. Some states have daily overtime requirements as well.

No. Exempt employees (typically salaried workers in executive, administrative, or professional roles) are not entitled to overtime pay under the FLSA.

Double time means earning twice your regular hourly rate. Some employers or state laws require double time for hours beyond certain thresholds or on holidays.
