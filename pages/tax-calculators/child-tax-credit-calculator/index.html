Child Tax Credit Formula (2026)

Base Credit
Maximum CTC = Number of Qualifying Children × $2,000
Phase-Out Calculation
Phase-Out Threshold: $200,000 (single/HOH) or $400,000 (MFJ)
Excess AGI = max(0, AGI − Threshold)
Reduction = ceil(Excess AGI / 1,000) × $50
Adjusted CTC = max(0, Maximum CTC − Reduction)
Refundable ACTC (Additional Child Tax Credit)
Refundable = min(Adjusted CTC, 15% × max(0, Earned Income − $2,500))
Max Refundable per child: $1,700

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the number of qualifying children under age 17 at year-end.
  2. Select your filing status.

  4. Enter your earned income from wages or self-employment for the ACTC calculation.

Example Scenario

A married couple filing jointly with 3 children under 17, AGI of $150,000, and earned income of $150,000:

  • Maximum CTC: 3 × $2,000 = $6,000
  • Phase-Out: AGI $150,000 is below $400,000 threshold — no reduction
  • Adjusted CTC: $6,000
  • ACTC Refundable: 15% × ($150,000 − $2,500) = $22,125, limited to $6,000 → $6,000 refundable

Frequently Asked Questions

The Child Tax Credit is $2,000 per qualifying child under age 17 in 2026, subject to income phase-outs beginning at $200,000 for single filers and $400,000 for married filing jointly.

The Additional Child Tax Credit (ACTC) is the refundable portion — up to $1,700 per qualifying child in 2026. This means even if your tax liability is zero, you may receive up to $1,700 per child as a refund.

The CTC phases out at $200,000 AGI for single filers and $400,000 for married filing jointly. The credit reduces by $50 for every $1,000 of income above the threshold.

A qualifying child must be under age 17 at year-end, a US citizen or resident, claimed as a dependent on your return, and have lived with you for more than half the year.
