Delivery Driver Earnings Formulas

Gross Hourly Rate
Gross/Hour = Deliveries/Hour x (Base Pay + Tip per Delivery)
Gross Weekly Income
Gross Weekly = Gross/Hour x Hours per Week
Weekly Vehicle Expenses
Weekly Expenses = Miles/Week x (Gas Cost/Mile + Depreciation/Mile)
Net Weekly Income
Net Weekly = Gross Weekly - Weekly Expenses
Effective Hourly Rate
Effective Rate = Net Weekly / Hours per Week

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter deliveries per hour — track this over several shifts for accuracy; 2–3 deliveries/hour is typical in suburban markets.
  2. Enter base pay per delivery — the platform's base pay before tips (visible in your earnings history).
  3. Enter average tip — calculate from your recent earnings history by dividing total tips by number of deliveries.
  4. Enter hours per week — only count hours you are actively on the road, not waiting at home.
  5. Enter gas cost per mile — divide your gas price by your car's MPG (e.g., $3.60/gal ÷ 30 MPG = $0.12/mile).
  6. Enter miles per week — include all miles driven while working, including dead miles between orders.
  7. Enter depreciation — $0.06–$0.10/mile is a conservative estimate for most sedans.

Example Scenario

Carlos drives for DoorDash on weekends. He completes 2.5 deliveries/hour, earns $5.50 base pay and $3.00 tips per delivery, works 20 hours/week, drives 150 miles/week, gas costs him $0.12/mile, and depreciation is $0.08/mile.

  • Gross hourly: 2.5 x ($5.50 + $3.00) = $21.25/hr
  • Gross weekly: $21.25 x 20 = $425
  • Weekly expenses: 150 x ($0.12 + $0.08) = $30
  • Net weekly: $425 - $30 = $395
  • Effective hourly rate: $395 / 20 = $19.75/hr
  • Annual net income: $395 x 52 = $20,540

Carlos nets about $19.75/hour — a solid side hustle income, especially considering the flexibility of gig work.
