How the EITC Is Calculated

Phase-In (building up to maximum)
Credit = Earned Income × Phase-In Rate
The credit increases with each dollar of earned income up to the maximum.
Plateau (maximum credit range)
Full credit between phase-in completion and phase-out start thresholds.
Phase-Out (credit reduces at higher income)
Credit = Maximum − (Income − Phase-Out Start) × Phase-Out Rate
Credit reaches $0 at the phase-out limit.
