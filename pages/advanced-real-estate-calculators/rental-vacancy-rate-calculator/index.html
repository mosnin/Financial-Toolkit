Vacancy Rate Formula

Vacancy Rate
Vacancy Rate = (Vacant Units / Total Units) x 100

Income loss is calculated by multiplying the number of vacant units by the average rent per unit over the time period.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter total units - the number of rental units in your property.
  2. Enter occupied units - the number of currently rented units.
  3. Enter average monthly rent - the average rent per unit.
  4. Enter the time period - how many months to calculate loss over.
  5. Click "Calculate Vacancy" to see your vacancy rate and income loss.

Example Scenario

A 10-unit property with 9 occupied units and $1,200 average rent has a 10% vacancy rate. The monthly income loss is $1,200 from the one vacant unit, totaling $14,400 over a 12-month period.

Frequently Asked Questions

A vacancy rate below 5-7% is generally considered healthy. Rates above 10% may indicate pricing issues, poor location, or property condition problems.

Vacancy rate is the number of vacant units divided by total units, multiplied by 100. It can also be calculated based on time: vacant days divided by total days in the period.

Each vacant unit represents lost rental income. A 10% vacancy rate on a property generating $10,000/month in potential rent means $1,000/month in lost income.

Reduce vacancy by pricing competitively, maintaining the property well, responding quickly to tenant needs, marketing effectively, and offering lease renewals to good tenants.
