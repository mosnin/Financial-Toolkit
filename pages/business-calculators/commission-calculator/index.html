Effective Rate

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your total sales amount for the period.
  2. Set your commission rate as a percentage.
  3. Optionally enter a base salary to see total compensation.
  4. Toggle tiered rates if your commission structure has multiple tiers.
  5. Click Calculate Commission to see your results.

Example Scenario

A sales representative earns a $40,000 base salary plus a tiered commission: 5% on the first $50,000 in sales, 8% on sales between $50,001 and $100,000, and 12% on anything above $100,000.

If they close $150,000 in sales for the year:

  • Tier 1: $50,000 x 5% = $2,500
  • Tier 2: $50,000 x 8% = $4,000
  • Tier 3: $50,000 x 12% = $6,000
  • Total Commission: $12,500
  • Total Earnings: $40,000 + $12,500 = $52,500
  • Effective Commission Rate: $12,500 / $150,000 = 8.33%

Frequently Asked Questions

Sales commission is calculated by multiplying total sales by the commission rate percentage. For example, $100,000 in sales at a 5% rate yields $5,000 in commission.

A tiered commission structure pays different commission rates at different sales levels. For example, 5% on the first $50,000, 7% on $50,001-$100,000, and 10% above $100,000. This incentivizes higher sales performance.

Average sales commission rates typically range from 5% to 15% depending on the industry. SaaS and software sales often have higher rates (10-20%), while retail may be lower (1-5%).

The effective commission rate is your total commission divided by total sales, expressed as a percentage. In tiered structures, this rate differs from individual tier rates and represents the blended rate across all tiers.

Base salary is the guaranteed portion of compensation paid regardless of sales performance. Total earnings equal the base salary plus any commission earned from sales.
