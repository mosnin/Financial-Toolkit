IRS Penalty Formulas

Failure to File (Late Filing)
5% of unpaid tax per month (or partial month) late, maximum 25% of unpaid tax.
Minimum penalty (if >60 days late): lesser of $485 or 100% of unpaid tax.
Failure to Pay (Late Payment)
0.5% of unpaid tax per month (or partial month) late, maximum 25% of unpaid tax.
Rate increases to 1% per month if taxes remain unpaid 10 days after IRS notice.
Underpayment of Estimated Tax
Penalty = Underpaid Amount × (IRS Rate / 100) × (Days / 365)
Applies quarterly to each period of underpayment.
Interest on Unpaid Tax
Interest = Unpaid Tax × (IRS Rate / 100) × (Months / 12)
Interest compounds daily. Current rate ≈ federal short-term rate + 3%.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Select the penalty type that applies to your situation.
  2. Enter the unpaid or underpaid tax amount.
  3. Enter the number of months the amount has been late or unpaid.
  4. Enter the current IRS underpayment rate (check irs.gov for current rate).
  5. Click Calculate Penalty.

Example Scenario

A taxpayer owes $8,000 in taxes and files 4 months late without paying:

  • Late Filing Penalty: $8,000 × 5% × 4 months = $1,600
  • Late Payment Penalty: $8,000 × 0.5% × 4 months = $160 (reduces late filing penalty)
  • Combined Penalty: $1,600 (late filing net) + $160 (late payment) = but capped; effectively $1,600
  • Interest (8% annual): $8,000 × 8% × (4/12) = $213
  • Total Owed: $8,000 + $1,600 + $213 = ~$9,813

Frequently Asked Questions

The IRS failure-to-file penalty is 5% of unpaid tax per month (or partial month) the return is late, up to 25% of the unpaid tax. If you file more than 60 days late, the minimum penalty is $485 (2026) or 100% of unpaid tax, whichever is less.

The failure-to-pay penalty is 0.5% per month of unpaid tax, up to 25%. When both penalties apply in the same month, the failure-to-file penalty is reduced by the failure-to-pay amount.

The underpayment penalty applies when quarterly payments are less than required. The penalty rate is the IRS federal short-term rate plus 3 percentage points, applied to the underpaid amount for the underpayment period.

File on time or request an extension. Pay at least the minimum amount due by the deadline. For estimated taxes, meet the safe harbor rule: pay 90% of current year tax or 100%/110% of prior year tax in quarterly installments.
