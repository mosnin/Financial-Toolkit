How the Calculation Works

This calculator uses a four-step process to evaluate your side hustle's true profitability:

Gross Profit
Gross Profit = Monthly Revenue - COGS - (Revenue x Platform Fee %)
Profit Margin
Profit Margin = (Gross Profit / Monthly Revenue) x 100
Net Profit After Time Cost
Net Profit = Gross Profit - (Hours/Month x Hourly Opportunity Cost)
Effective Hourly Rate
Effective Hourly Rate = Gross Profit / Hours per Month

The "Worth It" verdict compares your effective hourly rate to your opportunity cost. If your effective rate exceeds your opportunity cost, the hustle is financially favorable.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter monthly revenue — your total earnings from the side hustle before any deductions.
  2. Enter COGS — all direct product or supply costs for the month.
  3. Enter platform fees — the percentage taken by the marketplace or payment processor (e.g., Etsy, Shopify, PayPal).
  4. Enter hours per month — every hour spent including production, admin, shipping, and marketing.
  5. Enter your opportunity cost — what you could realistically earn per hour in an alternative activity.
  6. Click Calculate and review your gross profit, net profit, effective hourly rate, and verdict.

Example Scenario

Sarah sells handmade candles online with $2,500 monthly revenue, $800 in materials (COGS), a 5% platform fee, and spends 40 hours/month. Her alternative opportunity is $25/hour.

  • Platform fees: $2,500 x 5% = $125
  • Gross Profit: $2,500 - $800 - $125 = $1,575
  • Profit Margin: ($1,575 / $2,500) x 100 = 63%
  • Time cost: 40 hrs x $25 = $1,000
  • Net Profit After Time Cost: $1,575 - $1,000 = $575
  • Effective Hourly Rate: $1,575 / 40 = $39.38/hr

Since the effective hourly rate of $39.38 exceeds the $25 opportunity cost, this side hustle is financially worth it.
