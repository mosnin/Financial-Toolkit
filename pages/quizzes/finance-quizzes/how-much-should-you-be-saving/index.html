Frequently Asked Questions

In your 20s, saving 10–15% is a strong start. In your 30s, aim for 15–20% as retirement draws closer. By your 40s, 20–25% helps compensate for any earlier shortfalls. If you started late, 30%+ may be needed to reach your goals on time.

Higher income creates an opportunity to save more in absolute terms, but the percentage matters too. Lifestyle inflation — spending more as you earn more — is the biggest savings killer. Locking in a fixed savings rate before lifestyle creep sets in is essential.
