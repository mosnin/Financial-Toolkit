Frequently Asked Questions

Our calculators provide ballpark estimates based on industry averages. Actual costs vary by location, provider, and specific requirements. Use these estimates for initial budgeting and always get multiple quotes from actual service providers.

Compare multiple providers, book during off-peak times, bundle services when possible, consider partial DIY approaches, and negotiate prices. Getting at least three quotes helps ensure you get a fair price.

Consider the value of your time, the complexity of the task, and the risk of doing it wrong. Professional services save time and deliver better results for complex tasks, while simpler tasks may be worth doing yourself to save money.

Ask about licensing and insurance, total cost (including hidden fees), timeline, references, cancellation policy, guarantees, and what is included versus extra. Get everything in writing before work begins.

Start with our cost calculator for an initial estimate, then add 10-20% as a contingency buffer. Research average costs in your area, read reviews to understand what to expect, and get written quotes from at least three providers before committing.
