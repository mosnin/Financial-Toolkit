Comparison Formula

Each quote is evaluated using total annual cost and cost efficiency:

Total Annual Cost
Total Annual Cost = Annual Premium + Deductible (expected per claim event)
Cost per $1,000 Coverage
Cost per $1K = Annual Premium / (Coverage Amount / $1,000)

The best value is the quote with the lowest cost per $1,000 of coverage, balancing premium cost with the amount of protection provided.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter Quote 1 - annual premium, deductible, and coverage amount.
  2. Enter Quote 2 - same details for the second quote.
  3. Optionally enter Quote 3 - for a third comparison.
  4. Click "Compare Quotes" to see total costs and the best value option.

Example Scenario

Quote 1: $1,200/year premium, $500 deductible, $300,000 coverage. Quote 2: $1,000/year premium, $1,000 deductible, $250,000 coverage. Quote 1 costs $4.00 per $1K coverage while Quote 2 costs $4.00 per $1K - but Quote 1 offers $50,000 more coverage with a lower deductible, making it the better overall value.
