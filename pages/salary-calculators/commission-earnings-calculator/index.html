Commission Calculation Formula

For a flat commission rate:

Total Earnings Formula
Total Earnings = Base Salary + (Sales Amount x Commission Rate)

For tiered commissions, sales within each tier are multiplied by that tier's rate, and the results are summed.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your base salary - your guaranteed pay per period (0 for straight commission).
  2. Enter total sales amount - the dollar value of your sales.
  3. Enter the commission rate - your percentage commission on sales.
  4. Optionally enter Tier 2 details - a threshold and higher rate for sales above that amount.
  5. Click "Calculate Earnings" to see your total compensation breakdown.

Example Scenario

A sales rep with a $3,000 base salary sells $100,000 in products. With a flat 5% commission, they earn $5,000 in commission for total earnings of $8,000. With a tiered structure (5% on first $50,000, 8% on the rest), commission would be $6,500 for total earnings of $9,500.

Frequently Asked Questions

Sales commission is calculated by multiplying your total sales amount by the commission rate. For example, $100,000 in sales at 5% = $5,000 commission.

A tiered commission structure pays different rates based on sales volume. Higher tiers reward higher performance with increased commission percentages.

Base plus commission means you earn a guaranteed base salary plus commissions on sales. Straight commission means your entire income comes from commissions with no base salary guarantee.

Commission rates vary widely: real estate 2.5-3%, SaaS sales 5-10%, insurance 5-20%, retail 1-10%. Rates depend on the product and industry.
