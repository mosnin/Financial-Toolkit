Is Your Side Hustle Worth It?

This 8-question quiz evaluates your side hustle across the dimensions that truly matter — net profit, effective hourly rate, growth trajectory, enjoyment, scalability, and opportunity cost. Answer honestly and get a clear verdict: keep going, pivot, or stop.

Frequently Asked Questions

Divide your monthly net profit (revenue minus all expenses including taxes) by the total hours you spend on the hustle each month. For example, $400 net profit on 20 hours per month equals $20/hr. Compare this against your day job rate or what you could earn doing something else with those hours.

Opportunity cost is what you give up by spending time on your side hustle rather than on another activity — including rest, family time, a second job, or developing different skills. A side hustle that pays $10/hr may not be worth it if you could be earning $30/hr elsewhere or recovering energy for your main career.

Consider pivoting or stopping if: your effective hourly rate is below minimum wage after 6+ months of consistent effort, the hustle is stagnant or declining with no clear path to growth, it negatively impacts your health or relationships, or your skills are more valuable elsewhere. A pivot means changing your model or niche — not necessarily quitting entirely.
