Should You Rent or Buy?

The rent vs. buy debate doesn't have a universal answer — it depends on your market, your timeline, your finances, and your lifestyle priorities. This 8-question quiz weighs all of those factors, from the local price-to-rent ratio and your financial readiness to your flexibility needs and long-term goals, to help you make the decision that genuinely makes sense for your situation.

Frequently Asked Questions

The price-to-rent ratio divides a home's purchase price by annual rent for a comparable property. A ratio below 15 generally favours buying; 15–20 is neutral; above 20 tilts toward renting. In high-cost cities like San Francisco or New York, ratios often exceed 30, meaning renting is usually the better financial choice unless you plan to stay for a very long time.

Most financial models show that buying starts to beat renting financially after 4–7 years, accounting for transaction costs (typically 8–10% of the home's value to buy and sell). In high price-to-rent markets, the breakeven point can stretch to 10+ years. The shorter your expected stay, the stronger the case for renting.

Renting is the smarter financial choice in many markets and life situations.
