Depreciation Methods Explained

Straight-Line
Annual Depreciation = (Cost − Salvage Value) / Useful Life
Equal deduction every year for the asset's useful life.
MACRS (200% Declining Balance)
Year 1 Rate = 2 / Useful Life (with half-year convention)
Subsequent years: (2 / Useful Life) × Remaining Book Value
Switches to straight-line when SL gives larger deduction.
Section 179
Full asset cost deducted in Year 1 (up to annual limit ~$1.16M).
Best for maximizing immediate tax savings.
Annual Tax Savings
Tax Savings = Annual Depreciation × Marginal Tax Rate

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the asset cost (purchase price including installation).
  2. Enter the salvage value (estimated residual value at end of life; often $0 for MACRS).
  3. Enter the useful life in years (IRS MACRS classes: 3, 5, 7, 10, 15, 20 years).
  4. Select the depreciation method.
  5. Enter your marginal tax rate to calculate annual tax savings.

Example Scenario

A business purchases a $50,000 computer system with a $5,000 salvage value and 5-year useful life. Marginal tax rate: 24%.

  • Straight-Line: ($50,000 − $5,000) / 5 = $9,000/yr → Tax savings: $9,000 × 24% = $2,160/yr
  • MACRS 5-year: Year 1: 20% × $50,000 = $10,000 → Tax savings: $2,400 in year 1
  • Section 179: Deduct full $50,000 in Year 1 → Tax savings: $12,000 immediately

Frequently Asked Questions

Section 179 allows businesses to deduct the full cost of qualifying equipment or software in the year it is placed in service, up to $1,160,000 (indexed annually), rather than depreciating over multiple years.

MACRS (Modified Accelerated Cost Recovery System) is the depreciation method required by the IRS for most business assets. It uses accelerated depreciation schedules (3, 5, 7, 10, 15, 20 years depending on asset type).

Straight-line depreciation spreads the asset cost evenly over its useful life: Annual Depreciation = (Cost − Salvage Value) / Useful Life. It produces equal deductions each year.

Depreciation is a non-cash deduction that reduces taxable income. The tax savings each year equal the depreciation amount multiplied by your marginal tax rate.
