Real estate investment readiness requires solid personal finances first — no high-interest debt, an emergency fund, and at least a 15% to 25% down payment for an investment property. You also need to understand local market dynamics, vacancy rates, and expected cash flow. Our quiz evaluates all these factors.

Refinancing makes financial sense when you can lower your interest rate by at least 0.5%, plan to stay in the home long enough to recoup closing costs, want to switch from an adjustable to a fixed rate, or want to access home equity. Our quiz will help you gauge whether now is the right time.
