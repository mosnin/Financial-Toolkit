Renters Insurance Premium Formula

Renters insurance premiums are based on the amount of personal property coverage, liability limits, and your chosen deductible:

Premium Estimation Formula
Annual Premium = Base Rate + (Property Value / 1,000 x Rate) + Liability Adder - Deductible Discount

The base rate covers standard coverage minimums. Additional personal property value increases the premium proportionally. Higher liability limits add a modest cost, and a higher deductible provides a discount.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter personal property value - the total replacement cost of all your belongings.
  2. Select liability coverage - the amount of liability protection you want.
  3. Select your deductible - the amount you would pay out of pocket per claim.
  4. Click "Calculate Premium" to see your estimated annual and monthly costs.

Example Scenario

A renter with $30,000 in personal property who selects $100,000 in liability coverage and a $500 deductible would pay approximately $216 per year, or about $18 per month. This affordable coverage protects against theft, fire, water damage, and personal liability.

Frequently Asked Questions

Renters insurance typically costs between $15-$30 per month, or $180-$360 annually. The exact cost depends on your personal property value, liability coverage, deductible, and location.

Renters insurance covers personal property, liability if someone is injured in your rental, and additional living expenses if your rental becomes uninhabitable. It does not cover the building structure.

Create a home inventory of your belongings and estimate their total replacement cost. Most renters have $20,000-$50,000 in personal property including electronics, furniture, clothing, and kitchen items.

While not legally required in most places, many landlords require renters insurance as part of the lease. Even if not required, it is highly recommended to protect your personal belongings.
