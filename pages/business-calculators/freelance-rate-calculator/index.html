Freelance Rate Formula

Annual Revenue Needed
Revenue = (Desired Income + Expenses) / (1 - Tax Rate)
Hourly Rate
Hourly Rate = Annual Revenue / (Billable Hours/Week x Weeks/Year)
Day Rate
Day Rate = Hourly Rate x 8

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your desired annual take-home income (what you want to earn after taxes and expenses).
  2. Set your billable hours per week. Be realistic -- most freelancers bill 20-30 hours per week.
  3. Enter the number of weeks you will work per year (accounting for vacation and holidays).
  4. Add your estimated annual business expenses (tools, software, insurance, etc.).
  5. Set your estimated combined tax rate (self-employment + income tax).
  6. Click Calculate Rate to see your minimum hourly rate, day rate, and monthly revenue target.

Example Scenario

A freelance web developer wants to take home $80,000 per year. They estimate 25 billable hours per week, 48 working weeks, $12,000 in annual expenses, and a 30% tax rate.

  • Annual Revenue Needed: ($80,000 + $12,000) / (1 - 0.30) = $131,429
  • Total Billable Hours: 25 x 48 = 1,200 hours
  • Minimum Hourly Rate: $131,429 / 1,200 = $109.52/hr
  • Day Rate: $109.52 x 8 = $876.19/day
  • Monthly Target: $131,429 / 12 = $10,952/month
