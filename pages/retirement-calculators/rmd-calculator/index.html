RMD Formula

The IRS requires you to divide your account balance by a life expectancy factor:

RMD Formula
RMD = Account Balance / Distribution Period (from IRS Uniform Lifetime Table)

The distribution period decreases as you age, meaning you must withdraw a larger percentage each year. The IRS Uniform Lifetime Table provides the factor for each age starting at 72.

How to Use This RMD Calculator

  1. Enter your account balance - the total value as of December 31 of the previous year.
  2. Enter your age - your age during the current distribution year.
  3. Select the account type - the type of retirement account.
  4. Click "Calculate RMD" to see your required minimum distribution amount.

Example Scenario

A 75-year-old with a traditional IRA balance of $500,000 as of December 31 would use a distribution period of 24.6 years. Their RMD would be $500,000 / 24.6 = $20,325 for the year, or approximately $1,694 per month.
