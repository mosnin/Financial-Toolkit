Raise Percentage Formula

The raise percentage is calculated as:

Raise Percentage Formula
Raise % = ((New Salary - Current Salary) / Current Salary) x 100

To calculate a new salary from a percentage: New Salary = Current Salary x (1 + Raise% / 100).

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your current salary - your annual pay before the raise.
  2. Enter your new salary OR raise percentage - provide one and the calculator determines the other.
  3. Click "Calculate Raise" to see the full breakdown of your raise.

Example Scenario

If your current salary is $60,000 and you receive a 10% raise, your new salary is $66,000. That is a $6,000 annual increase, $500 more per month, and $230.77 more per biweekly paycheck.
