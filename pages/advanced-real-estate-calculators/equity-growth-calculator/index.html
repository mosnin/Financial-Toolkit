Equity Growth Formula

Home equity grows through two channels: appreciation and principal reduction.

Equity Calculation
Equity = (Home Value x (1 + r)^n) - (Loan Balance - Total Principal Paid)

Where r is the annual appreciation rate and n is the number of years.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your current home value - the estimated market value today.
  2. Enter your loan balance - the remaining mortgage principal.
  3. Enter the appreciation rate - expected annual home value increase.
  4. Enter annual principal payments - the amount of principal paid each year.
  5. Enter years to project - how far into the future to calculate.
  6. Click "Calculate Equity Growth" to see your projected equity.

Example Scenario

A $350,000 home with a $280,000 loan balance, 3.5% appreciation, and $8,000 in annual principal payments would have approximately $227,000 in equity after 10 years. The home would be worth about $493,000 with a remaining balance of $200,000.
