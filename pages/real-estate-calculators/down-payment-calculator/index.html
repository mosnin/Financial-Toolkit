Understanding Down Payments

Your down payment is the upfront cash you pay toward the purchase price. The remainder is financed through your mortgage. Larger down payments mean:

  • Lower monthly mortgage payments
  • Less interest paid over the life of the loan
  • No PMI requirement (at 20%+)
  • More home equity from day one
  • Stronger offer position in competitive markets

Minimum Down Payments by Loan Type

  • Conventional: 3% minimum (but 20% to avoid PMI)
  • FHA: 3.5% (with 580+ credit score) or 10% (500–579)
  • VA: 0% for qualifying veterans and active-duty service members
  • USDA: 0% for qualifying rural and suburban properties

Example Scenario

Jessica is targeting a $400,000 home and wants to put 20% down ($80,000) to avoid PMI. She currently has $30,000 saved and can save $1,500/month in a 4.5% APY high-yield savings account.

  • Down payment needed: $80,000
  • Savings gap: $50,000
  • With $1,500/month + 4.5% interest: approximately 31 months
  • Loan amount: $320,000
  • PMI avoided: saves roughly $133/month
