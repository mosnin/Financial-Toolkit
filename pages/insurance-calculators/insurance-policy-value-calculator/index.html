Policy Value Formulas

Policy value is assessed using several metrics:

Cost per $1,000 Coverage
Cost per $1K = Annual Premium / (Coverage Amount / $1,000)
Net Cost
Net Cost = (Annual Premium x Years Held) - Total Claims Received
Effective Value Ratio
Value Ratio = Coverage Amount / Annual Premium

A higher value ratio means more coverage per premium dollar. Net cost reflects the true out-of-pocket cost after accounting for claims received.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter annual premium - what you pay per year for the policy.
  2. Enter coverage amount - the maximum the policy will pay.
  3. Enter deductible - your out-of-pocket amount per claim.
  4. Enter total claims received - total payouts you have received from this policy.
  5. Enter years held - how long you have had this policy.
  6. Click "Analyze Policy" to see your policy's value metrics.

Example Scenario

A homeowner paying $1,500/year for $300,000 in coverage with a $1,000 deductible over 5 years has paid $7,500 total. If they received $5,000 in claims, their net cost is $2,500. The cost per $1K of coverage is $5.00/year, and the value ratio is 200:1, meaning they get $200 of coverage for every $1 in premium.
