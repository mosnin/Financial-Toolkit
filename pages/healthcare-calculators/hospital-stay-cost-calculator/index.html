How Hospital Cost Calculations Work

This calculator builds the total estimated cost from room charges plus procedure fees, then splits the bill between you and your insurer:

Estimated Total Hospital Cost
Total = (Daily Room Rate × Days) + Procedure Cost
Your Share
Your Share = Remaining Deductible + (Total - Deductible) × (Coinsurance % / 100)
Insurance Pays
Insurance = Total - Your Share

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter estimated days - use your doctor's estimate or an average for your procedure type.
  2. Select ward type - general ward for standard admissions; ICU for critical care stays.
  3. Select procedure category - choose the type of procedure to add the associated surgical fees.
  4. Enter your remaining deductible - how much of your annual deductible you still owe.
  5. Enter your coinsurance - the percentage you pay after meeting your deductible (commonly 20%).
  6. Click Calculate to see total cost, your share, and insurance share.

Example Scenario

A patient has a planned knee replacement surgery. The estimated stay is 2 days in a general ward. Total estimated cost: (2 days × $2,800) + $25,000 surgery = $30,600. They have $1,500 deductible remaining and 20% coinsurance. Their share: $1,500 (deductible) + 20% of $29,100 = $1,500 + $5,820 = $7,320. Their insurance covers the remaining $23,280. Understanding this in advance helps with financial planning and ensures you're not caught off guard.

Frequently Asked Questions

The average cost of a hospital stay in the US is approximately $2,500-$3,500 per day for a general medical admission and $5,000-$10,000+ per day for ICU care. Surgical procedures dramatically increase total costs and vary widely by hospital, region, and complexity.

After you meet your annual deductible, coinsurance is the percentage of costs you share with your insurer. With 20% coinsurance, you pay 20% of each covered bill and insurance pays 80%, until you reach your annual out-of-pocket maximum.
