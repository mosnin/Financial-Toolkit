Take-Home Pay Formula

Your take-home pay accounts for pre-tax deductions before calculating taxes:

Net Pay Formula
Net Pay = Gross Salary - Pre-Tax Deductions - Federal Tax - State Tax - FICA

Pre-tax deductions reduce your taxable income, so you pay less in federal and state taxes. FICA is calculated on gross pay before pre-tax deductions.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your annual gross salary - total compensation before any deductions.
  2. Enter pre-tax deductions - annual 401(k), health insurance, HSA, and FSA contributions.
  3. Select your filing status - single, married filing jointly, or head of household.
  4. Enter your state tax rate - use 0% for no-income-tax states.
  5. Click "Calculate Take-Home Pay" to see your complete pay breakdown.

Example Scenario

With an $80,000 salary, $6,000 in pre-tax deductions (401k + health insurance), filing as single in a 5% state tax state, your taxable income is $74,000. After approximately $8,600 in federal tax, $4,000 state tax, and $6,120 FICA, your take-home pay would be approximately $55,280 annually or $4,607 per month.
