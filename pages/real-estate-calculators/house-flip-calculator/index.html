House Flip Formulas

Profit Calculation
Total Investment = Purchase Price + Repair Costs + Holding Costs Commission = Selling Price x (Agent Commission % / 100) Selling Costs = Commission + Closing Costs Gross Profit = Selling Price - Purchase Price - Repair Costs Net Profit = Selling Price - Total Investment - Selling Costs ROI = (Net Profit / Total Investment) x 100

The total investment represents all money spent before the sale. Selling costs are deducted from proceeds to calculate your true net profit.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the purchase price -- what you paid or plan to pay for the property.
  2. Enter repair costs -- get detailed contractor bids and add a 10-20% contingency buffer.
  3. Enter holding costs -- the total of mortgage, taxes, insurance, and utilities during the renovation and selling period.
  4. Enter the expected selling price (ARV) -- the after-repair value based on comparable sales.
  5. Enter agent commission rate -- typically 5-6% of the selling price.
  6. Enter closing costs for the sale -- title, escrow, and transfer fees.

Example Scenario

Buying at $200K, Selling at $320K

Purchase price: $200,000. Repairs: $50,000. Holding costs (6 months): $12,000. Total investment: $262,000.

Selling price: $320,000. Agent commission (6%): $19,200. Closing costs: $5,000. Total selling costs: $24,200.

Net profit: $320,000 - $262,000 - $24,200 = $33,800. ROI: $33,800 / $262,000 = 12.90%.

Frequently Asked Questions

The 70% rule states that investors should pay no more than 70% of a property's after-repair value (ARV) minus repair costs. For example, if ARV is $300,000 and repairs cost $40,000, the maximum purchase price should be $300,000 x 0.70 - $40,000 = $170,000.

Holding costs include mortgage payments, property taxes, insurance, utilities, and HOA fees during the renovation and selling period. These typically run $1,500-$4,000 per month depending on the property and location.

Real estate agent commissions typically total 5-6% of the selling price, split between the listing agent and buyer's agent. Some investors sell without an agent (FSBO) to save on commission but may take longer to sell.

Most experienced flippers target a minimum net profit of 10-20% ROI. In dollar terms, many aim for at least $25,000-$50,000 per flip to justify the time, risk, and capital involved.
