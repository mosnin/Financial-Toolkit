Insulation Cost Formula

Insulation costs are calculated based on area, material type, and desired R-value:

Cost Formula
Total Cost = Area x (Material Cost/sqft + Labor Cost/sqft) x R-Value Factor

Base material costs per sqft: fiberglass $0.50-$1.00, blown-in $0.75-$1.50, spray foam $1.50-$3.50. Higher R-values require more material, increasing costs proportionally.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the area - total square footage to be insulated.
  2. Select insulation type - fiberglass batts, spray foam, or blown-in cellulose.
  3. Choose target R-value - based on your location and application.
  4. Click "Calculate Cost" to see material, labor, and total costs.

Example Scenario

Insulating a 1,500-square-foot attic with blown-in cellulose to R-38 would cost approximately $3,400. Material costs come to about $1,700 and labor adds about $1,700. The cost per square foot is approximately $2.25.

Frequently Asked Questions

Insulation costs per square foot installed: fiberglass batts $0.50-$1.50, blown-in cellulose $1.00-$2.50, and spray foam $1.50-$5.00. Costs increase with higher R-values.

Attics typically need R-38 to R-60, walls R-13 to R-23, and floors R-13 to R-30. Check local building codes for minimum requirements in your area.

Fiberglass batts are best for standard wall cavities. Blown-in is ideal for attics and retrofitting. Spray foam provides the best air sealing and highest R-value per inch.

Proper insulation can reduce heating and cooling costs by 15-30%. The EPA estimates average savings of 15% on heating and cooling costs. Payback period is typically 2-5 years.

Fiberglass batts and blown-in insulation can be DIY projects with proper safety equipment. Spray foam should be installed by professionals due to chemical handling and specialized equipment.
