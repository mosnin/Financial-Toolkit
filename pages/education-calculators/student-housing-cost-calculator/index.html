How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter on-campus room and board - use the annual cost from your school's published rates (this usually includes a meal plan).
  2. Enter monthly off-campus rent - your share if splitting with roommates.
  3. Enter utilities - electricity, gas, water, and internet split among roommates.
  4. Enter commute costs - monthly bus pass, gas, or parking.
  5. Enter monthly groceries - what you'd spend on food off campus instead of a meal plan.
  6. Click Calculate to see the true annual and 4-year comparison.

Example Scenario

A university charges $13,500 per year for on-campus room and board. Off-campus, a student splits a 3-bedroom apartment paying $750/month in rent, $120/month in utilities, $80/month for a bus pass, and $300/month on groceries — totaling $1,250/month or $15,000 annually. In this case, on-campus is actually $1,500 cheaper per year and $6,000 cheaper over four years — and that includes meals. The calculation completely changes if the student splits with one more roommate and reduces rent to $550/month.

Frequently Asked Questions

It depends heavily on location and the roommate situation. On-campus costs are all-inclusive but often non-negotiable. Off-campus can be cheaper when shared with multiple roommates, but you must add utilities, commuting costs, and groceries. This calculator helps you compare the true total cost.

Beyond base rent, include utilities (electric, gas, water, internet), renter's insurance, commuting costs (gas, bus pass, parking), laundry, and groceries instead of a meal plan. These extras can add $300-$700/month to off-campus living costs.

Most on-campus room and board packages include a required meal plan. Make sure your on-campus cost reflects the full room-and-board total, and enter your estimated monthly grocery cost for the off-campus option to ensure a fair comparison.

On-campus housing typically covers only the academic year (8-9 months). Off-campus leases usually run 12 months. Enter your actual annual cost for each option — the actual invoice amount for on-campus, and monthly off-campus costs multiplied by 12 months.
