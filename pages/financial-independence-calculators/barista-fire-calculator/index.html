Barista FIRE Calculator

Barista FIRE lets you retire early from your career with a smaller portfolio by supplementing investment income with part-time work. Your portfolio only needs to cover the gap between your retirement expenses and your part-time income. This calculator finds your Barista FIRE number, your timeline to reach it, and exactly what percentage of your expenses your part-time income covers — making early retirement achievable years sooner than standard FIRE.

How Barista FIRE Is Calculated

Formula
Gap Expenses = Annual Expenses − Annual Part-Time Income
Barista FIRE Number = Gap Expenses ÷ 0.04 (4% SWR)

Part-Time Coverage = Part-Time Income ÷ Annual Expenses × 100%
Savings vs Standard = Standard FIRE − Barista FIRE Number

Standard FIRE = Annual Expenses ÷ 0.04

Because your part-time income covers a portion of expenses, your portfolio only needs to fund the remaining gap. The smaller portfolio requirement is calculated by dividing that gap by the safe withdrawal rate. The difference between standard FIRE and Barista FIRE is the amount of capital you avoid needing to accumulate — potentially saving years of work.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your retirement expenses: Total annual spending in retirement including all essential and discretionary costs.
  2. Set your part-time income: Realistic annual income from a relaxed part-time role — barista, consultant, freelancer, seasonal worker.
  3. Add current savings and monthly contributions: All investment accounts combined.
  4. Review results: See your Barista FIRE number, timeline, and how many dollars you avoid accumulating versus standard FIRE.

Example Scenario

Sam is 36 with $200,000 saved, contributing $2,500/month. Annual retirement expenses: $55,000. Part-time income (part-time consulting): $22,000/year. Gap expenses: $33,000. Barista FIRE number: $825,000. Standard FIRE would require $1,375,000 — so Barista FIRE saves $550,000 in required savings. At 7% returns, Sam reaches Barista FIRE in approximately 10 years 4 months (age 46) versus an estimated 18+ years for standard FIRE. Part-time income covers 40% of total expenses.

Frequently Asked Questions

Barista FIRE is semi-retirement using a smaller portfolio supplemented by part-time income. The portfolio only needs to cover expenses beyond your part-time earnings, so the required savings are much lower than standard FIRE — enabling earlier career departure.

Barista FIRE Number = (Annual Expenses − Part-Time Income) ÷ 0.04. The portfolio only funds the expense gap. The more income you earn part-time, the smaller the portfolio needed — and the sooner you can leave full-time work.

Any flexible, lower-pressure income: part-time retail, food service, freelancing, consulting, seasonal work, or passion-based side businesses. Many people choose roles offering health benefits, social connection, and structure without career pressure.

Part-time income is typically planned for the early semi-retirement phase, often until Social Security eligibility or 59½ for penalty-free retirement account access. As your portfolio grows and these income sources activate, you can fully retire. Always plan for a transition to full portfolio funding eventually.
