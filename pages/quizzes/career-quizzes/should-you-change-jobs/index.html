Should You Change Jobs?

Changing jobs is one of the most impactful financial decisions you can make — but timing and preparation matter enormously. This quiz looks beyond gut feelings to evaluate your compensation gap, growth trajectory, job satisfaction, financial cushion, and risk exposure. Answer 9 questions to get a clear recommendation.

Frequently Asked Questions

On average, job changers see salary increases of 10–20% compared to 3–5% for internal raises. In competitive industries like tech, finance, or healthcare, increases of 20–30% are not unusual. Use a job offer comparison calculator to evaluate the full financial picture including benefits.

If you plan to job search while employed, you need minimal cash cushion — just time. If you plan to quit first, have at least 3–6 months of expenses saved, preferably 6–12 in an uncertain market. Factor in COBRA health insurance costs, which can run $500–$700/month.

Compare total compensation including base salary, bonus, equity, benefits, retirement match, PTO, and remote work savings. A higher base salary with worse benefits can net out to less. Use a job offer comparison calculator to put a dollar value on every element of the offer.
