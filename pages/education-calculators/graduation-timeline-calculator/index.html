Graduation Timeline Formula

The calculation is straightforward: determine remaining credits and divide by your semester pace.

Credits Remaining
Credits Remaining = Total Required - Credits Completed
Semesters Remaining
Semesters = ⌈Credits Remaining / Credits Per Semester⌉
Years Remaining
Years = Semesters Remaining / Semesters Per Year

The graduation date is calculated by adding the years remaining to today's date and rounding to the nearest typical graduation month (May or December).

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter credits completed - check your official transcript or student portal.
  2. Enter credits required - your degree's total credit requirement (commonly 120 for a bachelor's).
  3. Enter credits per semester - your current or planned load (15 is full-time; 9-11 is part-time).
  4. Enter semesters per year - 2 for fall/spring only; 3 if you include summer.
  5. Click Calculate to see your graduation timeline and full-time vs part-time comparison.

Example Scenario

A student has completed 45 credits toward a 120-credit degree. Currently taking 12 credits per semester across 2 semesters per year, they have 75 credits remaining. At 12 credits/semester that is 7 semesters (ceiling of 75/12), or about 3.5 years. Switching to full-time at 15 credits reduces that to 5 semesters — a full year saved. Adding a summer session (3 semesters/year) at 15 credits per term could finish the degree in just under 2 years.
