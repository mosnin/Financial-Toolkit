Tax Estimation Formula

Net Self-Employment Income
Net Income = Gross Income - Business Expenses
Self-Employment Tax
SE Tax = Net Income × 0.9235 × 0.153
Adjusted Gross Income (AGI)
AGI = Net Income - (SE Tax × 0.5)
Federal Income Tax
Federal Tax = Apply 2025 tax brackets to (AGI - Standard Deduction)
Quarterly Estimated Payment
Quarterly = Total Annual Tax / 4

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your gross annual freelance income — the total you invoice clients before any deductions.
  2. Add your estimated business expenses to reduce your net taxable income.
  3. Select your filing status, which determines your standard deduction and tax brackets.
  4. Enter your state income tax rate (find your state's rate on your state revenue department website).
  5. Click Estimate Taxes to see your full tax breakdown and quarterly payment amount.

Example Scenario

A single freelance designer earns $90,000 gross with $12,000 in business expenses and lives in a state with a 5% income tax rate.

  • Net Income: $90,000 - $12,000 = $78,000
  • SE Tax: $78,000 × 0.9235 × 0.153 = $11,021
  • AGI: $78,000 - $5,511 (half SE) = $72,489
  • Taxable Income: $72,489 - $14,600 (standard deduction) = $57,889
  • Federal Income Tax (2025 brackets): approx. $8,226
  • State Tax: $78,000 × 5% = $3,900
  • Total Tax: $23,147 | Quarterly Payment: $5,787
