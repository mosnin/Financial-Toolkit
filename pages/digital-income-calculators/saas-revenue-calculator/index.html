SaaS Revenue Formulas

MRR
MRR = Paying Customers x Average MRR per Customer
ARR
ARR = MRR x 12
Customer LTV
LTV = Average MRR per Customer / (Churn Rate / 100)
Net MRR Growth
Net MRR Growth = (New Customer MRR + Expansion MRR) - Churn MRR
6-Month Forecast
Month N MRR = Previous MRR x (1 + Growth Rate + Expansion Rate - Churn Rate)

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your paying customers — active subscribers generating recurring revenue.
  2. Set your average MRR per customer — divide total MRR by number of customers for this figure.
  3. Enter your monthly churn rate — the percentage of customers who cancel each month.
  4. Set your monthly growth rate — new customer additions as a percentage of current count.
  5. Enter your expansion revenue rate — additional MRR from upgrades/upsells as a percentage of current MRR.
  6. Click Calculate SaaS Metrics for MRR, ARR, LTV, churn cost, net MRR growth, and a 6-month forecast.

Example Scenario

A B2B SaaS tool has 150 customers at $99/month average, 1.5% monthly churn, 8% monthly growth, and 2% expansion revenue.

  • MRR: 150 x $99 = $14,850
  • ARR: $14,850 x 12 = $178,200
  • Customer LTV: $99 / 0.015 = $6,600
  • Monthly Churn Cost: $14,850 x 1.5% = $223
  • New Customer MRR: $14,850 x 8% = $1,188
  • Expansion MRR: $14,850 x 2% = $297
  • Net MRR Growth: $1,188 + $297 - $223 = $1,262/month

Frequently Asked Questions

A healthy SaaS business targets an LTV:CAC ratio of at least 3:1. Ratios above 5:1 indicate strong unit economics, while below 1:1 means the business loses money on every customer acquired.

SaaS LTV is calculated as Average Revenue Per Customer divided by Monthly Churn Rate. For example, if a customer pays $100/month and churn is 2%, LTV = $100 / 0.02 = $5,000.

Expansion revenue is additional MRR from existing customers through upgrades, upsells, or add-ons. It can partially or fully offset churn, creating a "negative churn" scenario where expansion exceeds cancellation losses.

Net MRR growth is new MRR from new customers plus expansion MRR from existing customers minus churned MRR from cancellations. It represents the true monthly change in recurring revenue.
