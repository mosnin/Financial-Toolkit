Total Compensation Formula

Total compensation includes all components of a job offer:

Total Compensation Formula
Total Comp = Base Salary + Bonus + Benefits Value + 401(k) Match + PTO Value

Compare total compensation, not just base salary, to find the truly better offer.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter Offer A details - salary, bonus, benefits, 401(k) match, and PTO value.
  2. Enter Offer B details - same components for the second offer.
  3. Click "Compare Offers" to see the total compensation comparison and recommendation.

Example Scenario

Offer A: $85,000 salary + $5,000 bonus + $8,000 benefits + $3,000 401(k) + $5,000 PTO = $106,000 total. Offer B: $90,000 salary + $3,000 bonus + $6,000 benefits + $4,500 401(k) + $4,000 PTO = $107,500 total. Offer B wins by $1,500 despite the smaller bonus and benefits.
