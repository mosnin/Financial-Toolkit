Retirement Age Formula

The calculator uses the future value of a series formula to project your savings growth:

Future Value Formula
FV = PV(1 + r)^n + PMT[((1 + r)^n - 1) / r]

Where FV is the future value (target nest egg), PV is the present value (current savings), r is the monthly return rate, n is the number of months, and PMT is the monthly contribution. The calculator solves for n to find when FV reaches your target.

How to Use This Retirement Age Calculator

  1. Enter your current age - your age today.
  2. Enter your current savings - the total amount you have saved for retirement right now.
  3. Enter your monthly contribution - how much you add to retirement savings each month.
  4. Enter your target nest egg - the total amount you want to accumulate before retiring.
  5. Enter your expected annual return - the average annual investment return you expect.
  6. Click "Calculate Retirement Age" to see when you can retire.

Example Scenario
