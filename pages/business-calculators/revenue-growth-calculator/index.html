Revenue Growth Formulas

Total Growth Rate
Total Growth = ((Ending Revenue - Starting Revenue) / Starting Revenue) x 100
Monthly Growth Rate (Compound)
Monthly Rate = ((Ending / Starting) ^ (1 / Months) - 1) x 100
Annualized Growth Rate
Annual Rate = ((1 + Monthly Rate) ^ 12 - 1) x 100
Projected Next Period
Projected = Ending Revenue x (1 + Monthly Rate) ^ Months

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your starting revenue (beginning of the period).
  2. Enter your ending revenue (end of the period).
  3. Specify the time period in months between the two revenue figures.
  4. Click Calculate Growth to see total growth, monthly rate, annualized rate, and a projection for the next equivalent period.

Example Scenario

A SaaS startup grew from $50,000 MRR to $120,000 MRR over 18 months.

  • Total Growth: (($120,000 - $50,000) / $50,000) x 100 = 140%
  • Monthly Growth Rate: ($120,000 / $50,000) ^ (1/18) - 1 = 4.97%
  • Annualized Growth Rate: (1.0497)^12 - 1 = 79.0%
  • Projected Next 18 Months: $120,000 x (1.0497)^18 = $288,000

Frequently Asked Questions

Revenue growth rate is calculated as ((Ending Revenue - Starting Revenue) / Starting Revenue) x 100. For compound monthly growth, use the formula: ((Ending / Starting) ^ (1 / months) - 1) x 100.

A good revenue growth rate depends on company stage and industry. Early-stage startups may target 15-20% month-over-month, while established companies consider 10-20% year-over-year strong growth.

Total growth is the simple percentage change over the entire period. Annualized growth normalizes it to a yearly rate using compound growth, making it useful for comparing different time periods.

Monthly growth rate uses the compound growth formula: ((Ending / Starting) ^ (1 / months) - 1) x 100. This gives the average compounded monthly rate, not a simple average.
