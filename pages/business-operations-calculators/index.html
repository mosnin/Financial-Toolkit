Frequently Asked Questions

Gross profit margins vary widely by industry. Software companies often achieve 70%+ margins, while retail businesses typically run 20–50% and manufacturing 25–35%. What matters most is whether your gross margin is sufficient to cover operating expenses and generate net profit. Use our Gross Profit Calculator and Operating Margin Calculator to benchmark your performance.

Studies consistently show that replacing an employee costs 50% to 200% of their annual salary when you account for recruiting, interviewing, onboarding, training, and productivity loss during the transition. For senior roles the cost can be even higher. Our Employee Turnover Cost Calculator breaks down each component so you can build the business case for better retention programs.

Working capital equals current assets minus current liabilities and measures your ability to meet short-term obligations. Positive working capital means you can pay bills as they come due. Negative working capital signals potential liquidity problems. Use our Working Capital Calculator to monitor this metric and ensure your business stays financially healthy.

The cash conversion cycle (CCC) measures how many days it takes for a dollar invested in inventory to come back as cash from a customer sale. A shorter CCC means faster cash flow and better liquidity. Our Cash Conversion Cycle Calculator shows you exactly where your cash is tied up so you can reduce delays in your operating cycle.

Churn rate is calculated as the number of customers lost during a period divided by the number of customers at the start of that period, expressed as a percentage. For SaaS businesses, a monthly churn rate above 2% is generally considered high. Our Customer Churn Rate Calculator also shows the revenue impact of your churn and the lifetime value implication.
