Project Pricing Formula

Base Price
Base = Estimated Hours × Hourly Rate × Complexity Multiplier
Price with Overhead
With Overhead = Base Price + Overhead Allocation
Final Price (with Margin)
Final = (Base + Overhead) / (1 - Profit Margin %)
Rush Price
Rush Price = Final Price × (1 + Rush Fee %)
Effective Hourly Rate
Effective Rate = Final Price / Estimated Hours

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your estimated hours for the project and your standard hourly rate.
  2. Set the complexity multiplier — use 1.0 for routine work, higher for specialized or difficult projects.
  3. Enter a rush fee percentage to see what the project would cost on an accelerated timeline.
  4. Add your overhead allocation — the share of annual overhead this project should absorb.
  5. Set your profit margin above break-even to reward business risk and fuel growth.
  6. Click Calculate Project Price to see your complete pricing breakdown.

Example Scenario

A freelance developer estimates 40 hours for a web app, charges $100/hr, applies a 1.25 complexity multiplier, $500 overhead, and 20% profit margin.

  • Base Price: 40 × $100 × 1.25 = $5,000
  • With Overhead: $5,000 + $500 = $5,500
  • Final Price (20% margin): $5,500 / 0.80 = $6,875
  • Rush Price (25% fee): $6,875 × 1.25 = $8,594
  • Effective Hourly Rate: $6,875 / 40 = $171.88/hr

Frequently Asked Questions

A complexity multiplier accounts for difficulty beyond standard work. A value of 1.0 is baseline; 1.5 means 50% more complex. Factors include unfamiliar technology, tight timelines, stakeholder count, and level of required expertise.

Yes. Rush fees compensate for rearranging your schedule, overtime, and additional stress. A 25-50% rush fee is standard for projects requiring turnaround in under half the normal timeframe.

Overhead includes software, marketing, accounting, insurance, and non-billable admin time. Divide total annual overhead by estimated projects per year for a per-project allocation. Typically $200 to $1,500+ per project.

A profit margin rewards business risk and funds growth. Most freelancers target 15-30% above cost. High-value, strategic projects can command 40-50% margin especially with specialized expertise.
