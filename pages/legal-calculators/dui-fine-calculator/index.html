DUI Cost Breakdown

The total cost of a DUI is the sum of multiple expenses:

Total DUI Cost
Total = Fines + Court Fees + Attorney Fees + DUI School + License Fees + Interlock + Insurance Increase

Each component varies by offense number (penalties escalate with repeat offenses), BAC level (higher BAC means enhanced penalties), and state (each state has different fine schedules and requirements). Insurance increases alone can add thousands of dollars over the years following a conviction.

Example Scenario

A first-time DUI with a BAC of 0.12% in California might include approximately $1,800 in court fines, $2,500 in court and attorney fees, $2,000 for DUI school and license reinstatement, and $6,000 in increased insurance premiums over 3 years, for a total estimated cost of approximately $12,300. A second offense or higher BAC would significantly increase these amounts.

Frequently Asked Questions

The total cost of a DUI typically ranges from $10,000 to $25,000 for a first offense, including fines, court fees, attorney fees, DUI school, license reinstatement, ignition interlock device, and increased insurance premiums over several years. Second and third offenses can cost significantly more.

Car insurance typically increases 40-80% after a DUI conviction and the increase usually lasts 3-7 years depending on the state. On average, a DUI adds $1,500 to $3,000 per year to insurance premiums.

Penalties increase dramatically with each subsequent DUI offense. Second offenses typically carry higher fines, longer license suspensions, mandatory jail time, and longer DUI education programs. Third offenses in many states are felonies with potential prison time and much higher fines.
