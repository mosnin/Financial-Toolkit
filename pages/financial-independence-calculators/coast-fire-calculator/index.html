Coast FIRE Calculator

Coast FIRE is the milestone where your existing investments — without any new contributions — will compound to your full FI number by your target retirement age. This calculator finds your Coast FIRE number (the present value of your FI goal), tells you whether you've already coasted, and shows the monthly surplus you'd free up if you stopped contributing to retirement accounts today.

How the Coast FIRE Number Is Calculated

Formula
FI Number = Annual Expenses ÷ SWR
Coast FIRE Number = FI Number ÷ (1 + r)^years

Where: r = annual return rate, years = target age − current age

Coasted if: Current Savings ≥ Coast FIRE Number
Monthly Surplus = Monthly Savings (freed up if no longer contributing)

The Coast FIRE number is the present value of your full FI number discounted at your expected return rate. The longer your runway to retirement, the smaller your Coast FIRE number — because compounding has more time to work. Once your current savings exceeds this amount, you've officially coasted: no new retirement contributions are technically required.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your current and target retirement ages: Longer runway = smaller Coast FIRE number.
  2. Add your current savings and annual retirement expenses: These determine your full FI number target.
  3. Set return rate and SWR: 7% return and 4% SWR are standard starting points.
  4. Enter monthly savings (optional): Used to show the monthly surplus freed up if you stop contributing today.
  5. Review results: See your Coast FIRE number, whether you've coasted, and the gap or surplus.

Example Scenario

Taylor is 35 with $180,000 saved, planning to retire at 60 with $70,000/year in expenses. FI number: $1,750,000. Coast FIRE number: $1,750,000 / (1.07)^25 = approximately $321,000. Taylor's $180,000 hasn't reached the Coast FIRE number yet — a gap of $141,000. Once Taylor's savings reach $321,000, compounding alone will grow it to $1,750,000 by age 60. Currently contributing $2,500/month — that monthly surplus would be freed up upon hitting Coast FIRE.

Frequently Asked Questions

Coast FIRE means your portfolio is large enough that — with no new contributions — it will compound to your FI number by retirement age. You can "coast" by only covering living expenses without needing to save more for retirement.

Coast FIRE Number = FI Number ÷ (1 + return rate)^years remaining. The longer until retirement, the smaller the number, since compounding has more time to grow the investment. This is essentially the present value of your FI goal.

After Coast FIRE, you no longer need to invest for retirement. Options include transitioning to a lower-stress career, working part-time (Barista FIRE), traveling, pursuing passion projects, or simply spending your previous investment contributions on life experiences.

Coast FIRE is a portfolio milestone — savings sufficient for compounding to handle retirement. Barista FIRE is a lifestyle strategy — working part-time for income and benefits while a smaller portfolio grows. The two often overlap: coasting to a Barista FIRE lifestyle is a popular combination.
