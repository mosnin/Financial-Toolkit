How Therapy Cost Calculations Work

Your actual therapy cost depends on the coverage structure your plan uses:

With Flat Copay
Monthly Cost = Copay per Session × Sessions per Month
With Coinsurance
Monthly Cost = Session Cost × (Coinsurance % / 100) × Sessions per Month
Annual Insurance Savings
Savings = (Session Cost × Annual Sessions) - Annual Out-of-Pocket

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter sessions per month - weekly therapy = 4 sessions; biweekly = 2 sessions.
  2. Enter the session cost - your therapist's standard fee (before insurance adjustments).
  3. Select coverage type - check your insurance card or plan summary for copay vs coinsurance.
  4. Enter your copay or coinsurance - the amount per session you pay after your deductible is met.
  5. Click Calculate to see your monthly cost, annual total, and what insurance saves you.

Example Scenario

A person attends therapy weekly (4 sessions/month) at $160 per session. Their insurance plan covers mental health with a $35 copay per session. Monthly out-of-pocket: $35 × 4 = $140. Annual cost: $1,680. Without insurance at the full rate: $160 × 48 = $7,680. Their insurance saves them $6,000 annually — making the choice to seek mental health support far more financially accessible than going out-of-network or self-pay.

Frequently Asked Questions

Without insurance, therapy typically costs $100-$250 per 50-minute session depending on therapist type, location, and specialty. Many therapists offer sliding scale fees based on income. Community mental health centers often provide low-cost or free therapy.

Under the Mental Health Parity Act, health plans covering mental health must provide benefits comparable to medical/surgical coverage. Most ACA-compliant plans cover outpatient mental health, though you may still owe copays or coinsurance after meeting your deductible.

A copay is a flat fee per session (e.g., $30) regardless of cost. Coinsurance is a percentage of the allowed amount (e.g., 20% of $150 = $30 per session after deductible). Many plans use copays for mental health services once the deductible is met.

Most major insurance plans now cover telehealth mental health services at the same rate as in-person therapy. Always verify with your insurer that your specific telehealth provider and platform are in-network before beginning treatment.
