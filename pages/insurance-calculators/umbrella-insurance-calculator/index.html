Umbrella Coverage Formula

Umbrella coverage needs are based on the gap between your assets at risk and existing liability protection:

Coverage Gap Formula
Coverage Gap = Net Worth - Existing Liability Limits
Recommended Coverage
Umbrella Coverage = Coverage Gap rounded up to nearest $1,000,000 (minimum $1M)

Umbrella policies are sold in $1 million increments. The first $1M typically costs $150-$300/year, with each additional $1M costing roughly $75-$100/year more.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your net worth - total assets minus total debts.
  2. Enter total assets - everything you own including home equity, vehicles, investments.
  3. Enter existing liability limits - combined liability from your current auto and home policies.
  4. Click "Calculate Coverage" to see your recommended umbrella policy amount.

Example Scenario

An individual with a $750,000 net worth, $1,000,000 in total assets, and $500,000 in existing liability coverage has a gap of $250,000. The recommended umbrella coverage would be $1,000,000 (minimum policy size), costing approximately $200 per year. This provides substantial protection for just over $16 per month.
