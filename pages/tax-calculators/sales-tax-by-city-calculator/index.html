How City Sales Tax Is Calculated

Combined Rate
Combined Rate = State Rate + County Rate + City Rate
Tax Amount = Purchase Price × Combined Rate
Final Price = Purchase Price + Tax Amount

Note: Product category affects tax rate in many cities. Groceries may be exempt or taxed at a reduced rate, while restaurant food is often taxed at the full combined rate.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the purchase price before tax.
  2. Select the city where the purchase is made.
  3. Select the product category to apply the correct rate.
  4. Click Calculate Sales Tax to see the breakdown.

Example Scenario

Buying a $500 television in Chicago, IL (general merchandise):

  • Illinois State Rate: 6.25%
  • Cook County: 1.75%
  • Chicago City: 1.25%
  • Combined Rate: 10.25%
  • Tax: $500 × 10.25% = $51.25
  • Final Price: $551.25
