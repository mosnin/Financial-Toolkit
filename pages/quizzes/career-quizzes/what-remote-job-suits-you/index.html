What Remote Job Suits You?

Not all remote work is the same — and not everyone thrives in every remote setup. Whether you want to freelance independently, join a remote team, work hybrid, or find a traditional role with flexibility, your work style, discipline level, and skills shape which setup will make you most productive and financially successful. Frequently Asked Questions

High-paying remote roles include software engineering ($120k–$200k+), data science, product management, UX design, digital marketing directors, remote sales (especially SaaS), and senior finance/accounting roles. Independent consultants and high-demand freelancers can also earn well above average. The salary-to-hourly calculator helps compare offers.

Remote employment provides stability, benefits, and predictable income. Remote freelancing offers higher potential earnings but requires managing taxes, benefits, and client acquisition. Freelancers typically need to charge 30–50% more than an equivalent salary to net the same income after accounting for self-employment taxes and benefit costs.

For remote employees, taxes are generally withheld as normal, though you may owe taxes in multiple states if you work remotely from a state different from your employer's location. For remote freelancers and contractors, you pay self-employment tax (15.3%) plus income tax. The contractor vs. employee calculator helps quantify this difference.
