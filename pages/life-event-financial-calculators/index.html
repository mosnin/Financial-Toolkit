Life Event Financial Calculators

Major life events — having a baby, getting married, going through a divorce, adopting a child, or relocating across the country — bring excitement, stress, and significant financial impact that is easy to underestimate. Our life event calculators help you anticipate the true costs associated with each milestone so you can plan ahead, build the right savings buffer, and avoid financial surprises during some of the most important transitions of your life. Whether you are preparing for a new chapter or navigating an unexpected change, these tools give you the clarity to make confident financial decisions.

Frequently Asked Questions

The first year of a baby's life typically costs between $12,000 and $25,000 depending on your location, insurance coverage, and childcare choices. Major expenses include medical bills for delivery and pediatric visits, diapers and formula, baby gear, and childcare if both parents work. Planning ahead with our New Baby Cost Calculator helps you identify where the biggest costs will land.

The average U.S. wedding costs between $30,000 and $35,000, though costs vary dramatically by region, guest count, and venue choice. The venue and catering typically account for about 50% of the total budget. Using a Wedding Budget Calculator helps you allocate funds across all categories and avoid overspending in any one area.

Financial advisors typically recommend saving 6 to 12 months of living expenses before a career change, plus any costs for retraining, certifications, or education. If the new career starts at a lower salary, you should also budget for the income reduction during the first year or two. Our Career Change Cost Calculator helps you model all of these factors together.

Relocation costs go far beyond hiring movers. You should budget for security deposits and first/last month rent, utility setup fees, temporary housing, travel, vehicle shipping if applicable, and the cost-of-living difference between your current and new location. For international moves, add visa fees, shipping, and currency exchange considerations. A comprehensive relocation budget typically runs $5,000 to $20,000 or more.

Most financial experts recommend an emergency fund covering 3 to 6 months of essential expenses, but the right amount depends on your industry, job market conditions, and personal obligations. Workers in volatile industries or with specialized roles that take longer to replace should aim for 6 to 9 months. Our Job Loss Emergency Calculator helps you determine the exact amount based on your specific monthly expenses.
