Car Depreciation Formula

This calculator uses a declining balance depreciation model with a mileage adjustment:

Depreciation Formula
Value = Purchase Price x (1 - Depreciation Rate) ^ Years x Mileage Factor

The first year uses a 20% depreciation rate, and subsequent years use 15%. A mileage factor adjusts the value based on whether the vehicle is driven more or less than the average 12,000 miles per year.

How to Use This Car Depreciation Calculator

  1. Enter the original purchase price - the price you paid when the vehicle was new.
  2. Enter the vehicle age - how many years old the vehicle is.
  3. Enter your annual mileage - how many miles you drive per year.
  4. Click "Calculate Depreciation" to see the current value, total depreciation, and year-by-year curve.

Example Scenario

Suppose you purchased a car for $35,000 three years ago and drive about 12,000 miles per year. After the first year the car would be worth roughly $28,000 (20% loss). After three years of 15% annual depreciation, the estimated current value would be approximately $20,230, representing a total depreciation of about $14,770.
