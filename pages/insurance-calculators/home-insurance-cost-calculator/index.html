Home Insurance Premium Formula

Home insurance premiums are estimated by applying a rate per $1,000 of coverage, adjusted by location risk and deductible choice:

Premium Estimation Formula
Annual Premium = (Coverage Amount / 1,000) x Rate per $1K x Location Factor x Deductible Factor

The national average rate is roughly $3.50 per $1,000 of coverage. Location risk can increase this significantly in disaster-prone areas. A higher deductible reduces the premium since you absorb more of the initial loss.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your home value - the current market value of your property.
  2. Select location risk - how prone your area is to weather events and crime.
  3. Enter coverage amount - the dwelling coverage you want (often equal to rebuild cost).
  4. Select your deductible - the amount you would pay out of pocket per claim.
  5. Click "Calculate Premium" to see your estimated annual and monthly costs.

Example Scenario

A homeowner with a $350,000 home in a moderate-risk area seeking $350,000 in coverage with a $1,000 deductible would pay approximately $1,470 per year, or about $122.50 per month. Increasing the deductible to $2,500 could lower the annual premium to roughly $1,250.
