What Side Hustle Suits You?

Not every side hustle is a good fit. The best one for you depends on your available time, budget, skills, and personality. This 9-question quiz maps your profile to one of five side hustle archetypes: Digital Creator, Service Provider, Seller/Reseller, Investor, or Educator.

A Digital Creator monetises content through platforms like YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, or Substack. Income streams include ad revenue, sponsorships, affiliate links, and digital products. It requires time to build an audience but can become highly scalable. Best suited to people who enjoy creating, communicating, and building in public.
