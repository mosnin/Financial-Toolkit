How the Calculation Works

The impact of a credit limit increase is straightforward:

Utilization After Increase
New Utilization = Current Balance / (Current Limit + Increase Amount) x 100

For example, if you have a $3,000 balance on a $10,000 limit (30% utilization) and receive a $5,000 increase, your new utilization becomes $3,000 / $15,000 = 20%.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your current credit limit - the maximum you can charge on this card.
  2. Enter the increase amount - how much additional credit you want to request.
  3. Enter your current balance - the amount you currently owe on this card.
  4. Click "Calculate Impact" to see your new utilization and estimated score impact.

Example Scenario

Rachel has a credit card with a $8,000 limit and a $4,000 balance, putting her at 50% utilization. She requests a $7,000 increase. If approved, her new limit would be $15,000 and her utilization would drop to 26.7%. This reduction from 50% to under 30% could improve her credit score by an estimated 20-40 points.
