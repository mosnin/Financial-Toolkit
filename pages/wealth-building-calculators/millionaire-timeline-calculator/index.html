Millionaire Timeline Formula

The calculator uses monthly compounding to project your portfolio balance at each step:

Monthly Portfolio Balance
FVm = P × (1 + r/12)m + PMT × [((1 + r/12)m − 1) / (r/12)]
Years to Target (Analytical Approximation)
t = ln(Target / P + PMT/r) / ln(1 + r/12) / 12

Where P is your starting balance, r is annual return rate (decimal), m is months elapsed, and PMT is the monthly contribution. The calculator simulates month by month to identify the precise crossing point.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your starting balance — your current investable savings or portfolio value.
  2. Enter monthly savings contribution — the amount you invest each month consistently.
  3. Enter annual investment return — your expected average annual return (7% is a common long-term estimate).
  4. Optionally enter your age — the table will show your age at each milestone year.
  5. Click "Calculate Timeline" to see how many years to $1M, $2M, and $5M, plus a full year-by-year breakdown.

Example Scenario

A 28-year-old with $25,000 invested, saving $1,200 per month at a 7% annual return, will reach $1 million in approximately 24 years at age 52. The $2 million mark comes around age 61, and $5 million by age 72. Of the $1 million, roughly $370,000 represents total contributions out-of-pocket — the rest is compounding doing the heavy lifting. Increasing monthly savings to $2,000 cuts the $1M timeline to about 18 years.
