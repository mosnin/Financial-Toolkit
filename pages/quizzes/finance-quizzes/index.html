Frequently Asked Questions

Most financial experts recommend saving at least 20% of your take-home income, with 10% to 15% going towards retirement accounts and the remainder to short-term goals or an emergency fund. Our savings quiz helps you benchmark your own rate.

A fully-funded emergency fund should cover three to six months of essential living expenses. If your income is variable or you are self-employed, aim for six to twelve months. Take the emergency fund quiz to see how your fund stacks up.

You are living paycheck to paycheck if you have little or no money left over after paying monthly bills, have less than one month of expenses saved, and would struggle to cover an unexpected cost of even a few hundred dollars. Our quiz can pinpoint exactly where you stand.

Financial independence means having enough invested assets to cover your living expenses indefinitely without needing to work. A common benchmark is 25 times your annual spending (the 4% rule). Take the financial independence quiz to see how close you are.
