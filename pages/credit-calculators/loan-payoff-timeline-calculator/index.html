Payoff Timeline Formula

The number of months to pay off a loan is calculated using:

Months to Payoff
n = -log(1 - (r x B / P)) / log(1 + r)

Where n is the number of months, r is the monthly interest rate (APR / 12), B is the current balance, and P is the monthly payment. Extra payments increase the effective monthly payment, reducing the number of months and total interest paid.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your current loan balance - the remaining amount you owe.
  2. Enter your annual interest rate - found on your loan statement.
  3. Enter your monthly payment - your regular monthly payment amount.
  4. Enter an extra payment amount (optional) - any additional amount you can pay each month.
  5. Click "Calculate Payoff" to see your payoff timeline and savings from extra payments.

Example Scenario

You have a $20,000 car loan at 6.5% with a $400 monthly payment. Without extra payments, you would pay it off in about 58 months (4 years, 10 months) with $3,135 in total interest. By adding just $100 extra per month, you would pay it off in 44 months (3 years, 8 months), saving 14 months and about $823 in interest.
