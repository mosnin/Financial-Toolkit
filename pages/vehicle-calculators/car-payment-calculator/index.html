Car Payment Formula

The monthly car payment is calculated using the standard amortization formula applied to the financed amount:

Financed Amount
Financed = Vehicle Price - Down Payment - Trade-In Value
Monthly Payment Formula
M = F [ r(1 + r)^n ] / [ (1 + r)^n - 1 ]

Where M is the monthly payment, F is the financed amount, r is the monthly interest rate (APR / 12), and n is the number of monthly payments.

How to Use This Car Payment Calculator

  1. Enter the vehicle price - the sticker or negotiated price of the car.
  2. Enter your down payment - cash you will pay upfront.
  3. Enter your trade-in value - the value of a vehicle you are trading in (if any).
  4. Enter the interest rate - the annual percentage rate (APR) offered by your lender.
  5. Enter the loan term - the number of months for repayment (e.g., 36, 48, 60, or 72).
  6. Click "Calculate Payment" to see your monthly payment, total interest, and total cost.

Example Scenario

Suppose you are buying a $35,000 car with a $5,000 down payment and a $3,000 trade-in. You secure a 6.5% APR loan for 60 months. The financed amount is $27,000. Your monthly payment would be approximately $528.28. Over 60 months, you would pay $4,697 in interest for a total cost of $36,697.
