Frequently Asked Questions

Storage unit costs vary by size and location, typically ranging from $50-$90 for a 5x5 unit to $150-$400+ for a 10x20 or larger unit. Climate control adds 25-50% to the base price.

Climate control is worth it for temperature-sensitive items like electronics, wooden furniture, artwork, documents, and clothing. It protects against extreme temperatures, humidity, and mold.

A 5x5 fits a small closet worth of items, 5x10 fits a one-bedroom apartment, 10x10 fits a two-bedroom home, 10x15 fits a three-bedroom home, and 10x20 or larger fits a four-bedroom home or more.

Many storage facilities offer discounts of 5-15% for prepaying several months in advance or signing a longer-term lease. Some also offer first-month free promotions.

Common additional fees include an administration fee ($15-$30), insurance ($10-$25/month), a lock purchase ($10-$20), and late payment fees. Always ask about total costs upfront.
